6
Exploitation

6 利用

THE ABILITY TO EMPATHIZE, to grasp accurately how another person feels and to feel compassionate in response, requires us to step outside ourselves momentarily to tune in to someone else. We turn down the noise of our own preoccupations and open ourselves to what the other person is expressing. We may or may not share the feelings being expressed, but we accept them without judgment or distortion. Even when we identify with another person’s feelings, we remain separate.

共情能力——准确把握他人的感受，并以富有同情心的方式做出回应——要求我们暂时走出自我，去体察另一个人。我们调低自身关切所发出的噪音，向对方所表达的内容敞开心扉。我们或许认同、或许并不认同对方所表达的感受，但我们会不加评判、不加扭曲地接纳它们。即便我们认同他人的感受，我们依然保持着各自的独立。

Although we do not actually become one with the other person in moments of empathy, we do bridge the gap between two separate beings. That cannot happen unless we are able to experience ourselves as separate in the first place. The sense of one’s Self as separate and autonomous is a developmental milestone that normally occurs in small increments between the ages of one to three or four. In order to read others accurately, we must first be able to see ourselves in realistic terms and identify our feelings as belonging to us.

尽管在共情的时刻我们并未真正与对方融为一体，但我们确实在两个独立个体之间架起了桥梁。然而，除非我们首先能够把自己体验为一个独立的存在，否则这一切无从发生。把自我体验为独立而自主的存在，是一个发展上的里程碑，通常在一到三四岁之间一点一滴地达成。要想准确地解读他人，我们首先必须能够现实地看待自己，并将自己的感受辨认为属于自己的东西。

Empathic parenting in the face of a small child’s intense feelings helps to form the building blocks of a developing capacity for compassion. Children aged ten to fourteen months have been observed to become agitated and disturbed when they see their mothers are distressed, and this may be the earliest expression of what will one day be empathy. By eighteen months, the emotionally healthy child becomes capable of internally regulating her own distress and able to offer comfort to others. Empathy will not develop, however, unless the child achieves a separate sense of Self and the capacity to tolerate a range of emotions, including shame. Bypassed shame—the shame that narcissistic people so deeply suppress that it remains beneath conscious awareness-stunts the growth of empathy. Without empathy, people have difficulty controlling aggressive impulses.

在面对幼儿强烈情绪时富有共情的养育方式，有助于搭建起同情心这一不断发展的能力的基石。有人观察到，十到十四个月大的孩子在看到母亲苦恼时会变得焦躁不安，这也许是日后将发展为共情的最早表现。到了十八个月大时，情绪健康的孩子便能够在内心调节自己的苦恼，并能给予他人安慰。然而，除非孩子获得了独立的自我感，以及容忍包括羞耻在内的一系列情绪的能力，否则共情便无法发展。被绕过的羞耻——即自恋者深深压抑、以至于始终处于意识觉察之下的那种羞耻——会阻碍共情的成长。没有共情，人就难以控制攻击冲动。

Driven by shame and prone to rage and aggression, the Narcissist never develops the capacity to identify with or even to recognize the feelings and needs of others. This is a person who, in terms of emotional development, got stuck around the age of one to two. Others are not seen as separate entities but rather as extensions of Self, there to do the Narcissist’s bidding. This, along with an underdeveloped conscience, tends to make them interpersonally exploitative.

自恋者受羞耻驱使，易于暴怒和攻击，他们从未发展出认同甚至辨认他人感受与需求的能力。就情绪发展而言，这种人停滞在了大约一到两岁的阶段。在他们眼中，他人并非独立的存在，而是自我的延伸，存在的意义就是听命于自恋者。这一点，再加上发育不全的良知，往往使他们在人际关系中倾向于利用他人。

A young woman—let’s call her Melanie—whom I had been treating for depression took months to find the courage to reveal to me a shocking family secret. Her mother, a retired professional woman, had applied for credit cards in Melanie’s name and, over an extended period of time, had run up huge bills that she had made no attempt to repay. Melanie, who earned a modest income as a civil servant and could barely afford her own expenses, was struggling to make payments as her mother continued to charge away. I asked her why she tolerated this situation. “Oh, you don’t want to make my mother angry!” she replied without hesitation. That, I guess, would have been worse than major credit card debt.

有一位年轻女子——我们就叫她Melanie吧——我一直在为她治疗抑郁症，她花了好几个月才鼓起勇气向我透露一个令人震惊的家庭秘密。她的母亲是一位退休的职业女性，曾用Melanie的名义申请信用卡，并在很长一段时间里刷出了巨额账单，却毫无还款之意。Melanie身为公务员，收入微薄，连自己的开销都勉强维持，却要在母亲不断刷卡的同时苦苦还款。我问她为什么要容忍这种情况。“哦，你可不会想惹我母亲生气！”她毫不犹豫地回答。我猜，那后果大概比巨额信用卡债务还要糟糕。

Stealing her credit was only one of the ways that this mother used her daughter mercilessly. She also expected her to become a paying tenant whenever the family’s income property was vacant and to help out with her younger sister’s college expenses. The lack of concern for or even awareness of Melanie’s wishes, needs, and circumstances was truly stunning. By using her without regard for her feelings, Melanie’s mother had taught her to think of her own value in very limited ways. Not surprisingly, Melanie only felt worthwhile when she was taking care of others, and she had a great deal of difficulty asserting herself. She needed to attach herself to someone more substantial in order to feel real.

盗用她的信用，只是这位母亲无情利用女儿的方式之一。每当家里那处出租房空置时，她还指望女儿充当付租金的房客，并帮忙负担妹妹的大学费用。她对Melanie的意愿、需求和处境毫不关心，甚至毫无觉察，这实在令人瞠目。母亲不顾女儿的感受地利用她，从而教会了她以极其狭隘的方式看待自身的价值。不出所料，Melanie只有在照顾他人时才感到自己有价值，而且她极难为自己据理力争。她需要依附于一个更有分量的人，才能感到自己真实地存在。

Exploitation can take many forms but always involves the using of others without regard for their feelings or interests. Often the other is in a subservient position where resistance would be difficult or even impossible. Sometimes the subservience is not so much real as assumed. The offense can be as mild as a one-sided friendship in which one gives and the other takes, as common place as a selfish lover or a demanding boss, or as horrendous as sexual predation and harassment on the job. It may or may not involve deceit but quite often includes distortions of reality.

利用可以采取多种形式，但总是涉及不顾他人感受或利益地使用他人。被利用者往往处于从属地位，反抗对其而言困难重重，甚至毫无可能。有时这种从属与其说是真实存在的，不如说是被想当然地默认的。这种冒犯可以轻如一段一方付出、一方索取的单向友谊，可以普通如一个自私的恋人或苛刻的老板，也可以骇人如职场中的性侵害与性骚扰。它可能涉及、也可能不涉及欺骗，但往往包含对现实的扭曲。

Jesse was one of the top salesmen at a large industrial chemical company. A divorced father in his early forties, he came to see me because his on-again-off-again girlfriend of three years had told him that if he didn’t get some professional help, it would be off again for good. Jesse didn’t want to lose her. They had a great sex life when she wasn’t angry with him, and she could cook all his favorite foods just the way he liked them. Besides, he told me with a little wink, the way she looked in a cocktail dress made the other guys green with envy whenever he took her to company parties.

Jesse是一家大型工业化学品公司的顶尖销售员之一。他是一位四十出头的离异父亲，之所以来找我，是因为他那位分分合合、交往了三年的女友告诉他，如果他不去寻求一些专业帮助，这次就要彻底分手了。Jesse不想失去她。在她不生他气的时候，两人有着美妙的性生活，而且她能把他爱吃的所有菜肴都做成他喜欢的口味。此外，他略带得意地朝我眨眨眼说，每当他带她去参加公司聚会时，她身穿鸡尾酒礼服的样子总让其他男人妒忌得脸都绿了。

Jesse wanted to know what I, as a woman, thought he should do to keep his girlfriend from breaking up with him. I asked him what he thought she wanted. “Oh,” he said, “she wants some kind of commitment. She’d like it if I moved in with her, showed her I could be a domestic type. She’d like a kid someday—I guess her time is running out. Me, I got two kids. I don’t really need that, you know.” And what, I asked, did Jesse need? “I just want things to stay like they are, only without her getting so mad all the time. The way she gets on my case is really a drag.”

Jesse想知道，作为一个女人，我认为他该怎么做才能不让女友跟他分手。我问他，他觉得她想要什么。“哦，”他说，“她想要某种承诺。她希望我搬去跟她同住，让她看到我可以是个顾家的人。她希望将来有个孩子——我猜她的生育时间不多了。可我嘛，已经有两个孩子了。你知道，我其实并不需要那个。”那么，我问，Jesse自己需要什么呢？“我只想让一切保持现状，只是别让她老是发那么大的火。她那样对我没完没了地数落，真让人扫兴。”

Jesse was in many ways a charming and likeable guy. He was very goal-oriented and energetic, and he knew how to have a good time. There wasn’t a mean bone in his body, but neither did he have an ounce of compassion for anyone else. He was about a quarter of an inch deep, and everything was about Me. When others became enraged by his lack of sensitivity, he seldom got angry back. It just puzzled him that anybody could get so upset when all he was trying to do was get ahead or be happy. He displayed many of the hallmarks of narcissism, not the least of which was his interpersonal exploitation and total lack of awareness or conscience about this character flaw.

Jesse在许多方面都是个迷人而讨人喜欢的家伙。他非常有目标、精力充沛，也懂得如何享受生活。他身上没有一丝一毫的恶意，但对别人也没有半点同情心。他的内心大概只有不到一厘米深，凡事都围绕着“我”。当别人因他的麻木迟钝而被激怒时，他很少回敬以怒火。他只是困惑不解：自己不过是想出人头地、想过得开心，怎么会有人为此如此恼火。他表现出诸多自恋的典型特征，其中尤为突出的便是他对他人的利用，以及对这一性格缺陷完全缺乏觉察或良知。

Jesse decided to distance himself from his girlfriend but couldn’t quite break it off as long as she was still willing to sleep with him. Seeing less of her gave him some respite from her anger, but it also made him feel anxious and alone. He hooked up with a couple of old girlfriends and even had sex with his ex-wife, who evidently still had feelings for him. His children suddenly became very important to him, and he boasted to me about how close he had become to his sixteen year old daughter. “She’s like my best friend,” he said proudly. “We can talk about anything.” He thought it was probably good for her that he had been intimate with her mother. “She feels like we’re a family again.” But, of course, this “family feeling” only lasted as long as it met Jesse’s needs.

Jesse决定与女友拉开距离，但只要她还愿意跟他上床，他就始终没法彻底了断。少见她让他从她的怒火中得到了些许喘息，却也让他感到焦虑和孤独。他重新勾搭上了几个旧情人，甚至和前妻发生了关系——前妻显然对他余情未了。他的孩子们忽然对他变得无比重要，他还向我吹嘘自己和十六岁的女儿关系有多亲密。“她就像我最好的朋友，”他骄傲地说，“我们什么都能聊。”他认为，自己和她母亲重温旧情对她大概是件好事。“她觉得我们又像一家人了。”但当然，这种“一家人的感觉”只在它满足Jesse需求的时候才持续。
