Magical Thinking

2 魔法式思维

THE NEED TO AVOID SHAME at all costs creates a continuing dilemma for the Narcissist, as life has a way of regularly doling out humbling experiences that cannot be taken in stride. There is always someone who is better, brighter, more beautiful, more successful, more anything-you-can-think-of. The fact that no one is perfect is of little comfort to Narcissists, however, because they see themselves as the exception to this natural law. Their challenge is to find a way to stay pumped up inside in order to hold these harsh realities at bay. The methods they typically employ involve a considerable amount of distortion and illusion, what psychologists call “magical thinking.”

不惜一切代价回避羞耻的需要，给自恋者带来了一个持续不断的困境，因为生活总会时不时地抛出一些令人难堪、无法泰然处之的经历。永远有人更优秀、更聪明、更美丽、更成功，在任何你能想到的方面更胜一筹。然而，没有人是完美的这一事实，对自恋者却几乎毫无安慰，因为他们把自己视为这条自然法则的例外。他们面临的难题，是要找到一种方法让内心始终保持膨胀，以便把这些残酷的现实挡在门外。他们通常采用的手段，包含了大量的扭曲与幻象，也就是心理学家所说的“魔法式思维”。

Consider a woman I will call Celeste, who every December holds a holiday open house that she is convinced is the party of the season. The planning of this event consumes her for months at a time. Well in advance, she becomes obsessed with considerations of what food and drink she will serve, how she will decorate her large and impressive home, who will be on the guest list, and, especially, what she will wear. It is important that she find a gown fit for a diva, because the evening’s featured entertainment is always Celeste’s now traditional rendering of “O Holy Night!,” accompanied by her son on the piano.

不妨想一想一位我称之为Celeste的女士，她每年十二月都会举办一场节日开放派对，并坚信那是当季最盛大的聚会。筹备这场活动会一连数月地占据她的全部心思。早在很久之前，她就开始痴迷于种种考量：要端出什么样的食物和饮品，如何装点自己那座宽敞气派的宅邸，邀请名单上该有谁，尤其是——自己该穿什么。找到一件配得上歌剧名伶的礼服至关重要，因为当晚的压轴节目，永远是Celeste那已成惯例的《圣善夜》献唱，由她儿子钢琴伴奏。

Celeste fancies herself someone who, had she not been swept off her feet by the dashing young attorney who became her husband, would have spent her life taking curtain calls in the magnificent opera houses of the world. Although he wooed her away from her calling, as she is fond of saying, she still has “the gift” and must share it with the cultured audience of “close friends and admirers” who are annually summoned to this command performance. The truth is, her guests numb themselves at her bar in order to survive the ordeal. Celeste, however, is unaware of their discomfort or, for that matter, that scarcely a one of these “close friends and admirers” considers her more than an eccentric acquaintance. On this evening and for all the hours spent in preparation and fantasy, no reality intrudes.

Celeste自以为，倘若当年没有被那位后来成为她丈夫的英俊年轻律师迷得神魂颠倒，她本该一生都在世界各地华丽的歌剧院里谢幕致谢。尽管他把她从自己的天职上引诱开来——这是她爱挂在嘴边的说法——但她仍然拥有“天赋”，必须把它分享给那群每年应召前来观赏这场御前演出的、有教养的“密友与仰慕者”。事实是，她的客人们靠在她的吧台前把自己灌得麻木，才得以熬过这场煎熬。然而，Celeste对他们的不适浑然不觉，同样也意识不到，这些“密友与仰慕者”中几乎没有一个把她当作比一个古怪的熟人更亲近的存在。在这一晚，在所有耗费于筹备与幻想的时光里，没有任何现实能闯进来。

Celeste has come to depend on her fantasies to insulate herself from an inner emptiness. She sees her world in a romanticized way, with herself and her loved ones in idealized roles. In her mind, her talent is exceptional, her husband and son are storybook heroes, her “friends” are practically royalty, and her life is a never-ending fairy tale. The stories she tells herself allow her to feel special and in control, and anything that might shatter her illusions is simply ignored or dismissed. Family and acquaintances alike learn to handle this denial of reality with kid gloves, because any serious rupture in the bubble she has created threatens to expose the shame of her self-deception and release an underlying rage.

Celeste已经变得依赖自己的幻想，用它来隔绝内心的空虚。她以一种浪漫化的方式看待自己的世界，让自己和所爱之人都扮演着理想化的角色。在她心里，她的天赋出类拔萃，丈夫和儿子是故事书里的英雄，她的“朋友们”简直就是皇室贵胄，而她的人生则是一则永不落幕的童话。她讲给自己听的这些故事，让她感到自己与众不同、一切尽在掌控，而任何可能击碎她幻想的东西，都被她干脆地无视或抹去。家人和熟人都学会了小心翼翼地应对她对现实的这种否认，因为她所营造的那个泡泡一旦被严重戳破，就有可能暴露她自欺背后的羞耻，并释放出潜藏的暴怒。

Not all of those who depend on idealization require themselves to be the centerpiece of the fantasy, however. Some, in fact, abhor the limelight and prefer to “bask in the glow” of another’s luminescence. These so-called closet Narcissists maintain their grandiosity and omnipotence through a connection to someone they can pump up. They are masters of the art of flattery and make the most devoted lovers or friends—up until the time when, for whatever reason, they can no longer sustain the illusion of their chosen one’s specialness. Then, sometimes without the slightest warning, their admiration simply dissolves, and they move on to some new object of worship. You will know you have been involved with one of these types when you feel like Cinderella after the clock strikes midnight, sitting there in rags with the pumpkin and the mice. In retrospect, you may realize that the way this person made you feel when things were good was also a little unreal. But when your jokes have never been so funny nor your thoughts so scintillating, when someone’s face lights up every time you enter the room, it can be pretty irresistible.

然而，并非所有依赖理想化的人都要求自己成为幻想的核心。事实上，有些人厌恶聚光灯，宁愿“沐浴”在他人的光芒之下。这些所谓的隐性自恋者，通过与某个他们能够吹捧抬高的人建立联系，来维系自己的浮夸与全能感。他们是奉承谄媚的高手，会成为最忠诚的爱人或朋友——直到某一刻，无论出于什么原因，他们再也无法维持对所选之人“特别”的幻象。然后，有时毫无半点预兆，他们的仰慕便骤然消散，转而去追捧某个新的崇拜对象。当你觉得自己像午夜钟声敲响后的灰姑娘，衣衫褴褛地坐在那里，身边只剩南瓜和老鼠时，你就会明白自己卷入了这类人之中。事后回想，你或许会意识到，当一切顺利时这个人带给你的那种感觉，其实也有几分不真实。可是，当你的笑话从未如此有趣、你的想法从未如此妙趣横生，当某人每次见你走进房间脸上都会亮起光彩时，这一切实在让人难以抗拒。

The fantasy world of Narcissists can have a seductive allure that promises to envelop you in its specialness. Their superficial charm can be enchanting, and they often appear complicated, colorful, and exciting as they draw you into their narcissistic web. Being singled out for attention can be an intoxicating sensation in any case, but when the admirer is a Narcissist, that lovely feeling often ends abruptly and unexpectedly. When you cease to be of use in pumping up this person’s fragile ego, you, too, may feel that the air has suddenly been let out of your own ego. This can be devastating, especially if it happens repeatedly in a significant relationship, such as one with a family member or a boss. It is not uncommon, in the presence of such individuals, to feel controlled, manipulated, helpless, and angry—or on an emotional rollercoaster ride. Narcissists exude a powerful force field that is difficult to stay clear of and nearly impossible to control once you have been drawn in. They play on whatever narcissistic vulnerabilities you may have left over from earlier experiences with similar characters.

自恋者的幻想世界有一种诱人的魅力，仿佛承诺要用它的独特之处把你整个包裹起来。他们表面的迷人风度令人着迷，在把你引入他们自恋之网的过程中，他们往往显得复杂、绚丽而充满激情。无论如何，被单独挑出来给予关注本就是一种令人沉醉的感觉，可一旦这位仰慕者是个自恋者，那份美妙的感觉常常会戛然而止、出人意料地终结。当你不再能为这个人脆弱的自我充气时，你也可能会觉得自己的自我忽然被放了气。这可能是毁灭性的，尤其当它在一段重要关系中反复发生时，比如与家人或老板的关系。在这类人面前，感到被控制、被操纵、无助和愤怒——或者像在坐情绪的过山车——是很常见的。自恋者会散发出一种强大的力场，让人难以与之保持距离，而一旦被卷进去，几乎不可能加以掌控。他们会利用你早年与类似人物相处后残留下来的任何自恋脆弱性来做文章。

In addition to magical thinking, there are other, more hurtful ways that Narcissists distort reality. The most toxic of these is a process whereby they transfer to someone else whatever evokes shameful feelings in themselves. What psychologists call “projection,” I have renamed shame-dumping, a common phenomenon in narcissistic families. A mother who is conflicted about her own sexual desires, for example, may call her teenaged daughter a slut and even succeed in getting the young woman to accept that label and begin behaving promiscuously. It is as if the daughter is a blank screen onto which the mother has projected her unacceptable lust. While it is effective in protecting the narcissistic parent from feeling shame, it is deadly for the child. Children who grow up in such toxic environments frequently suffer from low self-esteem when a parent’s shame projections become part of their identity.

除了魔法式思维之外，自恋者还有其他更具伤害性的扭曲现实的方式。其中最具毒性的，是一个把自己内心引发羞耻感的一切转嫁给他人的过程。心理学家称之为“投射”，而我把它重新命名为“倾倒羞耻”，这在自恋家庭中是一种常见现象。比如，一个对自身性欲心怀冲突的母亲，可能会骂自己十几岁的女儿是荡妇，甚至成功地让这个年轻女孩接受了这一标签，并真的开始行为放荡。这就好像女儿是一块空白的屏幕，母亲把自己无法接受的情欲投射了上去。这种做法在保护自恋父母免于体验羞耻方面颇为奏效，但对孩子却是致命的。在这种有毒环境中长大的孩子，当父母投射的羞耻成为他们身份认同的一部分时，往往会饱受低自尊之苦。

Magical thinking, exploitive idealization, and the devaluation of others via shame-dumping and belittling are all attempts on the part of the Narcissist to avoid feeling defective and insignificant. At best, these tactics create barriers to intimacy and acceptance. In a relationship with such a character, you will never know what it is like to be loved and appreciated for who you really are. At worst, the unending distortions will confuse you and wear away your self-esteem.

魔法式思维、利用式的理想化，以及通过倾倒羞耻和贬低来矮化他人，都是自恋者试图回避那种自感有缺陷、无足轻重的努力。这些手段往好里说，会筑起妨碍亲密与接纳的屏障。在与这样一个人的关系中，你永远体会不到因真实的自己而被爱、被欣赏是什么滋味。往坏里说，无休无止的扭曲会让你困惑，并一点点磨蚀你的自尊。
