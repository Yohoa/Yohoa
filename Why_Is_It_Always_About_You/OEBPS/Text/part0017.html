8
Childhood Narcissism
and the Birth of “Me”

8 童年期的自恋与“我”的诞生

EVERY CHILD IS BORN WITH the psychological hardwiring to become a Narcissist. In fact, this is one of the things that make the “terrible twos” (and sometimes the threes) so terrible, as all children go through a stage when grandiosity and omnipotence are normal ways of thinking, and the sense of entitlement that goes with these attitudes can trigger rage when an infantile will is thwarted. Shame is not in the emotional repertoire at the beginning of this stage, but it will become a major force for the child to contend with before completing early emotional development. How well children learn to manage shame is what will ultimately determine who becomes a Narcissist. It all begins with the task of forming a healthy sense of Self as distinct from one’s caregivers, what psychologists call “the process of separation-individuation.” This concept refers to the establishment of boundaries between Self and Other, the ability to distinguish what is Me and what is You. As we saw in the last chapter, the Narcissist lacks such clear boundaries, but in fact, we all started out that way.

每个孩子生来都带有发展成自恋者的心理“硬件”。事实上，这正是“可怕的两岁”（有时还有三岁）之所以如此可怕的原因之一，因为所有孩子都会经历一个阶段，在这个阶段里，夸大感和全能感都是正常的思维方式，而伴随这些态度而来的特权感，会在婴儿般的意志受挫时引发暴怒。羞耻在这一阶段之初尚不在情绪的曲目之中，但在完成早期情绪发展之前，它将成为孩子必须应对的一股主要力量。孩子学会管理羞耻的程度如何，最终将决定谁会成为自恋者。这一切都始于一项任务：形成一个与照料者相区分的健康自我感，也就是心理学家所说的“分离—个体化过程”。这一概念指的是在自我与他人之间确立边界，即分辨什么是“我”、什么是“你”的能力。正如我们在上一章所看到的，自恋者缺乏这样清晰的边界，但事实上，我们每个人最初都是那样开始的。


Primary and Secondary Narcissism

原发性自恋与继发性自恋

It was nearly a century ago when Sigmund Freud described the infant’s initial “autoerotic” (self-loving) state as one of “primary narcissism,” by which he meant that all of the baby’s “libido,” or life force, was focused on itself and its own needs. In the earliest days of life, he theorized, a “stimulus barrier,” or natural psychological shield, guarded the infant’s immature nervous system against sensory overload from outside. Within its own protective cocoon, he considered the infant an emotionally self-contained being not yet able to conceive of a Self, or of others either, for that matter.

将近一个世纪以前，西格蒙德·弗洛伊德（Sigmund Freud）把婴儿最初的“自体性欲”（自我爱恋）状态描述为一种“原发性自恋”，他的意思是，婴儿全部的“力比多”，即生命力，都集中在自己及自身的需求上。他推断，在生命最初的日子里，有一道“刺激屏障”，即天然的心理盾牌，保护着婴儿尚未成熟的神经系统，使其免受来自外界的感官超载。在自己这层保护性的茧壳之内，他认为婴儿是一个情绪上自给自足的存在，尚无法构想出一个自我，就此而言，也无法构想出他人。

We no longer think of children in this way, thanks to infant research that has shown us how very much newborns are aware and capable of. We now know that even very young babies want stimulation within tolerable limits and can formulate and test simple hypotheses about their “interpersonal world” right from the beginning. Nevertheless, Freud’s ideas about narcissism, as elaborated and amended by those who followed him, still form the basis of our understanding of a child’s psychological development, the birth of Self in earliest childhood.

如今我们不再以这种方式看待儿童，这要归功于婴儿研究，它向我们揭示了新生儿其实有着多么强的觉察力和能力。我们现在知道，即便是非常小的婴儿，也想要在可承受限度内获得刺激，并且从一开始就能就其“人际世界”提出并检验简单的假设。尽管如此，弗洛伊德关于自恋的观点，经由后继者的阐发与修正，至今仍构成我们理解儿童心理发展、理解最早期童年中自我之诞生的基础。

Freud postulated that when babies began to cry more around the age of two months, it meant that the stimulus barrier was breaking down and the outside world was flooding in in unpleasant ways. He pictured a frantic infant who no longer had the luxury of oblivious self-containment and was forced by this circumstance to recognize that soothing came from outside its own cocoon. The infant’s concept of Other was no more developed than its sense of Self, however, and the comforting breast, arms, face, and voice of the primary caretaker became, from this “narcissistic” perspective, part of a rudimentary Me that he likened to a bird’s egg composed of two yolks within the same shell.

弗洛伊德推测，当婴儿在两个月大左右开始更频繁地啼哭时，意味着刺激屏障正在瓦解，外部世界正以令人不快的方式涌入。他描绘出一个慌乱的婴儿，再也无福享受那种浑然不觉的自我封闭，并被这一处境迫使着去认识到：抚慰来自其茧壳之外。然而，婴儿对“他者”的概念，并不比其自我感发展得更成熟，于是，主要照料者那令人安慰的乳房、臂膀、面庞和嗓音，从这种“自恋”的视角看，便成了一个雏形“我”的一部分——他把它比作一枚由两个蛋黄共处一壳的鸟蛋。

Freud considered this awakening to the caretaking aspects of another being a progression from the initial state of auroerotism to the earliest form of love for others, with the child still “the center and core” of his own universe. This normal form of “primary” narcissism was thought to last through the first year of life, followed by a period of “secondary narcissism” in which fantasies of his own omnipotence were linked to his inflated perception of his parents, particularly his primary caretaker. By seeing himself as one with his powerful parents, the child could carry a certain sense of invincibility with him as he began to explore his environment.

弗洛伊德认为，这种对另一存在所具照料面向的觉醒，是从最初的自体性欲状态向最早期的爱他人形式的一种进展，而此时孩子仍是其自身宇宙的“中心与内核”。这种正常的“原发性”自恋形式被认为会贯穿生命的头一年，随后是一段“继发性自恋”时期，在此期间，孩子关于自身全能的幻想，与他对父母（尤其是主要照料者）的膨胀化认知联结在一起。通过把自己看作与强大的父母融为一体，孩子在开始探索周遭环境时，便能带着某种不可战胜之感。

Think for a moment of the one-year-old child who has just learned to walk and toddles off in all directions in search of adventure. The world is full of dangers, but the securely attached child has no fear at this stage. She is full of a robust quest for discovery and scarcely seems to notice that she is so small and everything around her is big and potentially menacing. I once observed a wobbly youngster who escaped her mother just long enough to step out onto an empty dance floor before a sizable crowd awaiting an awards ceremony. With all eyes on her, she looked around at the assembled mass of smiling faces and began to gyrate along with the music while obviously enjoying the crowd’s delight. Standing on the sidelines, her somewhat abashed mother hardly knew what to do, and it took some coaxing to persuade the little actress to cede the floor to the pending ceremony. A star is born? Maybe, but this sort of exaggerated confidence is normal in early childhood and signals that the child carries within her an inflated sense of Self that is linked to an equally inflated sense of her primary caretaker—even when that person is temporarily out of sight. If this looks like a junior version of the older Narcissist’s magical thinking, that’s because it is. But in toddlerhood, secondary narcissism is still a normal transitional stage that the healthy child relinquishes when she becomes better equipped psychologically to give it up. What prepares a child to let go of this narcissistic position is the gradual development of real competencies that do not depend on inflated fantasies to sustain a sense of efficacy and self-esteem.

不妨设想一下那个刚学会走路、朝四面八方蹒跚而去寻找冒险的一岁孩子。世界充满了危险，但安全依恋的孩子在这一阶段毫无惧色。她满怀着探索发现的强劲渴望，几乎没有注意到自己是如此渺小，而周遭的一切都那么庞大、潜藏着威胁。我曾观察到一个走路还晃晃悠悠的幼儿，趁着挣脱母亲的片刻，在一大群等待颁奖典礼的人面前走上了空荡荡的舞池中央。在众目睽睽之下，她环视着满堂微笑的面孔，随着音乐扭动起身体，显然很享受人群的欢愉。站在一旁、略感尴尬的母亲简直手足无措，费了一番劝哄，才说服这位小演员把舞台让给即将开始的典礼。一颗明星就此诞生？或许吧，但这种夸张的自信在幼年早期是正常的，它标志着孩子内心怀有一种膨胀的自我感，而这种自我感与同样膨胀的、对主要照料者的认知相联结——即便那个人暂时不在视线之内。如果说这看上去像是成年自恋者那种魔法式思维的幼年版，那是因为它确实如此。但在学步期，继发性自恋仍是一个正常的过渡阶段，健康的孩子在心理上更有能力放下它时，便会将其交出。让孩子做好放下这一自恋位置之准备的，是真实能力的逐渐发展——这些能力无需依赖膨胀的幻想来维系效能感和自尊。


The Birth of “Me”

“我”的诞生

The basic kit that we come equipped with at birth includes many things, but not what psychologists call a “Self.” There is, of course, sensation, of hunger and cold, of pain and comfort. And there is perception, too, of light and noise, and most especially, of smell. There is also instinct to root for food, and impulse to cry out when in distress. Each of us is born with a unique genetic endowment, and something called temperament, but the sum of these raw materials we bring from the womb does not yet constitute Self. This must await a second, psychological birth of what each individual will come to think of as “Me.”

我们出生时所配备的基本“工具包”包含许多东西，但唯独没有心理学家所说的“自我”。当然，里面有感觉——饥饿与寒冷的感觉，疼痛与舒适的感觉。也有知觉——对光与噪声的知觉，尤其是对气味的知觉。还有觅食的本能，以及在苦恼时哭喊出来的冲动。我们每个人生来都带有独一无二的遗传禀赋，以及某种叫作气质的东西，但我们从子宫里带来的这些原材料的总和，尚不构成自我。这必须等待第二次的、心理上的诞生——即每个人日后将视之为“我”的那个东西的诞生。

Like the body and like the mind, the Self unfolds in sequential fashion. It begins with a look from someone who loves us, a gaze so compelling and utterly essential to our very survival that we are convinced the face it emanates from is somehow part of us. The first rudimentary Me resembles that bird’s egg of Freud’s and includes a particular Other on whom we depend for everything that matters. She (usually) is so important, so powerful, that it will take many months before we fully realize that she is Not Me after all but rather someone quite separate. In the meantime, we take strength from her strength.

如同身体、也如同心智一样，自我是循序渐进地展开的。它始于一个爱我们的人投来的目光，这道目光是如此扣人心弦、如此对我们的生存至关重要，以至于我们确信它所发自的那张面庞，多少是我们自身的一部分。最初那个雏形的“我”，类似于弗洛伊德的那枚鸟蛋，它包含着一个特定的“他者”，我们一切要紧之事都仰赖于他。她（通常是“她”）是如此重要、如此强大，以至于我们要经过许多个月，才能彻底意识到她终究是“非我”，而是另一个相当独立的人。在此期间，我们从她的力量中汲取力量。

Around the age of two to four months, the infant becomes more capable of recognizing a particular caregiver as the one who feeds, soothes, and comforts. A “preferential” smile reserved just for this person appears, and this is thought to be the beginning of a psychological state known as “symbiosis,” an important concept in our understanding of how narcissism develops. Symbiosis means that the infant’s sense of Self is merged with the caregiving Other—let’s call her Mother—and the rest of the world remains pretty much unimportant. Mother and child are in their own private paradise.

在两到四个月大左右，婴儿变得更有能力把某个特定的照料者辨认为那个喂养、安抚和慰藉自己的人。一种专为这个人保留的“偏爱性”微笑出现了，这被认为是一种叫作“共生”的心理状态的开端——“共生”是我们理解自恋如何发展的一个重要概念。共生意味着婴儿的自我感与那位提供照料的“他者”——我们就叫她“母亲”吧——融为一体，而世界的其余部分则基本上无关紧要。母亲和孩子身处他们自己私密的乐园之中。

A few months later, however, our infant starts to hatch from this symbiotic egg and begins to inspect other people and notice that they are different from Mother. If held by someone else, he will gravely study and possibly try to grab hold of eyes, hair, nose, mouth, glasses, or jewelry, glancing at Mother if she is in sight, making rudimentary comparisons. He has grasped the concept of otherness; now he just has to figure out that Mother is one of them instead of part of Me. That will come later.

然而，几个月之后，我们的婴儿开始从这枚共生之蛋中孵化出来，并着手打量其他人，注意到他们与母亲不同。如果由别人抱着，他会一本正经地端详，甚至可能伸手去抓对方的眼睛、头发、鼻子、嘴巴、眼镜或首饰，并在母亲在视线内时瞥她一眼，做着雏形的比较。他已经掌握了“他者性”这一概念；现在他只需弄明白，母亲也是“他们”中的一员，而非“我”的一部分。那要等到以后了。

By seven to ten months of age, our little guy becomes able to move away from Mother, to crawl, to climb, and to pull himself upright while using her for support. She is still the apple of his eye, but that eye has begun to rove toward the whole big world that’s waiting to be explored. Independent mobility gives him the physical distance from which to contemplate Mother as a separate entity, but psychologically, she is now an extension of him and not yet a whole and separate person in his mind.

到了七到十个月大时，我们的小家伙变得能够离开母亲——能爬、能攀，还能扶着她借力把自己撑起来站直。她仍是他的掌上明珠，但那双眼睛已开始游移，望向那个等待被探索的整个广阔世界。独立的移动能力给了他一段物理上的距离，让他得以把母亲当作一个独立实体来端详，但在心理上，她此刻仍是他的延伸，在他心中还不是一个完整而独立的人。

He carries this “fused” sense of Self with him as he begins to walk at about ten to twelve months of age, and a period of great exuberance ensues that lasts through age sixteen to eighteen months. During this time, bumps, falls, and other frustrations seem to roll right off our sturdy little explorer, and as his adventures take him farther away from Mother, he becomes so absorbed that at times he seems to forget she is there. Then all of a sudden it is as if he runs out of his own steam, and he returns to her for what has been described as “re-fueling.” If he can’t find her in these moments, his whole demeanor changes—he may slow down, lose interest in his surroundings, and appear almost somber. Analytic observers describe this as the child turning inward, as if trying to find an image of Mother within himself. If someone else offers to stand in for her at this age, the child may burst into tears of protest. Only after “reunion” with her does he enthusiastically resume his happy explorations. She’s still The One, and still very necessary to his sense of confidence.

他带着这种“融合”的自我感，在大约十到十二个月大时开始行走，随后进入一段持续到十六至十八个月大的、充满蓬勃活力的时期。在这段时间里，磕碰、跌倒和其他种种挫折，似乎都从我们这位结实的小探险家身上一滑而过；而随着他的冒险把他带得离母亲越来越远，他变得如此专注，以至于有时仿佛忘了她还在那儿。然后，他会突然像是耗尽了自己的劲头，转身回到她身边，去做被人形容为“加油充电”的事。如果这些时刻里他找不到她，他的整个神态都会改变——他可能会放慢动作，对周遭失去兴趣，显得近乎郁郁寡欢。分析学派的观察者把这描述为孩子转向内心，仿佛是想在自己内部找到一个母亲的影像。如果在这个年龄有别人提出代替她，孩子可能会爆发出抗议的哭声。只有在与她“重聚”之后，他才会兴致勃勃地重新开始他快乐的探索。她仍然是那个“唯一”，对他的信心感来说仍然非常必不可少。

The child at the beginning of this period (aptly named “practicing”) is starting to walk but is not yet able to manage feelings independently. Mother’s presence, along with the psychological merger with her that still characterizes his inner life, allows him to cope with both the intense joy and excitement of discovery and the frustrations of being a small and vulnerable person in an expanding world. After periods of separation, her empathic response to his mood will have a far-reaching impact on his developing brain.

处于这一时期（恰如其分地被命名为“实践期”）之初的孩子，正开始学步，但还无法独立地处理情绪。母亲的在场，连同仍然主导着他内心生活的、与她的心理融合，使他得以应对发现新事物时那种强烈的喜悦与兴奋，以及身为一个渺小、脆弱之人置身于不断扩展的世界中所遭遇的种种挫折。在分离一段时间之后，她对他情绪所做出的共情回应，将对他发育中的大脑产生深远的影响。

Brain research on infants has shown that, during two critical periods, the first between ten and twelve months and the second between sixteen and eighteen months (both ends of the “practicing” phase), the part of the brain that regulates emotions is being hardwired for life. One of the things the child is “practicing,” in fact, is how to manage his own feelings, an ability that is essential to a separate sense of Self, an autonomous Me. The sensitive mother is tuned in to her child’s moods and helps tone down an overly excited or distressed toddler, but she also knows when to permit that extra bit of tension that allows him to develop his own emotional tolerance.

针对婴儿的脑研究表明，在两个关键时期里——第一个在十到十二个月之间，第二个在十六到十八个月之间（恰好是“实践期”的两端）——大脑中调节情绪的那部分正在被一生定型地“硬接线”。事实上，孩子所“实践”的内容之一，正是如何管理自己的情绪，而这种能力对于独立的自我感、对于一个自主的“我”来说，是必不可少的。敏感的母亲能体察孩子的情绪，并帮助过度兴奋或苦恼的幼儿平复下来，但她也懂得何时该允许那额外一点的紧张，好让他得以发展出属于自己的情绪承受力。

The child’s relationship with the mother shifts noticeably over the course of the practicing phase from approximately ten to eighteen months. If Mother has shown enough pleasure and interest during the symbiotic phase, the child approaches the prospect of moving away from her with high excitement. Around ten months of age, he spends more time awake and more awake time in play, as much as six hours a day. At first, Mother is more like a combination playmate and nursemaid, but over the next six months, she will become the “no-no” person, the one who introduces the cold-shower experience of socialization with her “prohibitive behavior.” The intense states of elation so characteristic of the child earlier in this phase begin to shift to “low arousal states” that look like a toddler version of somberness or even mild depression. This “low-keyed-ness,” however, is normal and has an important function—it helps further the growth of a part of the brain that governs energy conservation and the inhibition of emotion. By moving in and out of these low arousal states, the child is learning to tone down intense or unpleasant feelings with less assistance from the outside. Each new skill allows him to be more self-reliant and takes him a step closer to psychological autonomy.

在大约十到十八个月的实践期过程中，孩子与母亲的关系发生了显著的变化。如果母亲在共生期表现出了足够的愉悦与兴趣，孩子就会满怀高度的兴奋，去迎接离开她的前景。在十个月大左右，他清醒的时间更多了，清醒时用于玩耍的时间也更多，每天可达六个小时之久。起初，母亲更像是玩伴兼保姆的结合体，但在接下来的六个月里，她将变成那个说“不行—不行”的人，那个以其“禁止性行为”把社会化这盆冷水浇向孩子的人。这一阶段早期那么具有孩子特征的强烈兴高采烈状态，开始转向“低唤起状态”，看上去就像是幼儿版的郁郁寡欢，甚至轻度抑郁。然而，这种“低调状态”是正常的，并且有着重要的功能——它有助于进一步促进大脑中主管能量节约与情绪抑制的那部分的成长。通过在这些低唤起状态中进进出出，孩子正在学习如何在更少借助外部协助的情况下，平复强烈或不快的情绪。每一项新技能都让他能够更加自力更生，并使他向心理自主迈进一步。

In preparing children to live in harmony with the world of others, socialization aims to restrain undesirable behavior, including many acts they thoroughly enjoy. To persuade a child to give up these pleasures, it is necessary to invoke the powerful emotion of shame. For the child, the first experience of shame is a betrayal of the illusion of perfect union with Mother that has persisted up to this point. Her beloved face now may radiate shame, extinguishing joy and exuberance in an instant. Instead of being pumped up by Mother, the child now feels deflated, even injured. This is an essential and instructive wound, however, which teaches the child that Mother is not only separate but different, and that his place in the world will not always be on top of the mountain. But it is a wound that must be inflicted gently.

在让孩子做好准备、与他人的世界和谐相处的过程中，社会化的目标是抑制不可取的行为，其中包括许多他们乐在其中的举动。要劝说孩子放弃这些乐趣，就必须唤起羞耻这一强大的情绪。对孩子来说，第一次羞耻的体验，是对那种迄今为止一直存续的、与母亲完美合一之幻觉的背叛。她那张深爱的面庞此刻可能散发出羞耻，刹那间便扑灭了喜悦与兴高采烈。孩子非但没有被母亲鼓胀起来，此刻反而感到泄了气，甚至受了伤。然而，这是一道必要而富有教益的创伤，它教会孩子：母亲不仅是独立的，而且是不同的；他在世界上的位置，并不总是会高踞山巅。但这道创伤必须被温柔地施加。

Shame is a heavy burden for the one-year-old child, and he must depend on a sensitive, responsive, and emotionally approachable caregiver to soothe him. What is required are soft looks, warm touches, and kind words, initiated by Mother or other caretakers. The experience of elation, shame, and recovery then becomes a positive exercise in learning to manage shame, and another stepping-stone in the development of a healthy sense of Self. The child has learned that hurt feelings can be mended, that he is effective, and that his caregiver can be trusted. When this does not occur, the child perceives instead that his needs and feelings are shameful and unacceptable, and that he is bad. Emotionally, the young child needs compassionate help in managing emotions and protection from overwhelming feelings until his brain matures sufficiently for him to do this on his own.

对一岁的孩子而言，羞耻是一份沉重的负担，他必须依赖一个敏感、有回应、情感上易于亲近的照料者来抚慰自己。所需要的，是由母亲或其他照料者主动给予的温柔的目光、温暖的触碰和善意的话语。于是，兴高采烈、羞耻、再到复原的这段体验，便成为学习管理羞耻的一次积极演练，也成为发展健康自我感道路上的又一块垫脚石。孩子由此懂得：受伤的感情是可以被修复的，自己是有效能的，照料者是可以被信任的。当这一切没有发生时，孩子转而感知到的，是自己的需求和感受是可耻的、不可接受的，是自己很坏。在情感上，幼小的孩子需要有人富有同情地帮助他管理情绪，并在他的大脑尚未成熟到足以独自应对之前，保护他免受压倒性情绪的侵袭。

On the plus side, shame inhibits the natural egotism that comes to full blossom in the practicing stage, and it allows children to become more adept at interacting with others. They need to learn that they are unique and important, but no more unique and important than anyone else, especially their parents. Without humiliating or overwhelming them, caregivers must help children to evolve from a sense of grandiosity and omnipotence that comes from seeing themselves as fused with a wonderful and powerful Mother to a separate sense of Self that is more reality-based. Small doses of shame, followed by soothing, help children transform their grandiosity into a more realistic self-image. Parents and caregivers must be able to tolerate inducing this kind of stress, however, in order for children to develop emotionally. Sometimes that’s as hard for parents as it is for the child.

从积极的一面来看，羞耻抑制了那种在实践期充分绽放的天然自我中心倾向，并使孩子在与他人互动时变得更加娴熟。他们需要懂得：自己是独一无二、重要的，但并不比任何人——尤其是不比自己的父母——更独一无二、更重要。照料者必须在不羞辱、不压垮孩子的前提下，帮助他们从一种夸大感和全能感（源自把自己看作与美好而强大的母亲融为一体）演进到一种更立足于现实的独立自我感。小剂量的羞耻，紧随其后再加以抚慰，能帮助孩子把他们的夸大感转化为更切合实际的自我形象。然而，父母和照料者必须能够容忍施加这种压力，孩子才能在情感上得到发展。有时，这对父母来说，和对孩子来说一样艰难。

By the end of the practicing period, around the age of eighteen months, the child and the mother can no longer function effectively as a symbiotic Us. The illusion of Mother’s omnipotence gradually gives way to the child’s recognition that they are different and that she is interested in other people and activities apart from him. The delusions of grandeur that enabled him to explore the world so confidently begin to crash, creating a state of emotional disequilibrium. Until approximately age three, the once-exuberant child is clearly more aware of his real vulnerability and becomes preoccupied with Mother’s whereabouts and anxious if she leaves. When she is present, he demands that she share everything with him, hence the name “rapprochement” (French for closeness, or re-establishment of good relations) that has been given to this final phase in the separation-individuation process.

到了实践期结束、大约十八个月大时，孩子与母亲再也无法有效地作为一个共生的“我们”来运作了。关于母亲全能的幻觉，逐渐让位于孩子的这样一种认识：他们是不同的，而且她对自己以外的其他人和活动也感兴趣。那些曾让他如此自信地探索世界的妄自尊大，开始崩塌，造成一种情绪上的失衡状态。直到大约三岁，这个曾经活力四射的孩子，明显更加意识到自己真实的脆弱，变得心心念念关注母亲的去向，一旦她离开便会焦虑不安。当她在场时，他又要求她与自己分享一切，因此，在分离—个体化过程的这一最后阶段，便有了“和解”（rapprochement，法语意为亲近，或重建良好关系）这个名称。

The rapprochement child of eighteen to thirty-six months has more actual capability than the younger toddler but is much more fearful precisely because he can no longer sustain the illusion of his own omnipotence and grandiosity, nor of his mother’s fusion with him. His moods and behavior are marked with ambivalence as he alternately approaches Mother to share his world and recapture the joys of symbiosis, then pulls away angrily to assert independence. The rages and tantrums of this age express the child’s outrage at both his own growing awareness of his real place in the relative scheme of things and the loss of control over the powerful, benevolent Mother who was once as much a part of him as his own face and hands.

十八到三十六个月的和解期孩子，比更小的幼儿拥有更多实际能力，却也正因为他再也无法维系自身全能与夸大的幻觉、无法维系母亲与自己融合的幻觉，而变得更加充满恐惧。他的情绪和行为充满矛盾：他时而靠近母亲，要与她分享自己的世界、重温共生的欢乐，时而又愤怒地抽身离开，以宣示独立。这个年龄的暴怒与发脾气，表达的是孩子的一种愤慨——既针对他自己日益增长的、对自身在相对格局中真实位置的认识，也针对那位强大而慈爱的母亲的失控：她曾经像他自己的脸和手一样，是他的一部分。

At the end of this challenging phase of dramatic clinging and opposition, the healthy child emerges with a realistic sense of Self and an appreciation for the autonomy of others, which is normally well in place before the age of four. Individuation has occurred, and the child may now be considered to have a whole and separate sense of Me.

在这个充满戏剧性的依附与对抗的艰难阶段结束时，健康的孩子带着一种切合实际的自我感、以及对他人自主性的欣赏，浮现出来——这通常在四岁之前便已稳固确立。个体化已然发生，此刻这个孩子便可被视为拥有了一种完整而独立的“我”之感。

Here is how one empathic mother handled some of the challenges of the rapprochement stage:

下面就是一位富有共情的母亲如何应对和解阶段某些挑战的例子：


Emily: A Calm Place to Grow

Emily：一处平静的成长之地

Twenty-seven-month-old Emily awoke from her afternoon nap to find a stranger in the house. While she had been sleeping, Mother had been entertaining a friend for lunch, and she could hear an unfamiliar voice in the next room on the other side of her closed door. She called out from her bed, and Mother responded immediately. “We have company,” Mother told Emily softly, aware that her daughter was not yet fully awake. “Do you want to come meet Sylvie?”

二十七个月大的Emily午睡醒来，发现家里来了个陌生人。她睡觉的时候，母亲正在招待一位前来共进午餐的朋友，她隔着自己紧闭的房门，能听见隔壁房间里传来一个陌生的声音。她从床上喊了起来，母亲立刻回应了她。“家里来客人了，”母亲轻声告诉Emily，知道女儿还没完全睡醒。“你想出来见见Sylvie吗？”

A few minutes of private reconnection ensued before the pair emerged from the bedroom, Emily wrapped tightly around her mother’s torso with her blond curls buried in Mother’s neck. “She likes to be naked,” Mother commented casually to the guest, by way of explaining her daughter’s unclad state. Still entwined, the two sat down at the table, and Mother resumed her adult conversation while stroking Emily gently.

两人先私下重新亲近了几分钟，才一起从卧室里走出来——Emily紧紧地缠在母亲的身上，金色的卷发埋进母亲的颈窝。“她喜欢光着身子，”母亲随口向客人解释道，以此说明女儿赤身裸体的状态。母女俩依旧缠在一起，在桌旁坐下，母亲一边轻柔地抚摸着Emily，一边重新接上了与成年人之间的谈话。

Before long, Emily turned around and inspected the stranger warily. Mother acknowledged her joining the group and offered her some food and a drink. The conversation shifted to include Emily, who slowly began to participate by directing comments toward her mother. From time to time, she reached into her mother’s shirt to tug on her breast. “We’ve just been through weaning,” Mother explained to the guest, sometimes ignoring her child’s grasping hand and at other times simply removing it and redirecting Emily’s attention elsewhere. There was no annoyance in Mother’s response, nor was there an attempt to shame her daughter. None of those “You’re a big girl now” messages.

没过多久，Emily转过身，警惕地打量起这个陌生人。母亲对她加入这一群体表示了认可，并给她端来一些食物和饮料。谈话的内容也随之调整，把Emily也纳入进来，她则慢慢开始参与，方式是把话说给母亲听。她时不时把手伸进母亲的衬衫里，去揪母亲的乳房。“我们刚刚经历了断奶，”母亲向客人解释道，有时对女儿乱抓的小手不予理会，有时则只是把它移开，并把Emily的注意力引向别处。母亲的回应里没有恼怒，也没有试图羞辱女儿，全然没有那种“你现在是大孩子了”之类的信息。

Emily was by this time fully awake and relaxed in the stranger’s presence, but she was clearly not happy to share her mother’s attention. “No more talking,” she said to the two women, who interrupted their conversation to acknowledge her, then resumed chatting while trying to include her. But inclusion was not what she wanted. “No more talking,” Emily repeated. She was not obeyed.

此时Emily已经完全清醒，在陌生人面前也放松了下来，但她显然不乐意分享母亲的注意力。“别说话了，”她对两个女人说，她们停下谈话回应了她，随后又一边继续闲聊、一边设法把她也带进来。但她想要的并不是被带进来。“别说话了，”Emily又说了一遍。没有人听她的。

So she began to play with her food and swallow cherry pits in furtive defiance of her mother’s warning not to. “It’s not okay to throw food on the floor,” Mother reminded Emily quietly, again without anger or shaming. “Don’t swallow the cherry pits, Emily. You know why? You might choke.” And Mother continued to chat with her guest while holding Emily in her lap, interacting with her physically and verbally. Emily stopped playing with her food and began spitting out the cherry pits. “Come on, girlfriend,” Mother said to Emily. “Want to show Sylvie your room?”

于是，她开始玩弄起食物，并偷偷地吞下樱桃核，公然违抗母亲不许这么做的告诫。“把食物扔到地上可不行哦，”母亲平静地提醒Emily，依旧没有动怒，也没有羞辱。“别把樱桃核吞下去，Emily。你知道为什么吗？你可能会噎着。”母亲一边继续和客人闲聊，一边把Emily抱在膝上，与她在身体上和言语上互动着。Emily停止了玩弄食物，开始把樱桃核吐出来。“来吧，小姑娘，”母亲对Emily说，“想带Sylvie看看你的房间吗？”

Indeed, she did, as well as pictures of Daddy, who was much the topic of conversation. In her room, Emily showed the guest more pictures, toys, and, with obvious delight, the reflection of herself in the mirror. Then, for an encore, she sat down on her potty chair and peed. “Oh, good,” Mother responded in a tone that exactly matched her daughter’s quiet sense of accomplishment. “Do you want to pour it in the toilet?” And she did, and flushed it, too. “That’s so good, Emily,” Mother said matter-of-factly.

她确实想，还想给客人看爸爸的照片——爸爸正是谈话中常常提及的话题。在自己房间里，Emily给客人看了更多照片、玩具，还满心欢喜地给客人看了镜子里自己的倒影。然后，作为压轴节目，她坐到自己的便盆椅上尿了尿。“哦，真棒，”母亲用一种恰好与女儿那份安静的成就感相匹配的语气回应道。“你想把它倒进马桶里吗？”她真的倒了进去，还冲了水。“做得太好了，Emily，”母亲就事论事地说。

What is impressive about this mother’s parenting style is her easy acceptance of exactly where her child is developmentally and her ability to move seamlessly from nurturing and comforting to gentle socialization and then to playfulness and back. Infancy is more over than not for this two-year-old girl, but she is not yet fully ready to let go of the pleasures of being a baby. Notice her possessiveness of her mother’s body, her somber assessment of and slowness to warm to the stranger, and how intently she focuses on control of herself and her environment. Notice also how her mother’s consistently attuned responses defuse power struggles and respect Emily’s emerging autonomy without overplaying or underplaying the socialization card. And look at the results! Emily is well on her way to a healthy and separate sense of Self.

这位母亲的养育风格令人印象深刻之处，在于她对孩子所处发展阶段的那份从容接纳，以及她能够在养育与安慰、温和的社会化、再到嬉戏玩闹之间无缝切换、来回自如。对这个两岁的小姑娘来说，婴儿期大体上已经过去，但她还没有完全准备好放下当个宝宝的种种乐趣。请注意她对母亲身体的那份占有欲，她对陌生人那种郁郁的审视和迟迟才肯亲近的态度，以及她是多么专注地要掌控自己和周围的环境。也请注意，母亲那始终如一、协调一致的回应，是如何化解了权力斗争，又是如何在不过分、也不敷衍地打出社会化这张牌的同时，尊重了Emily正在萌发的自主性。再看看结果吧！Emily正稳步走在通往健康而独立的自我感的道路上。

The first two to three years of life are the age of narcissism when the child’s underdeveloped Self and lack of awareness of the otherness of others are normal. Grandiosity, omnipotence, magical thinking, shame-sensitivity, and a lack of interpersonal boundaries come with the package. We are meant to outgrow this stage, but we need the help of parents who can tolerate and love us while we get through to the other side. We need them to hold the boundaries that we don’t yet see, to recognize who we really are and can be, to help us manage shame and contain rage, and to teach us to live in a world of others. When that doesn’t happen, we can become stuck in childhood narcissism. Failure to complete the separation-individuation process is what leads to a narcissistic personality.

生命最初的两到三年是自恋的年代，在这一时期，孩子尚未发展成熟的自我，以及对他人之“他者性”缺乏觉察，都是正常的。夸大、全能、魔法式思维、羞耻敏感性，以及人际边界的缺失，都是这一整套“配置”的一部分。我们注定要长大、走出这一阶段，但我们需要父母的帮助——他们要能够在我们穿越到彼岸的过程中容忍我们、爱我们。我们需要他们守住那些我们尚看不见的边界，认出我们真正是谁、能够成为谁，帮助我们管理羞耻、容纳暴怒，并教会我们在一个充满他人的世界里生活。当这一切没有发生时，我们就可能卡在童年期的自恋里。未能完成分离—个体化过程，正是导致自恋型人格的原因。

In the next chapter, we will look at a particular kind of parent who is unable to assist the child through this passage. When the parent suffers from significant narcissism, the results are predictable. Some children will themselves become clones of their narcissistic parents, while others will be mysteriously drawn into relationships that require a familiar sacrifice of Self.

在下一章里，我们将考察一类无法帮助孩子顺利通过这段历程的特殊父母。当父母本身患有显著的自恋时，其结果是可以预料的。有些孩子自己将变成其自恋父母的翻版，而另一些孩子则会莫名其妙地被卷入种种关系之中，这些关系要求他们做出一种似曾相识的自我牺牲。
