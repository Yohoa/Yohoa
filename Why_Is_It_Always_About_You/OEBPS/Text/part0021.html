Strategy Two
Embrace Reality

11 策略二 拥抱现实

UNREALITY IS THE HALLMARK of narcissism. Whether it’s idealizations, expectations of perfection, manufactured images, illusions, distortions of fact, catastrophizing or other kinds of exaggerations, denial, or outright lying, Narcissists will go to great lengths to avoid any reality that evokes shame and to promote fanatasies that sustain their grandiosity and omnipotence. They require accomplices for this, people to admire them and do their bidding, and often we are all too willing to meet their needs. What draws us into their web is our own longing to feel more worthwhile, more alive. Just as they need us to regulate their intolerable shame, we may need them to fill an emptiness of our own.

不真实是自恋的标志。无论是理想化、对完美的期待、制造出来的形象、幻觉、对事实的扭曲、灾难化或其他种种夸大、否认，还是赤裸裸的撒谎，自恋者都会不遗余力地回避任何会唤起羞耻的现实，并极力推销那些维系其浮夸与全能的幻想。为此他们需要同谋，需要有人来钦羡他们、对他们唯命是从，而我们往往太过乐意去满足他们的需要。把我们引入他们之网的，是我们自己渴望感到更有价值、更鲜活的那份心愿。正如他们需要我们来调节他们难以忍受的羞耻，我们也可能需要他们来填补我们自身的某种空虚。

Except for those who are truly masochistic, no one goes into a relationship with a Narcissist looking to be demeaned, insulted, or scape-goated. No one wants to feel invaded, exploited, or afraid. No one longs to be around someone whose ego is as fragile as eggshell or who flies into a rage when thwarted. So why do so many Narcissists float like cream to the top of the social order? Why are they so prominent among our elected officials, sports idols, and entertainment figures, where we pump them up at our own expense? Why do they head large corporations and lead flocks of the faithful? Why do women swoon over arrogant men and men worship vain, shallow women?

除了那些真正受虐倾向的人，没有谁会怀着被贬低、被侮辱或被当替罪羊的念头去与一个自恋者建立关系。没有人想感到被侵犯、被剥削或心生畏惧。没有人渴望待在一个自我脆弱如蛋壳、一受挫便勃然大怒的人身边。那么，为什么有那么多自恋者像奶油一样浮到社会秩序的顶端？为什么他们在我们选出的官员、体育偶像和娱乐明星中如此显眼，让我们以自己为代价去给他们打气？为什么他们能执掌大公司、率领成群的信众？为什么女人对傲慢的男人神魂颠倒，男人对虚荣浅薄的女人顶礼膜拜？

What makes us vulnerable to the seductive allure of the Narcissist is our own need for inflation. If our self-esteem is a little shaky, if there is something missing from our lives, the Narcissist can offer a potent antidote.

让我们容易受到自恋者那种诱人魅力侵袭的，是我们自己想要膨胀的需要。如果我们的自尊有点摇摇欲坠，如果我们的生活中缺了点什么，自恋者便能提供一剂强效的解药。

Charlene grew up wanting to be a doctor like her powerful father, but a college romance that led to marriage and motherhood landed her in nursing instead. For many years, she worked in the trenches of her profession until one day she found herself the head nurse on a specialized unit of a prestigious hospital. Smart, savvy, and extremely hardworking, she came to the attention of the hospital’s CEO, a charming and ambitious man who used praise to manipulate his employees into making his grandiose dreams come true. When an administrative position became available in her department, Charlene was tapped for the job. Little did she realize, however, what a price she would pay for becoming a key player on the hospital’s dream team.

Charlene从小就想像她那位有权势的父亲一样当一名医生，但大学里的一段恋情把她引向了婚姻和为人母，结果她转而成了一名护士。多年来，她一直在自己这一行的第一线辛勤工作，直到有一天她发现自己成了一家名声显赫的医院某专科病区的护士长。聪明、精明、又极其勤奋的她，引起了医院首席执行官的注意——那是个迷人而野心勃勃的男人，惯于用赞美来操纵员工去实现他那些浮夸的梦想。当她所在的科室出现一个行政职位空缺时，Charlene被相中担任此职。然而她当时几乎没有意识到，成为医院这支梦之队的关键一员，她将要付出怎样的代价。

Over time, the jobs kept piling onto Charlene’s capable shoulders. When the budget got tight and the CEO didn’t want to sacrifice programming, Charlene kept saying yes to additional responsibilities to help maintain the hospital’s outstanding reputation. By the time she was regularly working sixty or more hours a week, it was not just her job that had grown. In four years, Charlene had gained seventy pounds and a Motrin habit. Her large office, which became a second home, was stacked from floor to ceiling with paperwork. She rarely took more than a few days of vacation, and her personal life had all but ceased to exist. Her boss, however, couldn’t say enough about her as he layered on the titles. The opportunity to be an important part of his grandiose fantasy was her reward for a life drained dry by work.

随着时间推移，工作不断堆到Charlene那双能干的肩膀上。当预算吃紧、首席执行官又不愿牺牲项目时，Charlene便一再答应承担额外的责任，以帮助维护医院出色的声誉。等到她每周固定工作六十小时甚至更多时，膨胀的已不只是她的工作。四年间，Charlene胖了七十磅，还染上了对布洛芬止痛药的依赖。她那间宽大的办公室成了她的第二个家，从地板到天花板堆满了文书。她很少休超过几天的假，个人生活几乎彻底消失。然而她的老板对她赞不绝口，一个又一个头衔往她身上加。能成为他那浮夸幻想的重要一环，便是她那被工作榨干的人生所得到的回报。

We’ve all known people like this who give themselves over to someone else’s vision. When that someone is a Narcissist, what the devoted believe is filling them up only makes them emptier, and as time goes on, it becomes necessary to embrace the unreality in order to justify the sacrifice. It’s enough to make you sick.

我们都认识这样的人，他们把自己交付给了别人的愿景。当那个“别人”是一个自恋者时，忠心耿耿者自以为正在充实他们的东西，反而只会让他们更加空虚；而随着时间推移，他们不得不去拥抱那不真实，以便为自己的牺牲找到正当理由。这足以让人作呕。

Barbara came to me with the sleep and appetite problems, the hopelessness and helplessness that are the unmistakable signs of depression. Young, well-educated, articulate, and attractive, Barbara should have been able to convert her considerable assets into a fulfilling life, but instead, she was struggling to end a marriage to a man she didn’t respect and working in a job she hated, and she had medical problems that never seemed to get fixed. She appeared to be stuck in her misery, unable to make decisions that would put her on a more positive path. It took a while, but I finally found out why.

Barbara来找我时，带着睡眠和食欲方面的问题，以及那种无望和无助——它们是抑郁症确凿无疑的征兆。年轻、受过良好教育、谈吐清晰、又有魅力，Barbara本该能够把自己可观的优势转化为一种充实的人生，然而实际上，她正挣扎着想结束与一个她并不尊重的男人的婚姻，做着一份自己厌恶的工作，还有一些似乎永远治不好的健康问题。她仿佛被困在自己的痛苦里，无法做出能把自己引向更积极道路的决定。过了一阵子，我终于弄清了缘由。

Barbara had a secret lover, a married man who was the center of her universe. This man and his wife were pillars in their community, and their idealized marriage was part of their mystique and their effectiveness at their work. Barbara was convinced, however, that whatever love they had once felt for one another had long since died, and she described how her lover and his wife lived very separate lives except when their public image required them to appear united. Barbara believed in the importance of the work they did and could sympathize with why it was difficult for her lover to leave his marriage. What she didn’t understand was how much of her own life she was sacrificing, not just for love but to help this man maintain his inflated and false public image.

Barbara有一个秘密情人，一个已婚男人，是她整个世界的中心。这个男人和他的妻子是当地社区的中流砥柱，他们那段被理想化的婚姻，是他们神秘魅力和工作上影响力的一部分。然而Barbara确信，他们彼此之间曾经有过的爱情，早已死去，她描述了她的情人和他的妻子如何过着各自独立的生活，只有在公众形象要求他们显得团结一致时才另当别论。Barbara相信他们所做工作的重要性，也能体谅她的情人为何难以脱离这段婚姻。她不明白的是，自己正在牺牲掉多少自己的人生——不只是为了爱情，更是为了帮这个男人维持他那膨胀而虚假的公众形象。

Barbara’s stories of her own unsatisfactory marriage were skewed to the negative as it became impossible for her to evaluate realistically her husband’s merits when she compared him to her idealized lover. She divorced him, partly because he had become a pariah to her and partly as a signal to her lover that she was ready to move their relationship to the next level. But when her lover did not take similar steps to free himself, she was devastated. She couldn’t even tend to her job situation because her whole life was on hold, waiting to see if her lover would end his marriage. It was not until she had surgery and a lengthy period of recovery that she realized the full impact of her dilemma. She was “there” in every way for a man who could not even care for her in her time of need.

Barbara讲述自己那段不如意婚姻的种种时，叙述都偏向负面，因为一旦把丈夫和她那位被理想化的情人相比，她就无法再现实地评判丈夫的优点了。她和丈夫离了婚，一方面是因为他在她眼中已成了避之不及的人，另一方面也是向情人发出信号：她已准备好把他们的关系推进到下一个阶段。可当她的情人并没有采取类似的步骤来让自己解脱时，她崩溃了。她甚至无暇顾及自己的工作状况，因为她的整个人生都被搁置着，等着看她的情人会不会结束他的婚姻。直到她做了一次手术并经历了漫长的康复期，她才真正意识到自己这一困境的全部冲击。她在方方面面都“在场”，守着一个在她最需要时连照顾她都做不到的男人。

Barbara left treatment before her situation was resolved, so I never learned whether her lover made her dreams come true or whether she ultimately had to find her way out of the web. At the time she left, however, it was clear that she had so collapsed herself into her lover’s narcissistic lifestyle that she had lost much of what she needed to be able to set herself free. Her idealizations had already cost her much of her young and promising life.

Barbara在她的处境得到解决之前就中断了治疗，所以我始终不知道她的情人最终是否让她梦想成真，又或者她到头来不得不自己找路逃出那张网。然而在她离开之时，有一点是清楚的：她已经如此彻底地把自己塌缩进了情人那自恋的生活方式之中，以致她丧失了许多本可以让自己获得自由所需要的东西。她的种种理想化，已经让她付出了大半段年轻而前途无量的人生作为代价。


Guidelines for Survival

生存指南

1. Instead of hitching your wagon to the star of a Narcissist, find your own dream. No matter how exciting they seem to be, steer clear of Narcissists and the unreality that surrounds them. The more you get caught up in their fantasies, the more you lose yourself.

1. 与其把你的车套到自恋者这颗星上，不如去寻找你自己的梦想。无论他们看上去多么令人兴奋，都要远离自恋者以及围绕着他们的那种不真实。你越是被卷入他们的幻想，就越会迷失自己。

2. See people for who they are, not who you want them to be. Idealization of others serves an important function at various times throughout life, but it is childlike thinking—not to mention potentially dangerous—to insist on someone’s goodness, or good intentions, when that person is exploiting or hurting you. The issue is not whether someone is good or bad but whether you can deal with that person’s particular shortcomings. The impact of the bad will not go away just because you don’t want to face it.

2. 要看清人本来的样子，而不是你希望他们成为的样子。在人生的不同阶段，对他人加以理想化都有其重要的作用，但当一个人正在剥削你、伤害你时，仍坚持认定他的善良或善意，则是一种孩子气的想法——更别提其中潜藏的危险了。问题不在于一个人是好是坏，而在于你能否应对此人那些具体的缺点。坏所带来的冲击，并不会仅仅因为你不愿面对它就消失。

3. Learn to accept that if a Narcissist lies, cheats, disrespects or hurts others, betrays confidences, takes advantage, or shows a lack of compassion, sooner or later you can expect to be on the receiving end of that same behavior. Don’t fall into the trap of thinking that something special about your relationship will spare you. That fantasy is evidence that you are caught in the narcissistic web and a signal to return to reality. If you must trust a Narcissist who behaves badly, trust that person to behave in narcissistic ways rather than to be “true to you.”

3. 要学会接受这一点：如果一个自恋者撒谎、欺骗、不尊重或伤害他人、辜负他人的信任、占人便宜，或表现出缺乏同情心，那么你迟早可以预料到，自己也会成为同样这些行为的承受者。不要落入这样的陷阱，以为你们关系中有什么特别之处会让你幸免。那种幻想恰恰证明你已经困在自恋之网里，也是一个该回到现实中来的信号。如果你不得不信任一个行为恶劣的自恋者，那就相信此人会以自恋的方式行事，而不要相信他会“对你忠诚”。

4. Don’t go into a relationship with a Narcissist thinking you are going to change that person, or that he or she will change because of feelings for you. Although people do sometimes change as a result of experiences in relationships, this requires something that the Narcissist lacks, the capacity to respond to compassion with compassion.

4. 不要带着“你将要改变这个人”，或者“他/她会因为对你有感情而改变”的念头去与一个自恋者建立关系。尽管人有时确实会因关系中的种种经历而改变，但这需要一样自恋者所缺乏的东西，那就是以同情回应同情的能力。

5. It is good to dream from time to time, but if you’re inclined to take long vacations from the real world, you may return to find that someone has broken in and ransacked your life while you were away. The best defense against the intrusions and exploitations of the Narcissist is a good solid grasp on your own narcissistic vulnerabilities and an appreciation of your own assets. Practice living in reality and striving to make that as rewarding as your own gifts allow. If you can’t control your own grandiosity or your need to idealize, you may be standing in the way of your own happiness.

5. 时不时地做做梦是好事，但如果你倾向于长时间从现实世界中“度假”出走，回来时你可能会发现，趁你不在，有人已经破门而入、把你的生活洗劫一空。抵御自恋者的侵入和剥削，最好的防御是牢牢把握住你自身的自恋脆弱性，并珍视你自己的长处。练习活在现实之中，并尽你的天赋所能去努力让这种生活变得有回报。如果你无法控制自己的浮夸，或控制自己想要理想化的需要，那么挡在你自己幸福路上的，可能正是你自己。
