The Narcissistic Society

19 自恋的社会

NARCISSISM IN ITS MOST PERVASIVE FORM is a serious mental illness that affects a person’s self-concept, attitudes, moods, behavior, relationships, and prospects for productivity and happiness. It afflicts not only those who carry the diagnosis but also those who live and work with such people, and especially those who love and are dependent on them. There is no pill to make it go away, and it tends to grow worse with age. At its most malignant, it is not even particularly treatable, because to benefit from the psychotherapy that could prove healing, the Narcissist first has to accept that there is something defective about the Self, and that is precisely what he or she cannot do.

自恋在其最为弥漫的形态下，是一种严重的精神疾病，影响着一个人的自我概念、态度、情绪、行为、人际关系，以及创造力与幸福的前景。它折磨的不仅是那些被确诊的人，还有与这类人共同生活和工作的人，尤其是那些爱他们、依赖他们的人。没有一颗药丸能让它消失，而且它往往随着年龄增长而愈发严重。在最恶性的程度上，它甚至难以治疗，因为要想从本可能带来疗愈的心理治疗中获益，自恋者首先必须承认自我存在某种缺陷，而这恰恰是他或她做不到的。

Although full-blown Narcissistic Personality Disorder is still considered rather unusual, its traits and symptoms are common enough that many of us encounter unhealthy narcissism in some form nearly every day. In fact, it seems to have permeated our culture in ways that reinforce individual pathology while simultaneously making it look almost normal. What becomes normal in the culture is then passed along to our children through the institutions and influences that shape their minds and character. While it is true that the most potent transmitter of narcissism from one generation to the next is parenting, individual parents and families find themselves fighting an uphill battle to raise healthy children in a world that is saturated with narcissistic images and values. And many parents have simply given up trying.

尽管全面发作的自恋型人格障碍仍被视为相当少见，但其特质与症状却足够普遍，以至于我们许多人几乎每天都会以某种形式遭遇不健康的自恋。事实上，它似乎已渗透进我们的文化，既强化了个体的病态，同时又使其看起来几近正常。文化中变得正常的东西，随后又通过塑造孩子心智与品格的各种机构和影响，传递给我们的下一代。诚然，自恋代代相传最有力的途径是养育方式，但在一个充斥着自恋形象与价值观的世界里，个别的父母和家庭发现自己要把孩子培养健康简直是逆水行舟。而许多父母干脆放弃了努力。

Although sociologists began commenting on cultural narcissism over twenty-five years ago, the “Me Decade” didn’t end in the 1980s, and the “Me Generation” spawned its own brood of self-absorbed entitlement monsters. If you look around you, you’ll see that the Seven Deadly Sins are not just a matter of individual pathology but are social phenomena that have kept cultural narcissism alive and thriving into the new millennium.

尽管社会学家二十五年前便开始评论文化自恋现象，但"唯我十年"并未在二十世纪八十年代终结，"唯我一代"又孕育出他们自己的一窝只顾自己的特权怪物。环顾四周，你会发现自恋七宗罪不只是个体病态的问题，更是一种社会现象，它使文化自恋一路存活并兴盛，迈进了新的千年。


Lack of Boundaries and the Denial of Limits

缺乏边界与对限度的否认

Intuitively, we recognize that we need secure parameters in order to function effectively, yet we routinely ignore boundaries or view them as obstacles to be overcome. Science has allowed us to defy gravity, transcend time and space, slow aging, cheat death, and even create life. We have been seduced into believing that there should be no limits, yet without them there is chaos and unreality. The current recipe for innovation involves “thinking outside the box,” and a modern cowboy who defies the rules is still our idea of a hero. The need to be grounded in reality seems stuffy and old-fashioned by comparison. We prefer images of unlimited possibilities that feed our grandiosity and omnipotence, creating the illusion that we can, and should, have it all. The sense of entitlement, both individual and collective, is pandemic today.

凭直觉，我们都明白要想有效地运转就需要稳固的界限，然而我们却常常无视边界，或把它们视为有待克服的障碍。科学让我们得以违抗地心引力、超越时空、延缓衰老、瞒过死神，甚至创造生命。我们被诱导着相信不应有任何限度，可一旦没有了限度，便只剩混乱与虚妄。当下创新的秘诀在于"跳出框框思考"，而一个无视规则的现代牛仔，仍是我们心目中的英雄。相比之下，脚踏实地、立足现实的需要显得迂腐而过时。我们更钟情于无限可能的图景，它们滋养着我们的夸大与全能感，制造出一种幻觉：我们能够、也应当拥有一切。这种特权感，无论是个体的还是集体的，如今已泛滥成灾。

There are many who have become alarmed about the way these messages have been imprinted on our children. That noble but ultimately misguided experiment of the late 1980s and 1990s, the Self-Esteem Movement, is a case in point. It all began in California with a state assemblyman named John Vasconcellos (aka “The Johnny Appleseed of Self-Esteem”), who in 1987 spearheaded the creation of a new curriculum for schoolchildren that added “responsibility, self-respect, and relationship” to the traditional “Three Rs” of elementary education. The idea was to combat the toxic effects of crime, violence, child abuse, chronic welfare, teenage pregnancy, and drug addiction (all fervently believed to result from not loving oneself enough) by inoculating children with “the social vaccine of self-esteem.” It was a grand vision.

许多人已经对这些信息烙印在孩子身上的方式感到警觉。二十世纪八十年代末至九十年代那场高尚却终究误入歧途的实验——自尊运动——便是一例。这一切始于加利福尼亚州，州议员John Vasconcellos（人称"自尊界的约翰尼·苹果籽"）于1987年率先推动为学童制定一套新课程，在小学教育传统的"三R"之外，又添上了"责任、自尊与人际关系"。其设想是，通过给孩子接种"自尊这剂社会疫苗"，来对抗犯罪、暴力、虐童、长期依赖福利、少女怀孕和吸毒成瘾的毒害（人们笃信这一切皆源于不够爱自己）。这是一个宏大的愿景。


Raging Entitlement

汹涌的特权感

While the working definition set by the California Task Force to Promote Self-Esteem included such laudable notions as accountability, setting realistic expectations, and respect for others, the sound and sociable core of this retro idealism got lost in the implementation. Some twenty years deeper into the century’s now-rampant Me-ism, the part about being responsible and concerned for other people’s feelings got lost. In the hands of the grown-up Me Generation, self-esteem for children became equated not with doing good but simply with feeling good. The character education that had helped to instill such good habits as honesty, helpfulness, and self-control in generations of American children had come to be viewed as too shaming.

虽然加利福尼亚州促进自尊工作组所设定的操作性定义中，包含了诸如担当责任、设立切合实际的期望、尊重他人等值得称道的理念，但这种复古理想主义中健全而合乎群体的内核，却在实施中流失了。随着这个世纪日益猖獗的唯我主义又向前推进了约二十年，关于负起责任、顾及他人感受的那一部分被丢失了。在长大成人的唯我一代手中，孩子的自尊不再等同于"行"善，而仅仅等同于"感觉"良好。那曾经帮助一代代美国儿童养成诚实、乐于助人、自我控制等良好习惯的品格教育，如今被视为过于令人羞愧了。

In the Era of Self-Esteem, children took their cues from the likes of “Pumsy,” an imaginary dragon who urged them to focus within themselves on their own inherent goodness. The operating assumption was that when young people embrace their natural beneficence, they automatically behave in ways that are “personally rewarding” and therefore socially sound. In other words, bad behavior is a product of bad feelings and can be overcome by good thoughts and positive self-affirmations. Everybody wins. Teachers did their part by supplying heavy doses of praise, assurances of specialness, and feedback that the child was “doing well,” even if performance was actually mediocre or poor. Schools were no longer allowed to group children by ability, offer enrichment for gifted students, or encourage competition that might make some feel “less than.”

在自尊时代，孩子们从"Pumsy"之类的形象那里获得指引——这是一条想象中的小龙，敦促他们向内审视，关注自身固有的良善。其基本假设是：当年轻人拥抱自己天性中的善良时，他们便会自动地表现出"于己有益"、因而也合乎社会规范的行为。换句话说，不良行为是不良感受的产物，可以靠良好的念头和积极的自我肯定来克服。皆大欢喜。教师们也尽其本分，给予大剂量的表扬、关于孩子有多特别的保证，以及"你做得很好"的反馈——哪怕实际表现平平甚至糟糕。学校不再被允许按能力对孩子分组、为天才学生提供拓展课程，或鼓励那种可能让某些孩子感到"低人一等"的竞争。

Intentionally or not, we were teaching our children that equal outcome is as much a right as equal opportunity, and that they could expect to be valued and rewarded regardless of what they did or how well they performed. An even more insidious message buried in the subtext was that feeling “bad” is not normal. If your feelings are hurt when you don’t get what you want, then something is wrong-you’re a victim. Someone or something else is to blame, and you’re entitled to recourse. Such thinking is part of the reason today’s children are angrier and more aggressive than previous generations.

无论有意还是无意，我们都在教导孩子：结果均等与机会均等一样，都是一项权利；无论他们做了什么、表现如何，都可以指望受到重视和奖赏。而潜藏在弦外之音里、更加阴险的一条信息是：感觉"糟糕"是不正常的。如果你得不到想要的东西时感到受伤，那一定是哪里出了问题——你是个受害者。该怪罪的是别人或别的什么，而你有权讨回公道。这种思维方式，正是当今孩子比前几代人更愤怒、更具攻击性的部分原因。

Although these attitudes are still deeply embedded in our culture and the messages we give our children, the Self-Esteem Movement has today been widely discredited. For one thing, those three extra “Rs” started to crowd out the original trio, and when the kids had to face the fact that they hadn’t mastered the basics, all the affirmations in the world couldn’t make them feel really good about themselves. And since hard work and persistence were not part of the package, the road back has been a rocky one. All that hype about specialness has left a trail of popped balloons in its wake, not to mention a landslide of antisocial behavior, especially as these entitled youngsters have become demanding teens.

尽管这些态度至今仍深深植根于我们的文化以及我们传递给孩子的讯息之中，自尊运动如今已广受质疑、名誉扫地。其一，那额外的三个"R"开始挤占了原本的三R；当孩子们不得不面对自己连基础都没掌握的事实时，天下所有的肯定也无法让他们真正对自己感觉良好。又因为勤奋与坚持本就不在这套方案之列，回头的路便走得格外坎坷。那一切关于"特别"的炒作，在身后留下了一连串戳破的气球，更别提随之而来如山崩般的反社会行为了——尤其是当这些充满特权感的孩子长成了苛求无度的青少年时。


Arrogance, Envy, and the Breakdown of Authority

傲慢、嫉妒与权威的崩解

By teaching kids to trust their inner voices before those voices have had a chance to mature, we have also undermined adult authority. Believing they are somehow more pure (un-adult-erated) than their elders conveniently frees us from having to socialize them, but it also deprives us of control when we need it. Children who are left unto themselves, who are taught that objectionable conduct is just being “different” and receive little guidance from parents who are preoccupied with themselves, not surprisingly reject the moral authority of adults to judge them or their behavior—especially when they are constantly exposed to Adults Behaving Badly.

在孩子内心的声音尚未来得及成熟之前，就教他们去信任那个声音，我们也由此削弱了成人的权威。相信他们比长辈更"纯洁"（未被成人污染），这一来便顺理成章地免去了我们教化他们的麻烦，可它也使我们在需要时丧失了掌控力。那些被放任自流的孩子，那些被教导说令人反感的举止只不过是"与众不同"、又从只顾自己的父母那里得不到什么指引的孩子，毫不奇怪地拒绝承认成人有评判他们或其行为的道德权威——尤其是当他们持续目睹"行为不端的大人"时。

According to the National Center for Education Statistics, the number of high-school principals who complained of serious disciplinary problems rose significantly during the 1990s. Notably, these increases were greater in affluent suburban schools than in the inner city. While verbal abuse of teachers declined in the 1990s by almost 10 percent in the halls of poverty, the trend was in the other direction in affluent and moderate-income schools. A Phi Delta Kappa poll showing a 17 percent increase between 1984 and 1997 of elementary-school teachers who said they had students disrupting class most of the time or fairly often showed that these problems have not been limited to hormonally challenged teens.

据国家教育统计中心的数据，在二十世纪九十年代，抱怨存在严重纪律问题的高中校长人数显著上升。值得注意的是，这种增长在富裕的郊区学校比在市中心贫民区更为明显。在贫困地区的校园里，九十年代对教师的言语辱骂下降了将近百分之十，而在富裕及中等收入的学校，趋势却恰好相反。一项Phi Delta Kappa的民意调查显示，在1984年至1997年间，自称班上"大部分时间"或"相当频繁"有学生捣乱的小学教师增加了百分之十七，这表明此类问题并不局限于荷尔蒙作祟的青少年。

The younger generation’s lack of respect for adults has a parallel in what might be called the secularization of the spiritual, the rejection of religions that are based on the authority of God in favor of a spirituality that is more pantheistic and self-determined. Notwithstanding an apparent revival of traditional religion in the past few years, from the Age of Aquarius to the more recent New Age movement, the dominant spiritual presence of the late twentieth century has not been God but rather some exalted form of Me.

年轻一代对成人缺乏敬重，与一种或可称为"灵性世俗化"的现象相互映照：人们摒弃以上帝权威为根基的宗教，转而青睐一种更趋泛神论、更由自我决定的灵性。尽管近年来传统宗教似有复兴之势，但从宝瓶座时代到晚近的新纪元运动，二十世纪后期占主导地位的灵性存在并非上帝，而是某种被抬高了的"自我"。

The narcissistic individual’s great difficulty bowing to outside authority may explain the popularity of the kind of spiritualism that emphasizes the deity within, one’s own internal “higher power.” Whether this represents a pinnacle of moral development (an internalization of the identification with authority) or an exercise in infantile grandiosity varies, of course, among individuals. When that higher power is just Me playing God, it is easy to fool oneself and fall into moral laxity. Such characters demand respect, approval, and admiration from others but don’t give the same in return.

自恋个体极难向外部权威低头，这或许可以解释那种强调内在神性、强调一个人自身内部"更高力量"的灵性主义何以如此流行。这究竟代表着道德发展的巅峰（对权威之认同的内化），还是一场幼稚的夸大表演，当然因人而异。当那个"更高力量"不过是"自我"在扮演上帝时，人就很容易自欺，堕入道德松弛之中。这类人要求他人的尊重、认可与钦佩，却不肯以同样的方式回报。


Moral Relativism and Shamelessness

道德相对主义与无耻

The fact that we have become so confused about right and wrong is another sign of the narcissistic times, a reflection of our difficulty functioning as adults. It is as if our collective conscience is not fully formed, and we are caught up in fantasies of grandiosity and omnipotence to protect ourselves from the shame of having to admit our own mistakes.

我们对是非如此糊涂不清，是自恋时代的又一征兆，也折射出我们难以以成年人的方式行事。仿佛我们集体的良知尚未成形，而我们沉溺于夸大与全能的幻想之中，以保护自己免受不得不承认自身过错所带来的羞耻。

As a society, we have a lot of trouble with the issue of personal accountability. We tend to think like small children, looking for someone else to blame when things go wrong. Consider how often the most sensational lawsuits are those against a large, powerful industry such as gun manufacturers or tobacco companies. These cultural icons represent the Big Bad Daddies on whom we project our needs for omnipotence. We envy their power, and if we buy a gun or smoke a cigarette, we feel more powerful ourselves. Since our grandiosity tells us that we are entitled to a risk-free world, the Big Bad Daddies are supposed to guarantee our safety even though we know that guns and cigarettes kill. The wounds we suffer when reality intrudes are as much narcissistic as corporeal. We can’t bear the shame of lost omnipotence and betrayed grandiosity, and we lash out in a retaliative rage.

作为一个社会，我们在个人担责这个问题上麻烦重重。我们往往像小孩子那样思考，出了岔子就去找别人来怪罪。想一想吧，那些最耸人听闻的诉讼，有多少是针对枪支制造商或烟草公司这类庞大而强势的行业。这些文化符号代表着"凶恶的大爸爸"，我们把对全能的需求投射到他们身上。我们嫉妒他们的力量，而当我们买下一把枪或抽上一支烟时，自己也觉得更有力量了。既然我们的夸大告诉我们，自己理应享有一个毫无风险的世界，那么这些"凶恶的大爸爸"就该保证我们的安全——尽管我们明知枪支和香烟会致命。当现实闯入时我们所受的创伤，既是身体上的，也同样是自恋上的。我们承受不了失去全能、夸大幻灭所带来的羞耻，于是在报复性的暴怒中拳打脚踢、四处发泄。

Assisting us in these cultural temper tantrums is a Narcissist’s dream of a legal system in which gamesmanship bends the truth beyond recognition. If an attorney can make a guilty party appear sympathetic, juries have notoriously been willing to overlook even the most egregious crimes. Particularly effective is the perpetrator-as-victim defense, in which our tendency to justify any behavior on the part of someone who seems victimized (shamed) is exploited.

在这些文化层面的耍性子中助我们一臂之力的，是一套堪称自恋者美梦的法律体系——在其中，钻空子的手段把真相扭曲得面目全非。如果一位律师能让有罪一方显得值得同情，陪审团就出了名地愿意对哪怕最令人发指的罪行视而不见。尤其奏效的是"加害者即受害者"的辩护策略，它利用了我们这样一种倾向：为任何看似受害（蒙羞）之人的行为辩白开脱。

It may just be that shame has become the ultimate social evil—or so it would seem in a world where values are so relative that violence, corruption, and deceit can be morally distilled to “personal choices.” Even when we know that some choices are unacceptable, we look beyond the chooser for someone or something else to blame. Nearly anyone can claim to be some kind of victim, making it difficult to distinguish when real tragedy has occurred and to maintain our sense of compassion for those who truly aren’t culpable.

或许羞耻已然成了终极的社会罪恶——至少在一个价值观如此相对、以至于暴力、腐败和欺骗都能在道德上被提炼为"个人选择"的世界里，看上去就是如此。即便我们明知某些选择无法接受，也仍要越过那个做出选择的人，去寻找别的人或别的东西来怪罪。几乎任何人都能声称自己是某种受害者，这就使我们难以分辨何时真正发生了悲剧，也难以对那些确实无可指摘的人保持我们的同情。


Distortions of Reality: “Image Is Everything”

现实的扭曲："形象即一切"

From political leaders to sports “heroes,” from business moguls to entertainment figures, the people who seem best able to evade consequences in today’s world are those who can fabricate and project the images we want to see. Manufactured image is one of the pillars of power, and powerful people—along with those who would aspire to power—rely on “spin doctors,” agents, publicists, press secretaries, and media consultants to control how we perceive them. We look on with jaded eye, knowing we are being manipulated but enjoying the show. Eventually, reality grows so distorted that we no longer know who or what to believe. We find ourselves in a narcissistic funhouse without a clue what’s behind the mirrors. We become mistrustful of our own perceptions, alienated not only from each other but from ourselves as well.

从政治领袖到体育"英雄"，从商界巨头到娱乐圈人物，在当今世界最善于逃避后果的，正是那些能够编造并投射出我们想看到的形象的人。精心制造的形象是权力的支柱之一，有权势的人——连同那些觊觎权力的人——都倚仗"舆论操纵专家"、经纪人、公关、新闻秘书和媒体顾问，来掌控我们对他们的观感。我们目光厌倦地旁观，明知自己正被操纵，却仍乐在其中。最终，现实被扭曲得如此厉害，以至于我们再也不知该相信谁、相信什么。我们发觉自己置身于一座自恋的哈哈镜屋，对镜子背后的真相一无所知。我们变得不再信任自己的感知，不仅与彼此疏离，也与自己疏离。

Yet we can’t seem to tear ourselves away from the illusions. When image is everything, we lose our appetite for what is real. We stand before a groaning board of pretty puff pastry and think we are about to eat a meal. A proliferation of images without substance stimulates our senses but leaves us spiritually malnourished and craving more. So we return to the buffet again and again. Though they are no more than empty calories, we internalize these images as cultural ideals, and they provide the scripts by which we judge our own worth and that of everything that touches our lives. It is through them that we come to know what is beautiful, what is good, what is desirable, and what has meaning.

然而我们似乎无法把自己从幻象中抽离出来。当形象成为一切时，我们便丧失了对真实之物的胃口。我们站在一张摆满漂亮酥皮点心、压得吱呀作响的餐桌前，还以为自己即将吃上一顿正餐。空洞无物的形象层出不穷，刺激着我们的感官，却使我们精神上营养不良、欲求不满。于是我们一次又一次回到这自助餐桌前。尽管它们不过是些空热量，我们却把这些形象内化为文化理想，它们提供了一套套脚本，让我们据以评判自己的价值，以及一切触及我们生活之物的价值。正是通过它们，我们才得知什么是美、什么是善、什么值得渴求、什么富有意义。

The power of images is an extremely seductive force. Ask the 1,840 American girls under the age of nineteen who had breast augmentation surgery in 1998 (up 57 percent from 1996 and 89 percent from 1992). The majority of these teens raided their college funds for cosmetic reasons alone and “to feel better about themselves,” many of them encouraged by mothers who had also been surgically altered to more closely resemble the cultural ideal. You may question their values or their youthful impatience, but you can’t argue with the fact that, even in this postfeminist era, large breasts can still give a woman a measure of power.

形象的力量是一股极具诱惑的势力。不妨去问问1998年接受隆胸手术的那1840名十九岁以下的美国女孩（相比1996年增长了百分之五十七，相比1992年增长了百分之八十九）。这些少女中的大多数，仅仅出于美容的理由、为了"对自己感觉更好"，便动用了自己的大学基金；她们中许多人是受到母亲的鼓励，而那些母亲自己也曾接受手术改造，以更贴近这一文化理想。你或许会质疑她们的价值观，或质疑她们年少的浮躁，但你无法否认一个事实：即便在这后女性主义时代，丰满的胸部仍能赋予一个女人几分力量。

As real authority has eroded, we sense that image manipulation is the way to get what we want. The sad truth for all of us is that in a world where falseness is a given, these are the strategies that work.

随着真正的权威被侵蚀殆尽，我们隐隐感到，操弄形象才是得偿所愿的途径。对我们所有人而言，可悲的真相在于：在一个虚假已成定局的世界里，正是这些手段才行得通。


When Competition Becomes Exploitation

当竞争沦为剥削

Flooded as we are with images that arouse and tempt us in every imaginable way, it is only natural that we should want to have what we see. The more pervasive the images, the more demand is created, and from the perception that supply is limited, competition is born.

既然我们被以一切可以想见的方式撩拨、诱惑着我们的形象所淹没，那么想要拥有自己所看到的东西，便再自然不过了。形象越是无孔不入，需求就被制造得越多；而从"供给有限"这一认知中，竞争便应运而生。

There is nothing intrinsically evil about competition; in fact, quite the opposite is true. We are all programmed with an aggressive instinct to fight, if necessary, for what we want and need. Competitive aggression, the kind in which two or more people apply energy toward a goal that cannot be shared, is a healthy striving that ends when, win or lose, the competition is resolved. In optimal circumstances, both winner and loser benefit from the struggle and can feel a sense of satisfaction in having participated and given their best effort.

竞争本身并无内在的邪恶；事实上，恰恰相反。我们每个人天生都被赋予了一种攻击本能，必要时会为自己想要和需要的东西而争斗。竞争性攻击——即两个或更多的人为了一个无法共享的目标而倾注能量的那种攻击——是一种健康的奋争，当胜负既定、竞争尘埃落定时便告结束。在最理想的情形下，胜者与败者都能从这番较量中获益，并因参与其中、尽了全力而感到满足。

Perhaps the reason that competitive aggression has gotten such a bad rap in recent decades is that, too often, it doesn’t end when the competition is over. There is something else going on, a meta playing field where one’s very worth as a human being is at stake. We’re all familiar with the ugly side of victory, with gloating winners not content with having prevailed until they can further humiliate the losers. This is not healthy aggression but exploitation and annihilation, the acting out of narcissistic rage. When it dominates the scene, competition becomes a dirty word.

近几十年来竞争性攻击之所以名声狼藉，原因或许在于：它太常常并不随竞争的结束而结束。其中还另有玄机——存在着一个元层面的竞技场，在那里，一个人作为人的价值本身正岌岌可危。我们都熟悉胜利那丑陋的一面：洋洋自得的赢家并不满足于占了上风，非要进一步羞辱输家才肯罢休。这已不是健康的攻击，而是剥削与歼灭，是自恋暴怒的付诸行动。当它主宰了局面时，竞争便成了一个肮脏的字眼。

The hallmarks of cultural narcissism are deeply woven into the fabric of our current society, the environment in which we love and fight, work and play, nurture and teach our children. For some time now, we have given unhealthy narcissism a place to grow. If we are to redeem ourselves, and future generations, we need to resist these forces, fighting for the principles of self-awareness and self-control, reality, good boundaries, and reciprocity wherever we encounter the Seven Deadly Sins. It’s a big job, and it begins at home.

文化自恋的种种标志，已深深织入我们当下社会的肌理之中——那正是我们相爱与争斗、工作与玩乐、养育与教导孩子的环境。一段时间以来，我们一直给不健康的自恋以滋长的空间。倘若我们要拯救自己，也拯救后世子孙，就需要抵抗这些势力，在凡是遭遇自恋七宗罪之处，都为自我觉察与自我控制、现实、良好边界以及互惠等原则而奋斗。这是一项浩大的工程，而它始于家庭。
