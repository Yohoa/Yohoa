Narcissists in Love: The Fusion Delusion

16 恋爱中的自恋者：融合妄想

NARCISSISTS ARE PEOPLE WHO never learned to make it on their own. Except for their fantasies of perfection, envy of others who have what they lack, and unacknowledged fears of humiliation, they are empty on the inside. They have no real Self to bring to a relationship with another person, but they desperately need someone else to join them in their emptiness and help them maintain emotional equilibrium. The ideal candidate is someone willing to become an extension of the Narcissist’s fragile ego, to serve as an object of admiration, contempt, or often enough both. The sign over their door ought to read: Abandon Self All Ye Who Enter Here.

自恋者是那些从未学会独自立足的人。除了对完美的幻想、对那些拥有他们所缺乏之物的人的嫉妒，以及不愿承认的对羞辱的恐惧之外，他们的内心一片空虚。他们没有真正的自我可以带进与他人的关系之中，却又迫切地需要有人加入他们的空虚，帮助他们维持情绪上的平衡。理想的人选，是一个愿意成为自恋者脆弱自我之延伸的人——充当被仰慕、被蔑视的对象，而往往两者兼而有之。他们门上的招牌应该写着：凡入此门者，皆当舍弃自我。


The Fusion Delusion

融合妄想

In a healthy passionate relationship, two people have as much regard for each other’s separate existence as they do for their own. They surrender themselves in moments of union and feel expanded rather than diminished by the tumble into love. As the boundaries of Self melt away, they merge with someone who represents their heart’s desire and transcend the existential aloneness that comes with psychological autonomy. They move in and out of such moments with ease, knowing that their separateness only enhances their union, and vice versa. They admire each other’s real attributes and can tolerate their loved one’s shortcomings without becoming ashamed or enraged.

在一段健康而热烈的关系中，两个人对彼此独立存在的尊重，丝毫不亚于对自身的尊重。他们在交融的时刻彼此交付，因坠入爱河而感到自我的扩展，而非缩减。当自我的边界消融时，他们与那个代表着内心渴望的人融为一体，超越了心理上的独立所带来的存在性孤独。他们能从容地进出这样的时刻，深知彼此的独立只会强化他们的结合，反之亦然。他们欣赏对方真实的特质，也能容忍所爱之人的缺点，而不至于感到羞耻或暴怒。

How different this is from the Fusion Delusion that passes for love among the narcissistically impaired. When two such lovers connect, the goal for one—and often enough for the other as well—is complete and total merger, the obliteration of one partner’s autonomy in the service of the other’s narcissism. In a psychological sense, there cease to be two separate people in this mutation of love. The fusion of two impoverished souls engaged in a dance of power suggests that there are narcissistic processes at work in both.

这与在自恋受损者之间被当作爱情的那种融合妄想是多么不同。当两个这样的恋人结合时，其中一方——而且往往另一方也是如此——的目标是彻底而完全的合并，即为了一方的自恋而抹杀另一方的独立。从心理学意义上讲，在这种爱的变体中，已不再存在两个独立的个体。两个贫瘠的灵魂在权力之舞中融为一体，这表明双方都有自恋的机制在运作。

The more flagrantly narcissistic partner is usually the one whose entitlement, grandiosity, arrogance, admiration-seeking, lack of empathy, or exploitation are most obvious to outside observers. This is often the more charismatic of the two, the one who draws attention, whether positive or negative. It is generally not difficult to discern that it is this person’s ego that is the dominant force in the relationship, the one that will eclipse and subsume the other. The resulting “undifferentiated ego mass” appears to be owned and controlled by the dominant partner. On the other side of this equation is a partner who tolerates or may even welcome being taken over in this way.

那个更为明目张胆的自恋伴侣，通常是其特权感、浮夸、傲慢、对仰慕的渴求、缺乏共情或剥削行为在外人看来最为明显的一方。这往往是两人中更具魅力的一个，是吸引注意力的那一个，无论这种注意是正面的还是负面的。一般不难看出，正是这个人的自我才是这段关系中的主导力量，它将掩盖并吞没另一方。由此形成的"未分化的自我团块"，似乎为这位占主导地位的伴侣所拥有和控制。而在这道等式的另一边，则是一位容忍、甚至可能乐于以这种方式被接管的伴侣。

The person who submits to the tyranny of a Narcissist often appears to be an enigma. Why would anyone choose, repeatedly and perpetually, to offer him-or herself as fuel for another’s consuming need for inflation, and at such a price? Why would someone sacrifice Self so completely for “love”?

那个屈服于自恋者暴政的人，往往显得像个谜。为什么会有人一再地、永无止境地选择把自己献出去，作为他人那吞噬一切的膨胀需求的燃料，而且代价如此之大？为什么会有人为了"爱"而如此彻底地牺牲自我？

The nickel answer to this question is that he or she has been programmed to self-effacement and self-abasement by earlier life experiences. Perhaps he had a narcissistic parent and learned to feel worthy only when meeting that person’s needs. Subsequent relationships that recreate the original dynamics seem to offer the possibility of a different outcome. “This time I will be loved for myself,” he hopes. But it’s not in the cards.

对这个问题最浅显的回答是，他或她早年的人生经历已将其塑造成了自我抹杀和自我贬低的人。也许他有一位自恋的父母，从而学会了只有在满足那个人的需求时才感到自己有价值。后来那些重现了最初互动模式的关系，似乎提供了一种不同结局的可能。"这一次，我会因我本来的样子而被爱，"他这样期盼着。但这是注定不会实现的。

A somewhat more sophisticated explanation is that in having learned to submit, she has figured out how to exert some power in an otherwise powerless situation, providing a fleeting sense of strength and control. Often, such morsels suffice for survival of the soul. And in extreme cases when someone has been so abused that self-hatred is great, there may even be a sort of relief in giving oneself over to someone perceived as more worthy.

一种稍微更复杂的解释是，在学会了屈服之后，她已经摸索出如何在一个原本无能为力的处境中施加某种力量，从而获得一种转瞬即逝的力量感和控制感。通常，这点滴的慰藉已足以维系灵魂的存续。而在极端的情况下，当一个人受到的虐待之深以致自我憎恨极其强烈时，把自己交付给一个被视为更有价值的人，甚至可能带来某种解脱。

Even healthy people are drawn to the merger aspects of passionate love, but they are able both to transcend boundaries and to maintain them. Their healthy narcissism leads them to admire someone who reflects their own ideals, to form attachments to others in their entirety and separateness, and to maintain a love relationship over an extended period of time—something that the Narcissist can accomplish only amid great conflict. There is an element of altruism in healthy love that mingles self-centeredness with self-sacrifice and concern for the loved one’s feelings and well-being, and sexual contact is more than a search for pleasure—it is also an expression of gratitude for the partner’s love.

即便是健康的人，也会被热烈爱情中那种融合的成分所吸引，但他们既能超越边界，也能维持边界。他们健康的自恋使他们去欣赏那个映照出自身理想的人，去把对方作为一个完整而独立的个体来依恋，并能将一段爱的关系长久地维系下去——而这是自恋者只有在巨大的冲突中才能勉强做到的。健康的爱中含有一种利他的成分，它将以自我为中心与自我牺牲、对所爱之人的感受和福祉的关切交织在一起；而性接触也不仅仅是对快感的追寻——它同时也是对伴侣之爱的一种感激的表达。

For Narcissists, however, fulfillment comes from having conquered or won the love object, annihilating that person’s separateness and replacing it with a fantasy “twinship.” They seek someone who will reflect back their importance and who can also be the receptacle for their off-loaded shame and envy. The partner is merely a means for the Narcissist to negotiate different parts of himself, amplifying his assets and ridding himself of defects.

然而对自恋者来说，满足感来自征服或赢得爱的对象，抹杀那个人的独立性，并以一种幻想出来的"孪生关系"取而代之。他们寻找的是一个能映照出他们的重要性、同时又能充当容器来盛放他们卸下的羞耻与嫉妒的人。伴侣不过是自恋者用来调和自身不同部分的工具——用以放大自己的长处，并清除自己的缺陷。

There are only two kinds of people of any use to Narcissists: those who can pump them up and those whom they can put down. The ones who can pump them up may do so either by admiring them or by sharing their own special attributes so the Narcissist can bask in their reflected glow. The ones whom they can put down allow Narcissists either to off-load shame via projection or to feel superior by comparison. The love objects of Narcissists often serve both functions. If you’re hooked on a Narcissist, prepare to tolerate regular doses of contempt while offering unending admiration, which the Narcissist is far more interested in than your love.

对自恋者而言，有用的人只有两种：能把他们捧起来的人，和能被他们贬下去的人。能把他们捧起来的人，要么通过仰慕他们，要么通过分享自己的特殊之处，好让自恋者沐浴在那反射而来的光辉之中。能被他们贬下去的人，则让自恋者得以通过投射卸下羞耻，或通过比较来感到优越。自恋者所爱的对象往往同时兼具这两种功能。如果你迷上了一个自恋者，那就准备好定期忍受一剂剂的蔑视，同时奉上无尽的仰慕吧——比起你的爱，自恋者对仰慕要感兴趣得多。

While it’s normal to idealize those we love, to the Narcissist, his own self-image requires that other people also see the love object in an idealized way. Toward that end, he must select someone who is beautiful, intelligent, accomplished, or otherwise widely recognized as exceptional. The Narcissist hopes to commandeer those admirable qualities that he or she lacks, acquiring “gilt” by association.

将我们所爱的人理想化本是正常的，但对自恋者来说，他自身的自我形象还要求其他人也以理想化的眼光看待他所爱的对象。为此，他必须挑选一个美丽、聪明、有成就，或在其他方面被广泛公认为出类拔萃的人。自恋者希望据为己有那些他自身所缺乏的令人钦佩的品质，借由这种关联给自己镀上一层"金"。


Smothering Heights: The Tale of Dennis and Christina

令人窒息的高地：Dennis 与 Christina 的故事

Of all the facets of his career as a professor of English literature, Dennis most enjoyed the lecture. He liked to arrive late so he could make an entrance, and he relished that moment just before his students began taking notes when all eyes were on him.

在英语文学教授这份职业的方方面面中，Dennis 最享受的是讲课。他喜欢迟到，好让自己能登场亮相，而他尤其陶醉于学生们尚未开始记笔记、所有目光都聚焦在他身上的那一刻。

It was at a moment such as this when he had first noticed Christina, and her large pale eyes and smooth complexion had reminded him of the heroines in the books he admired. Her beauty excited something primal within him, and it wasn’t long before he resolved to win her love.

正是在这样的一刻，他第一次注意到了 Christina，她那双大而浅色的眼睛和光洁的肤色，让他想起了他所钟爱的书中的女主人公。她的美丽唤起了他内心深处某种原始的冲动，没过多久，他便下定决心要赢得她的爱。

He began by wooing her surreptitiously with his words. She became his audience of one, the others fading into background as he courted her secretly, willing her to be stirred by his covert passion and to betray some yearning of her own. In time, she began to respond to him, just as he wished.

他先是用言语暗中向她示爱。她成了他唯一的听众，其余的人都退为背景，他偷偷地追求她，企图用自己隐秘的激情打动她，让她流露出某种属于她自己的渴望。渐渐地，她开始回应他，正如他所希望的那样。

By spring they were lovers, and possessing her was everything he had imagined it would be. She was perfection, and, he was convinced, completely and totally his. Being able to call her his own made him feel as if he, too, was a character in a romantic novel.

到了春天，他们成了恋人，而拥有她正如他想象中那般美好。她是完美的化身，而且他深信，她完完全全属于他。能够把她称作自己的人，让他觉得自己仿佛也成了一部浪漫小说中的人物。

She moved in with him after graduation, and although she had a degree in economics, that was of no consequence in the future he envisioned for them together. He planned that they would marry when he got tenure, and then they would have a child while on sabbatical in England. She would be the inspiration and the sounding-board for the novel he planned to write and would charm his friends at faculty gatherings and the small dinner parties that he would host to showcase their perfect love. With Christina, he felt himself to be the envy of every man in the English department.

毕业后她搬来与他同住，尽管她拥有经济学学位，但在他为两人共同设想的未来里，那并不重要。他计划等自己拿到终身教职后就结婚，然后在英国学术休假期间生一个孩子。她将成为他计划撰写的那部小说的灵感来源和倾诉对象，并在教职员聚会以及他为展示两人完美之爱而举办的小型晚宴上，迷倒他的朋友们。有了 Christina，他觉得自己成了英语系里每一个男人嫉妒的对象。

Although Narcissists intend for others to envy them their conquests, they are unaware of how envious they themselves feel of the ones who become their love objects. Narcissists are essentially competitive people, and the very qualities that attract them at the outset of a relationship in time begin to make them feel inferior by comparison. The person who seemed so capable of feeding their hunger for admiration later becomes a threat, and they need to tear that person down in order to build themselves back up. Whatever Narcissists admire in a loved one also diminishes them and therefore must be detroyed. Envy leads to contempt and contempt to destruction, leaving Narcissists frustrated and empty in their pursuit of perfection.

尽管自恋者希望别人嫉妒他们的征服成果，他们自己却意识不到，他们对成为其爱的对象的那些人怀有何等的嫉妒。自恋者本质上是好胜之人，而那些在关系之初吸引他们的特质，久而久之却开始让他们在比较之下自惭形秽。那个曾经如此善于满足他们仰慕之渴求的人，后来变成了一种威胁，于是他们需要把那个人贬低下去，以便重新抬高自己。自恋者在所爱之人身上欣赏的一切，同时也在贬低他们，因此必须被摧毁。嫉妒导致蔑视，蔑视导致毁灭，使自恋者在追求完美的过程中倍感挫败与空虚。

Envy, like shame, is a sordid emotion that Narcissists can’t tolerate. Instead of admitting to themselves that they’re envious, they get rid of the bad feelings by making them belong to someone else. By a trick of mind called projection, the envied person becomes the one who harbors these unacceptable thoughts, and the Narcissist remains admirable in his or her own eyes as well as the eyes of the world. By this same mechanism, Narcissists also attribute their own destructive urges to other people and are terrified of the real or imagined envy that surrounds them. These distortions make real intimacy impossible.

嫉妒和羞耻一样，是自恋者无法忍受的一种卑劣情绪。他们非但不向自己承认自己在嫉妒，反而通过把这些坏情绪转嫁给别人来摆脱它们。借助一种被称为投射的心理把戏，被嫉妒的人反倒成了怀有这些不可接受念头的人，而自恋者则在自己眼中以及世人眼中依然令人钦佩。通过同样的机制，自恋者还把自身的破坏性冲动归咎于他人，并对环绕在他们周围的、真实或想象中的嫉妒感到恐惧。这些扭曲使真正的亲密关系成为不可能。

For Narcissists, all relationships are about exploitation, and it’s eat or be eaten. The idea of making themselves vulnerable is no more than an invitation to be used. When someone becomes dependent on them, they feel as if they’re being taken advantage of, and fear of being exploited also makes them deny their own dependency. Ordinary reciprocity feels to them as if they’re being invaded and used, so they insist on relationships in which they always maintain the upper hand.

对自恋者来说，所有关系都关乎剥削，不是吃人就是被吃。让自己变得脆弱这个念头，在他们看来不过是邀请别人来利用自己。当有人开始依赖他们时，他们便觉得自己被占了便宜；而对被剥削的恐惧也使他们否认自身的依赖。寻常的互惠在他们感觉起来就像是被侵入、被利用，因此他们坚持要建立那种自己始终占据上风的关系。


Christina’s Story

Christina 的故事

It was my senior year, and I was pretty saturated with economics, thought I would take an elective completely outside of my major. Some of my friends had told me about this English professor who made the classics come alive. It sounded like fun and a good way to finish up my undergraduate studies, a chance to get a little culture before going on for my MBA.

那是我大四那年，我对经济学已经相当腻烦了，心想要选一门完全在我专业之外的选修课。一些朋友跟我提起过这位英语教授，说他能让那些经典作品活灵活现起来。这听上去挺有意思，也是结束本科学业的好办法，让我在去读 MBA 之前能稍微熏陶一点文化。

But what happened took me completely by surprise. Dennis was just mesmerizing. Sitting in his class, I felt practically spellbound, as if I was the only person in this big lecture hall, like he was talking just to me. Then at some point I realized that he was talking to me in some special way. I started hanging around after class on any pretext I could think of, and before long, I was madly in love with him. I had had a couple of boyfriends, but this was a man, ten years older than me. Usually, I tried to keep my relationships casual because I didn’t want anything to interfere with my education, and I had a career mapped out for myself that would keep me in school for a long time. But all that went out the window when I fell in love with Dennis.

但接下来发生的事完全出乎我的意料。Dennis 简直令人着迷。坐在他的课堂上，我几乎被迷住了，仿佛在这间偌大的阶梯教室里只有我一个人，仿佛他只是在对我一个人讲话。后来某个时刻，我意识到他确实是在用某种特别的方式对我说话。我开始想方设法找各种借口在下课后逗留，没过多久，我就疯狂地爱上了他。我以前交过几个男朋友，但这是一个男人，比我大十岁。通常我都尽量让自己的恋情保持随意，因为我不想让任何事情干扰我的学业，而且我为自己规划了一条会让我长期待在学校里的职业道路。可当我爱上 Dennis 时，这一切都被抛到了九霄云外。

In the spring, I went through the motions of applying to graduate programs, but I was pretty distracted. Whenever I tried to talk to Dennis about my career plans, he would kind of fade out. At first I thought maybe he was just sad about the prospect of our being separated and couldn’t talk about it. It actually made me feel closer to him to think that he cared that much. I figured I’d wait and see what happened and we’d deal with it when the time came.

那年春天，我例行公事般地申请了研究生项目，但其实心不在焉。每当我试图跟 Dennis 谈起我的职业规划，他就有点心不在焉、神游天外。起初我以为也许他只是为我们即将分离的前景感到难过，没法谈论这件事。想到他在乎到这种程度，实际上让我觉得跟他更亲近了。我心想，那就等等看吧，到时候再说。

After graduation, I moved in with him, and I’d been living there about a month when I found out I’d been accepted at NYU. I was so excited and I thought Dennis would be happy for me, too, but he got really cold, and I’ll never forget what he said to me. “Christina, if you turn your back on my love, you will never know the happiness you’ve missed. No one will ever love you the way I do.” That was it. There was no discussion, no compromise. He made me choose. I gave it a lot of thought—I really did—and I decided he was right. No career could be worth the love we had.

毕业后，我搬去和他同住。我在那儿住了大约一个月，就得知自己被纽约大学录取了。我激动极了，本以为 Dennis 也会为我高兴，可他却变得非常冷淡，我永远忘不了他对我说的话。"Christina，如果你背弃我的爱，你将永远不会知道自己错过了怎样的幸福。没有人会像我这样爱你。"就是这样。没有商量，没有妥协。他逼我做出选择。我想了很多——我是真的想了很多——最后我认定他是对的。没有什么事业值得我们拥有的这份爱去交换。

From that point on, he pretty much took charge of my life, and I just allowed myself to be swept along. When things go according to his plans, it’s like magic and we’re so happy. But if something doesn’t work out the way he wants it to, it’s a completely different story. He doesn’t just get disappointed like other people, it’s like he’s really angry. And he can stay that way for days, unless I can figure out how to cheer him up. Ever since our son came along, that’s been harder and harder to do. Byron was born with a cleft palate, and sometimes I think Dennis blames me. I know it sounds crazy—he adores me!—but sometimes I get this feeling that Dennis looks down on me, like maybe I’m intellectually inferior or something. Maybe I should have gone to graduate school after all, so he could still respect me. Sometimes I just feel so confused.

从那以后，他基本上掌控了我的生活，而我也任由自己被裹挟着随波逐流。当事情按照他的计划进行时，那简直如魔法一般，我们幸福极了。可一旦有什么事情没有如他所愿，那就完全是另一回事了。他不像别人那样只是失望，而像是真的发怒了。而且他能这样持续好几天，除非我能想出办法把他哄高兴。自从我们的儿子出生后，这件事就变得越来越难做到。Byron 生来就有腭裂，有时我觉得 Dennis 在怪我。我知道这听上去很疯狂——他那么宠爱我！——但有时我就是有种感觉，觉得 Dennis 看不起我，好像我在智识上比他低人一等似的。也许我当初真该去读研究生，这样他才还能尊重我。有时我只是感到如此困惑。

In Christina, Dennis found the perfect mate who perfectly mirrored his grandiosity and omnipotence and completely folded herself into him. Having won her love, his inflation was at first boundless. Love such as Dennis’s is like a drug, a perpetual high that insulates the lover from self-doubt and awareness of personal limits or shortcomings. But to maintain the intoxication, he must be able to control his most prized possession.

在 Christina 身上，Dennis 找到了完美的伴侣，她完美地映照出他的浮夸与全能感，并彻底地把自己融入了他。赢得她的爱之后，他起初的自我膨胀是无边无际的。像 Dennis 这样的爱就像一种毒品，是一种持续的亢奋，使这个爱者得以隔绝于自我怀疑以及对个人局限或缺陷的觉察之外。但要维持这种陶醉，他就必须能够掌控自己最珍视的财产。

The means by which the Narcissist controls the loved one are many and varied, a function of individual style, circumstances, and opportunity. They can be as sweetly beguiling as flattery and professions of love, as maddening as the carrot that dangles just beyond reach, as manipulative as the polar opposites of subservience and moral superiority, or as intimidating as a volcano about to explode. The goal is to maintain the obliteration of the loved one’s separateness, the Fusion Delusion.

自恋者用来掌控所爱之人的手段繁多而花样百出，因个人风格、处境和时机而异。它们可以像奉承和爱的表白那样甜蜜诱人，可以像那根悬在眼前却始终够不着的胡萝卜那样令人抓狂，可以像卑躬屈膝与道德优越感这两个极端那样工于心计，也可以像一座即将喷发的火山那样令人胆寒。其目标都是为了维持对所爱之人独立性的抹杀——即融合妄想。

It is important to Narcissists to choose those who will look up to them, recognize their special qualities, and make them feel important. While we all want to be valued by those we love, the Narcissist really needs the loved one to be a constant mirror of his or her worth. Any of us might feel disappointed, hurt, or even angry when those we love ignore or criticize us, but the Narcissist becomes enraged when the pipeline for inflation is ruptured. The loved one is not allowed to have or express feelings or opinions that contradict the needs of the Narcissist.

对自恋者来说，挑选那些会仰视他们、认可他们的特殊品质、并让他们感到自己很重要的人，是至关重要的。虽然我们都希望被自己所爱的人所珍视，但自恋者真正需要的，是让所爱之人成为他或她价值的一面恒常之镜。当我们所爱的人忽视或批评我们时，我们任何人都可能感到失望、受伤，甚至愤怒，但自恋者会在那条供给膨胀的输送管被切断时勃然大怒。所爱之人不被允许拥有或表达任何与自恋者需求相抵触的感受或意见。

Often the Narcissist selects a love object who is in some way deficient or less powerful, someone who can be easily manipulated or controlled. This is tricky, however. If this person becomes too much of a doormat, too degraded or flawed in the eyes of the Narcissist, idealization may be compromised. The Narcissist usually solves this problem by splitting good and bad perceptions of the love object so that the good can be preserved in some way and the bad remains walled off in a compartment that can be opened or closed at will. This is very much like the primitive way in which very young children are unable to see others as complex entities with both good and bad qualities. At any given moment, someone is either all good or all bad. And the Narcissist’s perception of the love object can change in an instant.

自恋者常常会选择一个在某方面有所欠缺或较为弱势的爱的对象——一个容易被操纵或控制的人。然而这也很微妙。如果这个人变得过于逆来顺受，在自恋者眼中过于卑微或有缺陷，理想化就可能受到损害。自恋者通常通过把对爱的对象的好与坏的感知进行分裂来解决这个问题，从而使好的一面能以某种方式被保留下来，而坏的一面则被隔绝在一个可随意开启或关闭的隔间里。这非常类似于幼童那种原始的认知方式——他们无法把他人看作兼具好坏品质的复杂个体。在任何给定的时刻，一个人要么全好，要么全坏。而自恋者对爱的对象的感知，可以在一瞬间发生转变。

Because of this inability to hold an image of the love object as both good and bad, the Narcissist will “forget,” overlook, or dismiss any of his or her own behavior that demeans this person, all the while expecting the love object to live up to idealized expectations, particularly in public and regardless of how much humiliation the Narcissist has inflicted. If the Narcissist can’t mobilize enough fantasy or denial to sustain the necessary idealization, the erstwhile love object may be discarded in favor of someone who is less tarnished. It is hard to continue to hold in high regard someone who has been made a slave, even if that person is one’s own slave.

由于无法在心中同时保有一个既好又坏的爱的对象的形象，自恋者会"忘记"、忽视或无视自己任何贬低这个人的行为，与此同时却又期望爱的对象不负其理想化的期待——尤其是在公开场合，无论自恋者已对其施加了多少羞辱。如果自恋者无法调动足够的幻想或否认来维持必要的理想化，那么从前所爱的对象就可能被抛弃，转而被一个污点较少的人取而代之。要继续敬重一个已被沦为奴隶的人是很难的，哪怕那个人是自己的奴隶。


Don Juans and Ice Queens

唐璜与冰美人

Narcissists are ultimately the worst lovers, with a full range of every sexual dysfunction imaginable and a tendency to use their partners selfishly and without guilt. Heterosexual and bisexual narcissistic men harbor a deep envy of women and fear being rejected and ridiculed by them. They tend to objectify their partners either as idealized but desexualized objects (the “pure” mother, or madonna), or those who can be freely enjoyed without love or admiration (the “soiled” mother, or whore). Conversely, they may also develop tender feelings for women who turn them off sexually. Many are Don Juans of one flavor or another, some aggressive, some merely compulsive, some rather childlike. The aggressive Don Juan is the guy who gets his jollies from frustrating or humiliating a woman he’s seduced, and he seems almost relieved when he dumps her after a brief tryst. The more compulsive Don Juan is just plowing through the female population looking for the one woman who won’t disappoint him. Then there’s the infantile Don Juan, an effeminate man-child who succeeds with women precisely because he lacks those masculine qualities that some women find threatening.

自恋者归根结底是最糟糕的情人，他们身上集齐了能想象到的各种性功能障碍，并倾向于自私地利用自己的伴侣而毫无愧疚。异性恋和双性恋的自恋男性内心深处对女性怀有深深的嫉妒，并害怕被女性拒绝和嘲笑。他们往往会把伴侣物化为两类对象：要么是被理想化却被去性化的对象（"纯洁的"母亲，或圣母），要么是无需爱或仰慕便可随意享用的对象（"被玷污的"母亲，或妓女）。反过来，他们也可能对那些让他们性致全无的女性产生温柔的感情。许多人是这样那样的唐璜——有的咄咄逼人，有的只是强迫性地追逐，有的则颇为孩子气。咄咄逼人的唐璜是那种从挫败或羞辱被他勾引到手的女人中获得快感的家伙，而当他在一段短暂的幽会之后把她甩掉时，他几乎显得如释重负。更具强迫性的唐璜则只是在女性人群中横冲直撞，寻找那一个不会让他失望的女人。还有那种幼稚型的唐璜，一个女里女气的大孩子，他之所以能在女人面前得手，恰恰是因为他缺乏那些某些女性会感到具有威胁性的男子气概。

Narcissistic women tend to be cold and calculating, with considerable hostility toward both men and women. They will use a partner as long as the hapless lover allows it, but if that lover musters some self-respect and finally bolts, they may be angry and spiteful but will never pine away or feel guilty. The more promiscuous among them see a prince in every new admirer, only to lose interest when he turns out to be a frog after all.

自恋的女性往往冷酷而工于算计，对男性和女性都怀有相当大的敌意。只要那个倒霉的情人肯容忍，她们就会一直利用对方；可一旦那个情人鼓起一点自尊、最终抽身离去，她们或许会愤怒和怀恨，却绝不会为之憔悴或感到内疚。其中更为水性杨花的，会在每一个新的仰慕者身上看到一位王子，却又在发现对方终究不过是只青蛙时失去兴趣。


Rockets for Laura

为 Laura 而放的烟火

Laura floats through the door and throws herself on the couch, surrendering to the well-worn cushions. “I think I’m in love!” she announces dreamily, swooning as she kicks off her shoes. “Do tell,” says her roommate Beth, “and who would be the Dupe du Jour?”

Laura 飘然进门，一头扑倒在沙发上，整个人陷进那些用旧了的靠垫里。"我想我恋爱了！"她梦呓般地宣布，一边踢掉鞋子一边陶醉得快要晕过去。"快说说，"她的室友 Beth 说，"今天的这位上当受骗者又是谁啊？"

“His name is David,” Laura replies, ignoring the verbal dart. “We’ve been eying each other at Starbuck’s for over a week, and today he finally made the move. He bought me a double latte, and we’ve been talking for hours. You wouldn’t believe how much we have in common! He’s a film student at USC—his uncle is a producer!—and he’s doing this project, David is, on—oh, what’s his name? that guy who’s running for city council from West Hollywood? Anyway, I told him about how I worked on the mayor’s campaign and what politics is like on the inside. And then it turns out he goes rock climbing on weekends, he’s hooked on Thai food, and—get this!—he has not one but two Labrador retrievers! I mean, could we possibly have anything more in common? Oh, and did I mention, he has Leo DiCaprio’s mouth, and he thinks I look like Neve Campbell? This is just too perfect!

"他叫 David，"Laura 回答，不理会那句揶揄。"我们在星巴克互相眉来眼去已经一个多星期了，今天他终于主动出击。他给我买了一杯双份拿铁，我们聊了好几个小时。你绝对想不到我们有多少共同点！他是南加州大学的电影系学生——他叔叔是个制片人！——而 David 正在做这个项目，主题是关于——哦，他叫什么名字来着？那个代表西好莱坞竞选市议会的人？反正吧，我跟他讲了我怎么参与市长竞选活动、政治圈内部是什么样子的。然后结果发现他周末去攀岩，他迷恋泰国菜，还有——你听好了！——他养的不是一只而是两只拉布拉多寻回犬！我是说，我们还能有比这更多的共同点吗？哦，我有没有提到，他长着一张莱昂纳多·迪卡普里奥那样的嘴，而且他觉得我长得像 Neve Campbell？这简直太完美了！

Roll ’em, thinks Beth, who has seen this movie about five times now in the two years she has been sharing an apartment with Laura. Act One always plays like this: Laura comes home on Cloud Nine, certain she has met the Love of Her Life. In Act Two, Beth gets to meet The Guy, who has a few things going for him but is hardly the god Laura has portrayed him to be. The plot features a romance that takes off like a rocket, peaks somewhere in the ozone, and then, in Act Three, comes crashing back to Earth. The time frame usually spans several months, but sometimes it’s all over in a matter of weeks. The weirdest part of the story is that Laura always walks away from the crash without a scratch. Whatever disappointment she feels can usually be salved with a trip to the mall and a night on the town, where she can strut her stuff before the local swains. Beth is beginning to get the picture that, when it comes to love, Laura’s more interested in the launch than the journey—and her companion is just along for the ride.

开拍吧，Beth 心想。在与 Laura 合租公寓的这两年里，这部电影她已经看过大约五遍了。第一幕总是这样上演：Laura 喜不自胜地回到家，确信自己遇上了此生挚爱。到了第二幕，Beth 就会见到那位"如意郎君"，他确有几分长处，却根本不是 Laura 所描绘的那位天神。剧情的特点是一段像火箭般升空、在臭氧层某处达到顶点的恋情，然后在第三幕里轰然坠回地面。时间跨度通常是几个月，但有时几周之内就全剧终了。这故事最离奇的部分在于，Laura 每次都能从坠机中全身而退、毫发无伤。无论她感到怎样的失望，通常一趟逛商场加上一晚出门寻欢就能抚平——在那里她可以在本地的追求者面前展示自己的风采。Beth 开始看明白了：在爱情这件事上，比起整段旅程，Laura 对发射的那一刻更感兴趣——而她的同伴不过是搭个便车罢了。

Less adventuresome narcissistic women than Laura tend to settle into stable relationships with partners they consider to be “the very best.” Others may be fixated on unavailable love objects whose very remoteness permits them to remain hermetically sealed in their perfection, all the better to protect the Narcissist from the risk of being devalued in a real relationship. Sometimes they attach themselves to extremely narcissistic and accomplished partners, believing themselves to be the inspiration for the partner’s greatness or even ending up running that person’s life.

那些不像 Laura 那么爱冒险的自恋女性，往往会安顿于与她们眼中"最最优秀"的伴侣建立的稳定关系中。另一些人则可能执着于无法得到的爱的对象，正是这些对象的遥不可及，使她们得以将其完美地密封保存起来，从而更好地保护自恋者免遭在一段真实关系中被贬低的风险。有时她们会依附于极度自恋且成就斐然的伴侣，自认为是对方伟大成就的灵感源泉，甚至最终把那个人的生活打理得井井有条、操于股掌之间。

Some of these arrangements involve an exhibitionistic Narcissist and a partner who is “in the closet.” The closet Narcissist is an unassuming type who has her feelers out for someone she can idealize. She needs to put the love object on a pedestal in order to hold herself together, because if her partner is wonderful and she can have him, then all the insecurities inside her will go away. In the Narcissist-closet Narcissist couple, it is actually the latter who is in control, feeding the grandiosity of the love object in order to inflate herself via osmosis. These relationships can be quite successful, as long as the idealizations and illusions can be sustained. But when unpleasant reality intrudes, love implodes.

其中一些组合包含了一个爱出风头的自恋者和一个"隐藏型"的伴侣。隐藏型自恋者是那种不事张扬的人，她暗暗伸出触角，寻找一个可以让她加以理想化的对象。她需要把爱的对象捧上神坛，才能让自己维系不散，因为只要她的伴侣足够出色、而她又能拥有他，那么她内心所有的不安全感就会烟消云散。在自恋者与隐藏型自恋者构成的这一对中，实际上掌控全局的是后者——她不断喂养爱的对象的浮夸，以便通过渗透的方式来膨胀自己。只要理想化和幻想能够维持下去，这类关系可以相当成功。但当不愉快的现实闯入时，爱便轰然内爆。


Complementarity: The Love of Justin and Luisa

互补：Justin 与 Luisa 之爱

They met while Justin was still a promising young concert violinist and Luisa was studying merchandising at the local junior college. He admired her beauty and gentle demeanor, she the promise he represented of one day escaping a mundane blue-collar existence. Until she met him, all of Luisa’s hopes were pinned on becoming a buyer in a high-class department store. Now she saw her destiny in him—he would travel and become famous, and she would devote her life to helping him make his dreams come true. She was convinced that he was exceptional, and from the moment she met him, her world became larger and more meaningful.

他们相遇时，Justin 还是一位前途无量的青年音乐会小提琴家，而 Luisa 正在当地的社区学院学习商品营销。他欣赏她的美貌和温柔的举止，她则欣赏他所代表的那种希望——有朝一日摆脱平庸的蓝领生活。在遇见他之前，Luisa 的全部希望都寄托在成为一家高档百货公司的采购员上。如今，她在他身上看到了自己的命运——他将四处巡演、声名鹊起，而她将倾尽一生去帮助他实现梦想。她深信他出类拔萃，而从遇见他的那一刻起，她的世界就变得更广阔、更有意义了。

Justin had never known anyone quite like Luisa. There had been many women in his life, but each had become tiresome after a time. They had always turned out to be selfish and demanding, not understanding of his goals and the delicate temperament of a gifted musician.

Justin 从未认识过任何像 Luisa 这样的人。他的生命中曾有过许多女人，但每一个过了一段时间都变得令人厌烦。她们最终总被证明是自私而苛求的，无法理解他的目标，也无法理解一位天才音乐家那纤细敏感的气质。

In Justin’s eyes, Luisa was really quite enchanting. He loved her lustrous dark hair and delicate hands and the way she laughed at his jokes. She was one of the few women he had ever encountered who consistently “got” his humor. It was as if they shared one mind. And she loved his music. She would beg him to play for her and was delighted with his compositions. She believed he could do anything, and since she had come into his life, he, too, felt his star was rising.

在 Justin 眼中，Luisa 实在相当迷人。他爱她那一头乌黑光泽的秀发、纤细的双手，以及她听他讲笑话时发笑的样子。她是他所遇见过的、为数不多能始终"领会"他幽默的女人之一。仿佛他们心意相通、共享同一个头脑。而且她热爱他的音乐。她会央求他为她演奏，并为他的作曲而欣喜不已。她相信他无所不能，而自从她走进他的生命，他也觉得自己正星运高照、节节攀升。

So sure was Justin of his future that he grew intolerant of any career frustration that seemed to challenge his sense of superiority. His peers in the orchestra found him arrogant and increasingly difficult to get along with. Although he was younger than most of the others, he seemed to expect them to recognize that he was special. When the first chair became vacant and was offered to someone else, he walked out of rehearsal. He was summarily fired.

Justin 对自己的前程是如此笃定，以至于他变得无法容忍任何似乎挑战其优越感的事业挫折。乐团里的同行们觉得他傲慢，而且越来越难以相处。尽管他比大多数人都年轻，他却似乎期望他们承认他是与众不同的。当首席的位置空出来、却被让给了别人时，他径直离开了排练。他立刻被解雇了。

Luisa was as mystified as Justin was outraged, but she was certain that better things were in store for him as a solo performer of his own compositions. She soothed and comforted him endlessly, urging him to look at this turn of events as an opportunity to redirect his career. She could support them while he made the magic happen. She knew it wouldn’t be long.

Luisa 和愤怒的 Justin 一样困惑不解，但她确信，作为一名演奏自己作品的独奏家，更美好的前程正等待着他。她不停地安抚、宽慰他，敦促他把这一变故看作重新规划事业的契机。在他施展魔法、成就一番事业的同时，她可以养活他们两个人。她知道，那一天不会太遥远。

Weeks passed, and then months, as Justin nursed his deeply wounded pride and tried half-heartedly to resurrect his star. He brooded, he drank, he slept late, and occasionally he made phone calls to agents or picked up his abandoned violin. Luisa tried to encourage him, but her patience began to falter and she could feel the disillusionment creeping in. Her ministrations became mechanical, but Justin never noticed.

几个星期过去了，接着又是几个月，Justin 抚舔着自己深受重创的自尊，三心二意地试图让自己的星运起死回生。他郁郁寡欢，他酗酒，他睡到日上三竿，偶尔给经纪人打打电话，或者拿起那把被他弃置一旁的小提琴。Luisa 试着鼓励他，但她的耐心开始动摇，她能感觉到幻灭正悄然蔓延而来。她的悉心照料变得机械麻木，可 Justin 从未察觉。

One day, alone in their apartment, Justin stumbled across Luisa’s diary and, without hesitation, he began to thumb through its contents. He was shocked by what he read. Luisa—his Luisa!—had poured out her heart … about another man! She was in love, or so it seemed, with someone whom she described as her soulmate. The relationship had been going on for some time, and he was on the verge of losing her. Impossible!

一天，独自待在他们的公寓里时，Justin 偶然翻到了 Luisa 的日记，于是毫不犹豫地开始翻看里面的内容。他被读到的东西惊呆了。Luisa——他的 Luisa！——竟在里面倾诉衷肠……写的是另一个男人！她爱上了，至少看上去是爱上了，一个被她形容为灵魂伴侣的人。这段关系已经持续了一段时间，而他正濒临失去她的边缘。不可能！

From that day forward, Justin could not look at Luisa in the same way. She had fallen from the pedestal he had erected for her, smashed to the ground, and like Humpty-Dumpty, she couldn’t be put back together again. When he confronted her, she tried to reassure him, but clearly the light had gone out of her eyes, too. It was over, and, Justin thought ruefully, she was just like the rest after all.

从那天起，Justin 再也无法用从前的眼光看待 Luisa 了。她已从他为她筑起的神坛上跌落，摔得粉碎，而且像那个蛋人 Humpty-Dumpty 一样，再也无法被重新拼凑起来。当他质问她时，她试图让他安心，可她眼中的光芒显然也已经熄灭了。一切都结束了，Justin 懊丧地想，她到头来还是和其余那些人一个样。

Luisa, for her part, was left feeling strangely flat. She tried to connect with her feelings. Well, there was resentment toward Justin for letting her down and guilt about her longings for another man, but when all was said and done, Luisa mostly just felt empty. Surely there was more to love than this. Next time, she vowed, she would have to set her sights higher.

至于 Luisa，她则莫名地感到一阵索然无味。她试着去触碰自己内心的感受。嗯，是有一些对 Justin 让她失望的怨恨，也有一些对自己渴望另一个男人的内疚，但说到底，Luisa 主要只是感到一片空虚。爱情想必不止于此吧。她暗下决心，下一次，她一定要把眼光放得更高一些。

The narcissistic love match is inherently unstable. Any intrusion of reality can destabilize the relationship, leading to chronic or intermittent conflict, misery, trips to the couple counselor, or dramatic ruptures that bring the union to an end.

自恋式的爱情组合本质上是不稳定的。现实的任何一点闯入都可能动摇这段关系，导致长期或间歇性的冲突、痛苦、求助于伴侣咨询师，或是以戏剧性的决裂而告终的结合。

When the Narcissist can find support outside the relationship—career, family, friends, or other interests—that keep him or her feeling pumped up, the pressure on the partner may be minimal. But frustrations at work, job loss or retirement, disruptions in other needed relationships, and losses in status or rewards from other pipelines usually lead to more demands on the partner to pick up the slack.

当自恋者能在这段关系之外找到支持——事业、家庭、朋友或其他兴趣爱好——来让自己持续处于膨胀的状态时，伴侣所承受的压力可能是微乎其微的。但工作上的挫折、失业或退休、其他所需关系的破裂，以及来自其他供给渠道的地位或回报的丧失，通常都会导致他对伴侣提出更多要求，让对方来填补这一空缺。

It is the nature of human beings to seek more satisfying solutions to life’s challenges over time and to strive toward a fully realized evolution of Self. Even a seed of emotional health wants to grow. Just as primary narcissism is a transient state in early childhood, so may narcissistic relationships be way stations on our journey to mature love. But sometimes the hard part is figuring out if, when, and how to move on.

随着时间的推移，去寻求对人生挑战更令人满意的解决之道、并努力迈向自我的充分实现与演进，乃是人之天性。哪怕只是一粒情感健康的种子，也渴望生长。正如原发性自恋只是幼年时期一种短暂的状态，自恋式的关系也可能只是我们通往成熟之爱旅途中的一处中转站。但有时，难的地方在于弄清楚：是否要往前走，何时往前走，以及如何往前走。


Guidelines for Survival: When You Are in Love with a Narcissist

生存指南：当你爱上一个自恋者时

What draws us into love relationships with Narcissists? For one thing, they really are “special,” although not always in the ways they would like to be. Their tendency to pursue idealized fantasies can spin a magic web around mundane reality, appealing to your need for glamour and excitement. And when their hunger for admiration makes them want to please you, it can feel like real love. In some cases, their grandiosity may seem to offer something that is missing from your life. Even their arrogance can be appealing if your self-esteem is a little shaky and you’re easily impressed with someone who seems so confident. And to be the love choice of such a celestial body—ooh-la-la!—can be quite intoxicating, at least for a while.

是什么把我们引入与自恋者的爱情关系之中呢？首先，他们确实是"特别的"，尽管未必是以他们所希望的那种方式。他们追逐理想化幻想的倾向，能在平淡无奇的现实周围编织出一张魔法之网，迎合你对璀璨与刺激的需求。而当他们对仰慕的渴求使他们想要取悦你时，那感觉简直就像真正的爱。在某些情况下，他们的浮夸似乎能提供某种你生命中所缺失的东西。即便是他们的傲慢，倘若你的自尊有点摇摆不定、又容易被一个看上去如此自信的人所打动，也可能颇具吸引力。而能够成为这样一颗璀璨天体所选中的爱人——哦啦啦！——至少在一段时间内，是相当令人陶醉的。

Passionate love, with its obsessions, longings for merger, attendant feelings of grandiosity and omnipotence, and deep ties to our primitive past, is so full of narcissistic elements that it is often difficult to distinguish what is deliriously normal from what is essentially unhealthy. If my beloved doesn’t always understand me or put my needs first, does that mean he’s unempathic or she’s selfish? If I’m expected to do things I don’t particularly want to do from time to time, does that mean I’m being abused or exploited? Is it wrong to want to be in control? No, no, and no. So how do I know if my love relationship is healthy or narcissistic?

热烈的爱情——连同它的痴迷、对融合的渴望、随之而来的浮夸与全能之感，以及与我们原始过往之间的深刻联系——充满了如此多的自恋成分，以至于人们往往难以分辨什么是令人神魂颠倒的正常，什么又是本质上不健康的东西。如果我的爱人并不总是理解我、并不总是把我的需求放在首位，这是否意味着他缺乏共情、或者她很自私？如果我时不时被要求去做一些我并不特别想做的事，这是否意味着我正在遭受虐待或剥削？想要掌控局面，难道有错吗？不，不，都不是。那么，我该如何知道我的爱情关系究竟是健康的，还是自恋式的呢？

The core element of a relationship that is fundamentally narcissistic is the Fusion Delusion, that fantasy that we are, or should be, completely and forever One. Separateness is a threat. Envy lurks around every corner. Boundaries are not respected because they don’t exist to begin with. A power imbalance, with domination on one side and submission on the other, is common, although the partners may swap roles from time to time. This does not make the relationship reciprocal, because genuine mutuality is also nonexistent. If any of this seems uncomfortably familiar, let’s see how the Survival Strategies apply when you’re in love.

一段从根本上说是自恋的关系，其核心要素便是融合妄想，即那种幻想——我们是、或者应该是，完完全全且永永远远地合为一体。独立是一种威胁。嫉妒潜伏在每一个角落。边界得不到尊重，因为它们从一开始就根本不存在。一方支配、另一方屈从的权力失衡是很常见的，尽管双方可能时不时互换角色。但这并不能使这段关系成为互惠的，因为真正的相互性同样并不存在。如果上述任何一点让你感到一种不安的熟悉，那么让我们来看看，当你身处恋爱之中时，这些生存策略该如何运用。

Strategy One: Know Yourself

策略一：认识你自己

Our earliest experiences of loving and being loved replay throughout our lives, and love’s longings may beckon us all to revisit those toddler days of union with a wonderful and powerful Mother. Each of the developmental dramas in the Birth of Me—symbiosis, practicing, and rapprochement—shape our perceptions of Self and others in ways that determine who and how we love. If we were securely attached in childhood to someone who nurtured our autonomy, we will choose a partner who does the same and will not need fantasy to sustain love. But if our autonomy was thwarted, stranding us on the outskirts of separation-individuation, we may re-enact the dynamics of fusion, grandiosity, and omnipotence with others whose personal boundaries are similarly blurred.

我们最早关于爱与被爱的经历，会在我们的一生中反复重演，而爱的渴望或许会召唤我们所有人，去重访幼年时与一位美好而强大的母亲合为一体的那些日子。"我的诞生"中的每一幕发展剧——共生、实践和重新趋近——都以决定我们爱谁以及如何去爱的方式，塑造着我们对自我与他人的感知。如果我们在童年时安稳地依恋于一个滋养了我们自主性的人，我们就会选择一个同样如此的伴侣，并且无需依靠幻想来维系爱情。但如果我们的自主性受到了阻挠，把我们搁浅在分离-个体化的边缘地带，我们就可能与他人重演那种融合、浮夸与全能的互动模式——那些人的个人边界也同样模糊不清。

For some of us, the period of symbiosis—when we first became psychologically merged with Mother—was anything but blissful. If she was nonresponsive during this stage, we may find ourselves longing for love without really expecting that it will ever come. If, on the other hand, she was overly intrusive, we may habitually defend our boundaries from any and all invaders, including the well-intentioned. When we come from such “misattuned” backgrounds, to love is to be unfulfilled, and to let love in is to invite engulfment and annihilation. Only fantasy love, love with built-in obstacles, or unrequited love may seem safe enough. The Narcissist may seem like a good match for our low expectations.

对我们当中的一些人来说，共生期——也就是我们最初在心理上与母亲合为一体的那段时期——绝非什么幸福美满的时光。如果她在这一阶段毫无回应，我们便可能发现自己渴望着爱，却又并不真正指望它会到来。反过来，如果她过度侵扰，我们便可能习惯性地抵御任何及一切入侵者，包括那些怀着善意的人。当我们来自这种"失调"的背景时，去爱就意味着得不到满足，而让爱进来就意味着招致被吞没和被毁灭。唯有幻想中的爱、自带障碍的爱，或是单恋，才显得足够安全。自恋者，看上去或许正与我们那低低的期待相般配。

If you grew up in a family in which one or both parents were Narcissists, chances are good that you are vulnerable to love relationships that have a distinctly narcissistic flavor. Here are some of the ways this legacy might affect you now:

如果你成长于一个父母中有一方或双方都是自恋者的家庭，那么你很有可能容易陷入那种带有鲜明自恋色彩的爱情关系。以下是这份遗产可能在当下影响你的一些方式：

• You fall in love quickly or frequently, or conversely, you are afraid to let someone get close for fear that you will be exploited or rejected.

• 你坠入爱河又快又频繁；或者相反，你害怕让任何人靠近，唯恐自己会被利用或被拒绝。

• Only “perfect love” seems safe or exciting enough, or, at the other end of the spectrum, you usually pick inappropriate or unavailable partners.

• 只有"完美的爱"才显得足够安全或足够令人兴奋；或者，处于另一极端，你通常会挑选不合适或无法得到的伴侣。

• After you’ve known them awhile and the first blush of romance fades, loved ones seem to reveal flaws or become difficult.

• 在你认识对方一段时间、最初那阵恋爱的甜蜜消退之后，所爱的人似乎就开始暴露出缺点，或变得难以相处。

• You’ve never been in love and can’t imagine what it would be like, or the flip side, you can never seem to get enough love from someone you care about.

• 你从未恋爱过，也无法想象那会是什么样子；或者反过来，你似乎永远无法从一个你在乎的人那里得到足够的爱。

• You have trouble giving, or taking, in an intimate relationship.

• 在一段亲密关系中，你在给予或接受方面有困难。

• You need to idealize, or devalue, a loved one in order to feel good about yourself.

• 你需要把所爱之人理想化或贬低，才能让自己感觉良好。

• You have trouble apologizing to your loved one, or just the opposite, you take responsibility for everything unpleasant that happens between you.

• 你很难向所爱之人道歉；或者恰恰相反，你把你们之间发生的一切不愉快都揽到自己身上、独自承担。

• You often feel hurt, or disappointed, in love.

• 在爱情中，你常常感到受伤或失望。

Remember, no one had a perfect childhood, and we all experience conflict in close relationships from time to time. But if you feel you tend to lose yourself in an intimate relationship or have difficulty recognizing someone else’s needs and rights, if you are threatened or confused about boundaries, you will need to try to figure out what is going on in you and in the relationship that may be a re-enactment of unhealthy narcissism. If possible, try to engage your partner in an exploration of the problems. The openness to explore this together is a good sign. One of the best ways to recover from the impact of unhealthy narcissism is to create a healthy love relationship in the present. This begins with respectful, empathic communication and an understanding of each other’s “buttons.”

请记住，没有人拥有完美的童年，我们所有人都会时不时在亲密关系中经历冲突。但如果你觉得自己倾向于在一段亲密关系中迷失自我，或者难以识别他人的需求和权利，如果你对边界感到受威胁或困惑，你就需要努力弄清楚，你自身以及这段关系中正在发生的、可能是不健康自恋之重演的，究竟是什么。如有可能，试着让你的伴侣一同参与对这些问题的探究。愿意一起探讨这件事，本身就是一个好兆头。从不健康自恋的冲击中恢复过来的最佳途径之一，就是在当下建立一段健康的爱情关系。这要从相互尊重、富有共情的沟通，以及对彼此"敏感按钮"的了解开始。

Strategy Two: Embrace Reality

策略二：拥抱现实

Your accurate assessment of your own strengths and weaknesses, as well as those of your partner and of the relationship, is your best tool in managing unhealthy narcissism. You will have to look past any need you may have for perfection, as well as whatever distortions your partner contributes to making things unreal. This can be painful and difficult, because it involves disengaging from the Fusion Delusion-to whatever extent this has been a part of your relationship. You are actually recreating that developmental step from early childhood in which you begin to see yourself and the object of your affection as having both good and bad qualities, in other words, being whole, complex, and ultimately imperfect. To be able to do this without overwhelming rage or shame is a good sign.

你对自己长处与短处的准确评估，以及对你的伴侣和这段关系的长处与短处的准确评估，是你应对不健康自恋的最佳工具。你将不得不越过你自身可能存在的对完美的需求，也越过你的伴侣为把事情弄得失真而添加的种种扭曲。这可能是痛苦而艰难的，因为它涉及到从融合妄想中抽身——无论这种妄想在多大程度上一直是你们关系的一部分。你实际上是在重现幼年时期的那一发展步骤：在那一步里，你开始把自己和你钟情的对象都看作兼具好坏品质的存在，换句话说，看作完整、复杂、并最终并不完美的人。能够做到这一点而不被压倒性的暴怒或羞耻所淹没，是一个好兆头。

Many people fear the loss of idealization in a love relationship. They think they won’t be able to keep love alive without a sense of mystery based on hiding flaws, denying defects, and obscuring unpleasant truths. They need to create and maintain a fantasy world that protects their love from harsh reality. If this is your theory of love, it will take a great deal of courage to test that hypothesis. What you stand to gain is the freedom to be yourself and to be loved for who you really are, warts and all. Healthy lovers are able to idealize their loved one’s unique real attributes with full awareness of their equally real shortcomings. They don’t expect perfection, perfect attunement to their needs, or perfect compliance with their wishes. Instead, they relish the ways they are challenged and enriched by interaction with someone truly separate.

许多人害怕在一段爱情关系中失去理想化。他们认为，倘若没有一种建立在掩饰缺点、否认瑕疵、模糊不快真相之上的神秘感，他们就无法让爱保持鲜活。他们需要创造并维持一个幻想世界，以保护他们的爱不受残酷现实的侵扰。如果这就是你关于爱的理论，那么去检验这一假设将需要极大的勇气。而你有望获得的，是做你自己、并因你真实的样子（连同所有的瑕疵）而被爱的那份自由。健康的爱者能够把所爱之人独特而真实的特质加以理想化，同时又充分意识到他们同样真实的缺点。他们不期待完美，不期待对方完美契合自己的需求，也不期待对方完美顺从自己的意愿。相反，他们津津有味地享受着与一个真正独立的人互动时所受到的挑战与丰盈。

Strategy Three: Set Boundaries

策略三：设立边界

Good self-boundaries are a sign of separateness and emotional strength. Evaluate the health of your love relationship by considering the following boundary issues and how you and your partner negotiate any differences you may have:

良好的自我边界，是独立性和情感力量的标志。请通过思考以下这些边界问题，以及你和你的伴侣如何协商你们之间可能存在的任何分歧，来评估你们爱情关系的健康程度：

• How do you handle separate time, separate friends, and separate interests?

• 你们如何处理各自独立的时间、各自的朋友和各自的兴趣爱好？

• How do you make decisions about how to use your leisure time?

• 关于如何利用闲暇时间，你们是怎样做决定的？

• Is there privacy within the relationship, or do you open each other’s mail; go through each other’s purse, wallet, or pockets without asking; eavesdrop on each other’s phone conversations or read each other’s e-mail?

• 这段关系中是否存在隐私，还是说你们会拆看彼此的信件、未经询问就翻查彼此的钱包、皮夹或口袋、偷听彼此的电话或阅读彼此的电子邮件？

• Can you have your own thoughts, feelings, or other personal experiences without having to explain or describe them to your partner unless you want to?

• 你能否拥有属于自己的想法、感受或其他个人体验，而不必向你的伴侣解释或描述它们——除非你自己愿意？

• Are you able to disagree without feeling wounded or angry?

• 你们能否在意见不合时不感到受伤或愤怒？

• What has happened when—or what do you think would happen if—you insisted on creating or enforcing such boundaries? Are you able to ask without fear of an angry or shaming response, or some kind or retaliation? Would your partner listen and respond with respect?

• 当你坚持建立或维护这样的边界时，曾发生过什么——或者你认为会发生什么？你能否在提出要求时，不必担心招来愤怒或羞辱式的回应、或某种报复？你的伴侣会带着尊重去倾听并回应吗？

All couples must negotiate boundaries that are comfortable for both partners. How you resolve your differences—with respect and accommodation, or with manipulation and anger—can be a measure of the unhealthy narcissism in your relationship. If boundary conflicts are a source of discomfort, consider whether there are narcissistic forces at work.

所有伴侣都必须协商出让双方都感到舒适的边界。你们如何化解分歧——是带着尊重与迁就，还是带着操纵与愤怒——可以用来衡量你们关系中不健康自恋的程度。如果边界冲突是不适的根源，那就想一想，是否有自恋的力量在其中作祟。

Some unhealthy relationships have boundaries that are too rigid. Two people coexist in their own separate worlds without ever having moments of merger, much less a continuing sense of deep connection. Can you get your partner’s attention when you really need to? Would each of you be willing to make a sacrifice for the other’s well-being? Is your sexual relationship satisfying, or is it wooden and perfunctory? Do you often feel alone while with this person? When two very self-absorbed people find themselves in a relationship of convenience, the “love” may be of the self-aggrandizing variety, an attachment that confers, for example, wealth or social advantage and the illusion of specialness. In this case, there is no Fusion Delusion but rather a deep resistance to becoming vulnerable and surrendering to the transcendent experience of passionate love.

有些不健康的关系，其边界则过于僵硬。两个人各自在彼此分隔的世界里共存，从不曾有过交融的时刻，更谈不上一种持续的深层连接感。当你真正需要时，你能引起伴侣的注意吗？你们各自是否愿意为对方的福祉做出牺牲？你们的性关系是令人满足的，还是僵硬而敷衍的？和这个人在一起时，你是否常常感到孤独？当两个极度以自我为中心的人发现彼此身处一段权宜性的关系中时，那种"爱"可能属于自我夸大的类型——一种能带来诸如财富或社会优势、以及那种自命不凡之错觉的依附。在这种情况下，不存在融合妄想，而是存在一种对变得脆弱、对屈服于热烈之爱那种超越性体验的深深抗拒。

Strategy Four: Cultivate Reciprocal Relationships

策略四：培养互惠的关系

The hallmark of mature love is reciprocity, in which two people have as much regard for each other as they do for themselves. The Narcissist, in contrast, does not love in any mature, reciprocal, or nurturant sense of the word. It is all about Me. In narcissistic love, one person’s Self is submerged in the service of propping up another’s fragile ego. Rather than the self-transcendence of two, there is only the self-inflation of one.

成熟之爱的标志是互惠，在这种互惠中，两个人对彼此的尊重一如对自身的尊重。与之相反，自恋者并不会以任何成熟、互惠或滋养性的意义去爱。一切都关乎"我"。在自恋式的爱里，一个人的自我被淹没，沦为支撑另一个人脆弱自我的工具。这里没有两个人的自我超越，有的只是一个人的自我膨胀。

Reciprocity is about give and take. Narcissists may think of themselves as givers, but they only give what they want. This is selfish giving, without recognition of what others want or need. It’s really more a convoluted form of taking, self-deluded and self-inflating. If someone consistently experiences another’s real longing as a demand or an imposition and responds with annoyance, distancing, or contempt, there is no reciprocity. Conversely, those who are unsure of their own worth and uncomfortable with asking or taking make their own complementary contribution to lopsided narcissistic love. Reciprocity requires self-esteem on both sides of the equation.

互惠关乎给予与索取。自恋者或许自认为是给予者，但他们只给予自己想给的东西。这是一种自私的给予，全然不顾他人想要或需要什么。它实际上更像是一种迂回曲折的索取，既自我欺骗又自我膨胀。如果一个人始终把另一个人真切的渴望体验为一种要求或一种强加，并以恼怒、疏远或蔑视来回应，那就不存在互惠。反过来，那些对自身价值没有把握、对开口索求或接受感到不自在的人，也对这种失衡的自恋之爱做出了他们那一份相辅相成的"贡献"。互惠要求等式两端都具备自尊。

True reciprocity also requires mutual trust. If you are having trouble with this aspect of an intimate relationship, see if you can let down your guard with a friend or family member, someone with whom you feel safe. Once you know what it feels like to be both a giver and a receiver, you may be able to translate that experience to a romantic relationship. To be successful, however, you will need to choose a partner who is not a Narcissist. Lose your heart to love, but keep your Self for you.
