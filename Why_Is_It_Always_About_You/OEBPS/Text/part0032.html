Becoming Better Parents

20 成为更好的父母

RECENTLY , A ROPER NATIONAL YOUTH SURVEY asked teenagers what they thought was wrong with America today. They were given fifteen options to choose from, and these were their top four choices: selfishness/people not thinking of the rights of others (56 percent), people who don’t respect the law and the authorities (52 percent), wrongdoing by politicians (48 percent), and lack of parental discipline of children and teens (47 percent). The young people, it seems, are well aware of the narcissism that floods our world.

最近，一项Roper全国青少年调查询问青少年，他们认为当今美国出了什么问题。调查给出十五个选项供他们挑选，而以下是票数最高的四项：自私自利／人们不顾及他人的权利（百分之五十六）、人们不尊重法律与权威（百分之五十二）、政客的不当行为（百分之四十八），以及父母对儿童和青少年缺乏管教（百分之四十七）。看来，年轻人对这个泛滥于我们世界的自恋现象心知肚明。

Most of the narcissism in turn-of-the-century American society is a product of two things: cultural influences and faulty parenting. The two are closely linked. When the baby boomers threw off the shackles of traditional values as repressive and unenlightened and the point of reference became peers who were trying to reinvent parenting to suit their own self-centered needs, what we got was a whole lot of people justifying choices that had less to do with what was good, or even good enough, for children than with what fulfilled parents as individuals. In the pursuit of their own happiness, too many parents have indulged in grandiosity, omnipotence, envy, and perfectionism, ignoring the separate needs of their children and misunderstanding or misreading the developmental underpinnings of their sometimes challenging behavior.

世纪之交美国社会中的大部分自恋，是两样东西的产物：文化影响与失当的养育。二者紧密相连。当婴儿潮一代把传统价值观当作压抑而蒙昧的枷锁加以挣脱，参照系变成了那些试图按照自身以自我为中心的需求重新发明养育之道的同龄人时，我们得到的便是一大群为自己的选择辩护的人——而那些选择，与其说关乎什么对孩子是好的、甚至够好的，不如说关乎什么能让作为个体的父母得到满足。在追求自身幸福的过程中，太多父母沉溺于夸大、全能、嫉妒和完美主义，无视孩子作为独立个体的需求，误解或误读了孩子那些时而棘手的行为背后的发展根源。

Look around. Do you know parents who:

环顾四周。你是否认识这样的父母：

· 对孩子抱有不切实际的期望？

• Micromanage their children’s lives without regard for their feelings and wishes?

· 不顾孩子的感受与愿望，对孩子的生活事无巨细地横加干涉？

· 借孩子来活出自己的人生？

• Use their children to meet their own needs?

· 利用孩子来满足自己的需求？

• Infect their children with their fears and anxieties?

· 把自己的恐惧与焦虑传染给孩子？

• Treat their children as friends or confidants?

· 把孩子当作朋友或倾诉知己？

• Put their children in the middle of conflicts with the other parent?

· 让孩子夹在自己与另一方父母的冲突中间？

How about parents who are:

还有这样的父母呢：

• Overly controlling?

· 过度控制？

• Intrusive?

· 侵入越界？

• Sexually suggestive or coarse in a child’s presence?

· 在孩子面前言行带有性暗示或粗俗不堪？

• So busy with their own lives that they can’t participate in their children’s?

Two narcissistic conceits have governed our journey to the current culture of narcissism. One is that I can have it all—and should. The other is that no one should be allowed to interfere with my pursuit of happiness and self-fulfillment. These two notions have clearly found their way into modern parenting practices, ensuring that the Seven Deadly Sins of Narcissism will be with us for some time to come.

有两种自恋的自负，主导了我们走向当下这一自恋文化的历程。其一是：我能够拥有一切——而且理应如此。其二是：任何人都不应被允许妨碍我对幸福与自我实现的追求。这两种观念显然已渗入现代的养育实践之中，确保了自恋七宗罪还将与我们相伴一段时日。


Seven Parental Attitudes That Create Narcissistic Children

造就自恋孩子的七种父母态度

1. My child is special and deserves to have everything, every experience, and every advantage. If I can’t provide it, society should.

1. 我的孩子很特别，理应拥有一切、体验一切、享有一切优势。如果我提供不了，社会就应该提供。

The word “special” has lost its meaning in today’s world. It used to mean possessed of some unusual quality or superior in some way.

"特别"一词在当今世界已失去了它的含义。它从前的意思是拥有某种不同寻常的品质，或在某方面更为出众。

Now, many people view feeling special as an intrinsic part of self-esteem and expect, as a matter of routine, to be made to feel special by others. “I didn’t feel special,” we hear people say, as if they were somehow gypped. This is narcissistic entitlement, and it is an unconscious and pervasive part of the way we have come to think.

如今，许多人把"觉得自己特别"视为自尊不可或缺的一部分，并理所当然地期待别人让自己感到特别。"我没觉得自己特别，"我们听人这样说，仿佛他们莫名其妙被坑了一笔。这就是自恋式的特权感，它已成为我们思维方式中一个无意识而又无所不在的组成部分。

Certainly, every child is special to his or her parents, or we hope that they are. Children are also unique persons and entitled to be treated with kindness and respect for their individuality. But there is no reason others should regard my child as “special” just because I do. That attitude is rooted in a narcissistic failure to recognize that others are separate and not obliged to think and feel exactly as we do.

当然，每个孩子对其父母而言都是特别的——或者说我们希望如此。孩子也是独一无二的个体，有权得到善待，其个性也理应受到尊重。但没有任何理由要求别人仅仅因为我把孩子看得特别，就也得把他看得"特别"。这种态度，根源在于一种自恋式的认知失败：未能认识到他人是独立的个体，并没有义务与我们想得一模一样、感受得一模一样。

In America, children are entitled to protection from abuse and neglect, and to a free public education. There are many, many other things that we would like children to have, but it pretty much falls on their parents, or the generosity of others, to provide these things. They are not entitlements. It may indeed “take a village” to raise a child, in the sense that the larger community and society have an investment in that child’s welfare and healthy development, but children are first and foremost the responsibility of the people who bring them into the world or those who legally adopt or care for them. Most parents expect to provide for their children, even if they have to make sacrifices to do it. Unfortunately, not all parents share this sense of responsibility.

在美国，儿童有权免受虐待和忽视，有权接受免费的公共教育。我们还希望孩子能拥有许许多多别的东西，但提供这些东西基本上要落在他们父母身上，或仰仗他人的慷慨。它们并不是孩子理所当然该得的。养育一个孩子或许确实"需要举全村之力"，意即更大的社区与社会对这个孩子的福祉和健康成长都负有一份投入；但归根结底，孩子首先是那些把他们带到这世上的人、或那些合法收养或照料他们的人的责任。大多数父母都打算供养自己的孩子，哪怕为此不得不有所牺牲。遗憾的是，并非所有父母都怀有这份责任感。

The child—or adult—who expects to be treated as special is doomed to a lifetime of disappointment and relationship problems when others fail to reflect his or her unreal self-image.

一个期待被当作特别之人对待的孩子——或成年人——注定要一生失望，并在人际关系上麻烦不断，因为他人无法反射出他或她那个不真实的自我形象。

2. My child should never suffer. (If my child is unhappy, I am a bad parent.) Failure is always a negative experience and should be avoided at all costs.

2. 我的孩子绝不应该受苦。（如果我的孩子不开心，那就是我这个父母当得不称职。）失败永远是一种负面的体验，应当不惜一切代价加以避免。

Let’s face it: Suffering is part of the human condition. As much as we would like to shield children from pain, sometimes things happen that are outside our control. Sometimes these things are of tragic proportion, such as disease, disaster, and even death. Yet most of the time they are very ordinary, such as losing in competition or not getting to have the latest fad toy. Parents today turn themselves inside out to spare their children sufferings both large and small. One has only to look at the frenzied demand for particular toy items during the holiday season, or the pressure to keep up with the latest fads in kiddie culture. But these parents are missing an important point when they put themselves through such extraordinary hoops. Children, like adults, can be helped to face life’s disappointments or even crises and to become stronger for having done so. With good parental support, obstacles and even failures are what build character.

让我们正视这一点：受苦是人之常情的一部分。尽管我们多么希望为孩子挡去痛苦，可有时事情的发生超出了我们的掌控。有时这些事是悲剧性的，比如疾病、灾难，乃至死亡。然而大多数时候它们都极为寻常，比如竞赛落败，或没能得到最新流行的玩具。今天的父母为了让孩子免受大大小小的痛苦而绞尽脑汁。只需看看节日季里人们对某些特定玩具近乎疯狂的抢购，或是追赶儿童文化中最新潮流的压力便知。但这些父母在让自己穿过如此非同寻常的重重火圈时，却忽略了一个重要的道理。孩子和成年人一样，是可以在帮助下去面对人生的失望甚至危机，并因此变得更坚强的。在父母良好的支持下，障碍乃至失败正是塑造品格的东西。

Feeling tender-hearted toward one’s child is a good quality, but sometimes what makes it difficult for parents to tolerate a child’s suffering is that the parent feels it is happening to him or her. Our pain is not our children’s pain but rather something that may have been triggered by overidentification with the child. This is harmful in two ways: One, it takes away from the child’s experience by making it “all about me” and depriving the child of empathic support at a time of distress, and two, we sometimes go too far to “control away” the pain we cannot tolerate. If we cannot tolerate our children’s pain, we risk creating an unreal world of indulgence and anxious overcontrol. Not only does the child come to believe in entitlement to that unreal world, but he or she also misses opportunities to master distress.

对孩子心怀柔软是一种好品质，但有时父母之所以难以忍受孩子受苦，是因为父母觉得受苦的是自己。我们的痛苦并不是孩子的痛苦，而很可能是因对孩子过度认同而被勾起的某种东西。这在两个方面有害：其一，它把一切都变成"关乎我"，从而抢走了孩子的体验，并在孩子苦恼之际剥夺了他所需的共情支持；其二，我们有时会为了"控制掉"自己无法忍受的痛苦而做得太过。如果我们容不下孩子的痛苦，就有可能营造出一个由纵容和焦虑的过度控制构成的不真实世界。这样一来，孩子不仅会以为自己理所当然该拥有那个不真实的世界，还会错失掌控苦恼的机会。

These are the children who become “entitlement monsters,” and those tantrums we see are not just bad behavior, they represent a genuine lack of skill in responding to the day-to-day obstacles of life. Children need opportunities to master feelings of frustration, loss, disappointment, loneliness, boredom, envy, guilt, and anger—and help doing so from compassionate, available adults. They need experiences that give them a realistic sense of themselves in the world: winners and losers, good and bad, self-reliant and dependent, givers and takers, assertive and compassionate.

正是这些孩子日后成了"特权怪物"，而我们所看到的那些发脾气并不只是行为不端，它们反映出孩子在应对日常生活障碍时确实缺乏技能。孩子需要机会去掌控挫败、失落、失望、孤独、无聊、嫉妒、内疚和愤怒等情绪——也需要富有同情心、随时在场的成年人在这方面给予帮助。他们需要那些能让自己对自身在世上的位置形成现实认知的经历：有胜也有败，有好也有坏，既能自立也会依赖，既是给予者也是索取者，既能坚定主张也心怀慈悲。

3. What’s good for me is good for my child. If the sacrifices I make for my child interfere with my self-actualization, my unhappiness will be worse for my child than if I didn’t make sacrifices in the first place.

3. 对我好的，就对我的孩子好。如果我为孩子所做的牺牲妨碍了我的自我实现，那么我的不快乐对孩子的危害，会比我当初根本不做牺牲还要大。

This attitude is rooted in a parent’s failure to see the separateness of his or her child. The more difficult truth is that parents’ and children’s needs are often very different, competing, and even mutually exclusive. Parents of the 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s sometimes made choices that were good for them but very traumatic for their children. Divorces that might have been prevented left children estranged from one or both parents, in the middle of parental conflict and custody disputes, and with a series of quasi or would-be stepparents who moved into and out of their lives, wreaking havoc on the child’s sense of secure attachment. Parents indulged in chemical dependency and other addictive or compulsive behavior that made them emotionally unavailable or worse. Parents pursued careers or lifestyles that subjected children to prolonged parental absences and inconsistent care-taking. Sometimes, these choices were necessary, but sometimes they were merely self-indulgent, without regard for the real impact on children. “What’s good for me is good for my child” became a way of thinking that dismissed children’s separate needs and interests and prevented adequate support for the children. This attitude is no different from putting on blinders or looking the other way when one’s own child is suffering.

这种态度，根源在于父母未能看到自己孩子作为独立个体的存在。更难以面对的真相是：父母与孩子的需求往往大相径庭、彼此竞争，甚至互不相容。二十世纪七十、八十、九十年代的父母，有时做出的选择对他们自己有利，对孩子却造成深重的创伤。本可避免的离婚，使孩子与父母一方或双方疏离，夹在父母的冲突与抚养权争夺之间，还得面对一连串走马灯般进出他们生活的准继父母或候选继父母，把孩子的安全依恋感搅得天翻地覆。父母沉溺于药物依赖以及其他成瘾或强迫行为，使自己在情感上无法回应孩子，甚至更糟。父母追逐事业或某种生活方式，使孩子长期得不到父母的陪伴，受到的照料也时有时无。有时这些选择是迫不得已的，但有时它们不过是自我放纵，全然不顾对孩子的真实影响。"对我好的就对我的孩子好"成了一种思维方式，它无视孩子作为独立个体的需求与利益，使孩子得不到足够的支持。这种态度，与在自己孩子受苦时戴上眼罩、视而不见并无二致。

This contributes to prolonged narcissism in children by blurring boundaries and forcing children to rely on their own underdeveloped resources in times of stress. These are often the children who “raise themselves” and are left with a combination of unresolved omnipotence, profoundly conflicted dependency needs, anger, or difficulty trusting others.

这会模糊边界，迫使孩子在承受压力时只能依靠自己尚未发展成熟的内在资源，从而助长孩子身上自恋的延续。这些往往是"自己把自己养大"的孩子，最终留下的是未化解的全能感、深陷矛盾的依赖需求、愤怒，或难以信任他人等种种问题的混合体。

While it is undoubtedly true that children are better off with happy parents than with unhappy ones, what is best for children is when parents can find happiness in being parents.

尽管毫无疑问，孩子有快乐的父母比有不快乐的父母要好，但对孩子最有利的，是父母能够在为人父母这件事本身中找到快乐。

4. Children need freedom of expression. Their spirits are “pure,” and without adult interference, they will naturally blossom into superior beings. There’s no use trying to stop problem behavior by being authoritarian. That just alienates kids by shaming them needlessly.

4. 孩子需要表达的自由。他们的心灵是"纯净"的，只要没有成人的干预，他们自然会绽放成更优秀的人。靠摆出权威的姿态去制止问题行为是没用的，那只会无端羞辱孩子，把他们越推越远。

Nobody likes to be the bad guy, especially with his or her own child. But belief in the purity of “the natural child” is a narcissistic distortion of reality that conveniently lets the parent off the hook for socializing a child to become a functioning member of society. Children deprived of parental instruction do not usually evolve into “good” people; instead, they are more likely to have behavioral problems—and possibly unresolved infantile narcissism.

没有人喜欢当那个坏人，尤其是面对自己的孩子。但对"天然之子"之纯净的笃信，是一种自恋式的现实扭曲，它正好让父母得以推卸把孩子教化成能正常融入社会的一员这一责任。得不到父母教导的孩子，通常并不会成长为"好"人；相反，他们更可能出现行为问题——并可能留下未化解的幼稚自恋。


A Mind of His Own

有自己一套主意的孩子

“Someone ought to teach that kid to get along,” was the buzz around the teachers’ lounge when word got out that eight-year-old Johnny had started another ruckus on the playground. They all felt pretty sorry for Ruth, Johnny’s teacher, who kept having to explain to various parents why their kids were coming home with torn clothes, bruises, and the occasional bloody nose. Ruth had had problem kids before, but never one like Johnny—trouble just seemed to erupt all around him whenever the class had free time.

"该有人教教那孩子怎么跟人相处了。"当八岁的Johnny又在操场上闹出乱子的消息传开时，教师休息室里议论纷纷。大家都挺替Ruth难过——她是Johnny的老师，总得不停地向各位家长解释，为什么他们的孩子回到家衣服被扯破、身上带着淤青，偶尔还流着鼻血。Ruth以前也带过问题孩子，可从没遇见过Johnny这样的——每逢班里有自由活动的时间，麻烦就像在他周围炸开一样。

Ruth had tried talking to Johnny, but she couldn’t get through. He always had an explanation about why he was right and someone else was wrong. He seemed to expect other kids to do things his way, and when they resisted he would become haughty and provocative. He was utterly lacking in the routine skills of give-and-take.

Ruth试着跟Johnny谈过，却谈不进去。他总有一套说辞，解释为什么自己是对的、别人是错的。他似乎指望其他孩子都照他的方式来，一旦别人不从，他就变得傲慢而咄咄逼人。他完全缺乏那种你来我往、互谅互让的寻常本领。

So Ruth called a conference with Johnny’s parents. Frankly, she was a little surprised that she hadn’t heard from themJohnny seldom came out of the fray unscathed and had taken home a few nicks and bumps of his own. But when she finally sat down with them face to face, they seemed totally unfazed by the tales of their son’s behavior.

于是Ruth约了Johnny的父母面谈。说实话，她还有点惊讶之前竟没收到他们的任何反馈——Johnny很少能毫发无损地从混战中全身而退，自己也带着几处擦伤和磕碰回家。可当她终于和他们面对面坐下来时，他们对儿子种种行为的描述却显得毫不在意。

“We’ve always taught Johnny to fight his own battles,” said Johnny’s mother, Kris. Indeed, thought Ruth, beginning to see the larger problem. “Johnny’s always had a mind of his own,” his father, Greg, chimed in. The pride in his voice was not lost on Ruth. “Yes,” she answered carefully, “Johnny is certainly very sure of himself, and he has no problems expressing what he wants.” Kris and Greg beamed at each other. “How-ever,” Ruth continued, “Johnny does seem to have some difficulty hearing what others want, and also, I’m afraid, respecting how they feel. He becomes quite aggressive when he doesn’t get his way.” She braced herself, expecting to see an adult version of Johnny’s now-familiar temper. “They’re just kids,” said Kris, shrugging, “and kids fight. If you leave them alone, they’ll work it out. That’s how we handle it at home, and it works for us!”

"我们一向教Johnny自己的仗自己打，"Johnny的母亲Kris说。可不是嘛，Ruth心想，开始看清了更深层的问题。"Johnny从小就有自己的一套主意，"他父亲Greg接口道。他语气里的自豪Ruth听得一清二楚。"是的，"她谨慎地回答，"Johnny当然很有自信，要表达自己想要什么也毫无障碍。"Kris和Greg相视而笑。"不过，"Ruth接着说，"Johnny似乎确实不太能听进别人想要什么，而且恐怕也不太尊重别人的感受。一旦不能如愿，他就会变得相当有攻击性。"她做好了心理准备，预料会见识到Johnny那套如今人人熟知的脾气的成人版。"他们不过是孩子嘛，"Kris耸耸肩说，"孩子打打闹闹很正常。你别管他们，他们自己会解决的。我们在家就是这么处理的，对我们挺管用！"

Children need parental input to get through early childhood narcissism. They need help in having their infantile omnipotence and grandiosity deflated, and in regulating the shame that comes with having the bubble of narcissism popped. They need strong, nurturing parental figures who can serve as models for idealization. They need corrective feedback about what parents believe to be right and true and important. They need parents who have self-control and who practice what they preach.

孩子需要父母的投入，才能度过幼年早期的自恋阶段。他们需要帮助，好让自己婴儿般的全能感和夸大被一点点放掉气，并调节自恋的泡泡被戳破时随之而来的羞耻。他们需要强大、给予滋养的父母形象，足以充当理想化的榜样。他们需要关于父母心中何为对、何为真、何为重要的纠偏性反馈。他们需要懂得自我控制、且言行一致的父母。

It is by internalizing the values of an idealized parent that children develop conscience. If we wish our children to know right from wrong, we not only have to teach them, but also have to be people they can admire.

正是通过内化一个被理想化的父母的价值观，孩子才发展出良知。如果我们希望孩子明辨是非，那么我们不仅要教导他们，自己也必须成为他们能够敬仰的人。

5. Empathy for a child is the same as treating him or her as a friend, sharing all my feelings and “modeling” closeness. Being honest means telling a child whatever I’m thinking or feeling and disclosing any dirty laundry from my past or any problems I’m having now. Children need to know the truth about their parents at all times.

5. 对孩子共情，就等同于把他当朋友看待，把我所有的感受都与他分享，并"示范"何为亲密。所谓诚实，就是把我所思所感统统告诉孩子，把我过去的任何丑事、当下遇到的任何麻烦都和盘托出。孩子无时无刻都需要了解关于父母的真相。


The Little Grown-Up

小大人

Tina’s mother likes to tell people her daughter was “born strong.” To Mom, Tina is someone she can count on to help around the house and to look after the younger children when she feels like retiring to her bedroom with the curtains drawn. Tina seems wise beyond her years, and when Mom feels depressed, as she often does, the ten-year-old is such a comfort. She even understands why Mom is unhappy with Dad.

Tina的母亲喜欢逢人便说她女儿"天生坚强"。在妈妈眼里，Tina是个靠得住的人——能帮着操持家务，还能在她想拉上窗帘躲进卧室时照看弟弟妹妹。Tina看上去远比同龄人懂事；每当妈妈情绪低落时（这是常有的事），这个十岁的孩子总能给她莫大的慰藉。她甚至能理解妈妈为什么对爸爸不满。

Tina feels sad for her mother, but she adores her father and has a special place in his life, too. The two of them are a team, taking care of business, taking care of Mom. Tina and her father shop together, cook together, and Sundays are their day to play cards together, just the two of them. Like Mom, Dad turns to Tina as a confidante. She knows his sorrows and his aggravations, and secretly, she is on his side.

Tina为母亲感到难过，但她崇拜父亲，在父亲的生活里也占有一个特殊的位置。父女俩是一个团队，一起打理各样事务，一起照顾妈妈。Tina和父亲一起购物、一起做饭，星期天则是他俩单独在一起打牌的日子。和妈妈一样，爸爸也把Tina当作倾诉的知己。她了解他的忧愁与烦恼，而暗地里，她是站在他这一边的。

Tina was known even in kindergarten as “the little girl who uses big words.” Since then, she has been a top student and usually the teacher’s pet. So when she began acting defiant in class and talking back at home, everyone was surprised. Her parents figure she must be starting puberty a little ahead of schedule.

早在上幼儿园时，Tina就以"那个会用大词的小女孩"而闻名。自那以后，她一直是尖子生，通常还是老师的宠儿。所以，当她开始在课堂上表现出叛逆、在家里顶嘴时，大家都很吃惊。她的父母琢磨着，她大概是青春期来得比预期早了一点吧。

Empathy for a child is based on an understanding of that child’s real needs and capacities at each stage of development, as well as an intuitive feel for the child’s unique rhythms and idiosyncracies and a realistic assessment of what the child can handle at any given time. It is also based on caring enough to maintain good parental boundaries.

对孩子的共情，建立在以下几点之上：理解孩子在每个发展阶段的真实需求与能力，对孩子独特的节奏和个性怀有一种直觉式的体察，以及对孩子在任一特定时刻所能承受之事作出切合实际的评估。它还建立在足够的关爱之上——足够到愿意维持良好的父母边界。

Good parental boundaries mean not treating a child as a friend, confidant, or confessor. While such treatment may indeed make a child feel “special,” it is the wrong kind of specialness. It communicates to a child that he or she is an equal, and that the parent and child roles have been obscured. Parents who indulge in this kind of behavior with their children are often surprised when their children have little or no respect for authority, either the parent’s or that of any other adult. This is because they have been allowed to share in what they perceive as the parent’s grandiosity and omnipotence at the expense of developing a real Self. Although it may be difficult for a warm and caring adult to see how this is inappropriate, children often experience this kind of chumminess as intrusive as well as inappropriately gratifying. Once again, it is more about what the parent needs than what is good for the child, and it is by no means harmless .

良好的父母边界意味着不把孩子当作朋友、知己或忏悔的对象。这样对待孩子，固然可能让孩子觉得自己很"特别"，却是一种错误的特别。它向孩子传递的信息是：他或她与父母平起平坐，父母与孩子的角色界限被模糊了。沉溺于以这种方式对待孩子的父母，常常会惊讶地发现，自己的孩子对权威——无论是父母的还是任何其他成年人的——几乎毫无敬意。这是因为他们被允许分享了他们眼中父母的夸大与全能，代价则是真实自我的发展受阻。尽管一个热心而慈爱的大人或许很难看出这有何不妥，但孩子往往会把这种称兄道弟般的亲密体验为一种侵入，同时也是一种不当的满足。再说一遍，这更多关乎父母的需要，而非什么对孩子有益的东西，而且它绝非无害。

6. Sex is natural, and children should not be made to feel shameful about their sexuality by adults behaving in “repressed” ways.

6. 性是自然的，不应由行为"压抑"的成年人让孩子为自己的性而感到羞耻。


The Family That Plays Together …

一起玩乐的一家人……

It is a holiday weekend at the cineplex, and a matinee showing of a highly publicized PG-13 movie has drawn a crowd of about sixty, approximately a quarter of whom are children under the age of ten accompanied by their parents. A family of four has settled into a back row, passing the popcorn and soft drinks, enjoying some quality time together. In the opening sequence of the movie, a man and a woman in bed are engaged in a little après-sex talk when the woman disengages herself, starts to behave oddly, and then suddenly turns into a malevolent “she-bot.” As semiautomatic bullets spray from her nipples and her face falls off to reveal her cyborg circuitry, the little girl in the back row shrieks, “Mommy, it’s too scary!” “No, it’s not,” her mother replies, and the child falls silent. Pass the popcorn.

这是一个假日周末的多厅影院，一场宣传声势浩大的PG-13级电影的午后场吸引了约六十人，其中约莫四分之一是由父母陪同的十岁以下儿童。一家四口在后排坐定，传递着爆米花和软饮料，共享着一段亲子时光。电影的开场镜头里，一男一女躺在床上正进行着一点事后的枕边絮语，忽然女子抽身离开，举止变得古怪，随即猛地变成了一个心怀恶意的"女机器人"。当半自动子弹从她的乳头喷射而出、面孔脱落露出体内电子机械的线路时，后排那个小女孩尖叫起来："妈妈，太吓人了！""不吓人，"她母亲答道，孩子便不作声了。再递一把爆米花来。

The movie, as it turns out, is one long sexual intercourse joke, with pantomiming of perverse sexual acts, crude references to body parts and bodily functions, and an assortment of characters demonstrating various coital positions as they hop into and out of bed with one another, all in ribald humor. The children in the theater grow restless and noisy, but no one—not one family—leaves.

事实证明，这部电影从头到尾就是一个冗长的性交笑话，充斥着对变态性行为的肢体模仿、对身体部位和生理功能的粗鄙暗示，以及形形色色的角色钻进钻出彼此的床铺、演示着各式各样的交媾体位，无不裹挟着下流的笑料。影院里的孩子开始坐立不安、吵吵嚷嚷，可没有一个人——没有哪怕一家人——离场。

Parents since the 1970s have put a lot of stock in modeling behavior that they want to instill in their children. This is perhaps nowhere more misguided than in the overexposure of children to sexual stimulation that they are developmentally unprepared to handle.

自二十世纪七十年代以来，父母们十分看重以身作则、示范他们想要灌输给孩子的行为。而这一点误入歧途之处，或许莫过于让孩子过度暴露于他们在发展上尚未准备好去应对的性刺激之中。

Sometimes children are overstimulated as a result of the deluge of sexual material in our culture. It is a symptom of our narcissistic times that adult cravings for sexual stimulation are so blatantly allowed to undermine a wholesome environment for children. Concerned parents must guard against the tendency to feel overwhelmed and confused by, or indifferent to, these influences. Even if you cannot fully protect your child from sexual overstimulation (and you can’t), your willingness to try creates a kind of boundary that children can internalize as a self-protective barrier. Children need all the protection we can offer from that which they are developmentally unready to handle.

有时，孩子之所以受到过度刺激，是我们文化中泛滥成灾的性内容所致。成年人对性刺激的渴求竟被如此明目张胆地允许去侵蚀本应有益于孩子的健康环境，这正是我们这个自恋时代的一种症候。有心的父母必须警惕，别让自己被这些影响弄得不知所措、一头雾水，或干脆漠然置之。即便你无法彻底保护孩子免受性的过度刺激（你也确实做不到），你愿意去尝试这一姿态本身，就构筑起了一道边界，孩子可以将其内化为一道自我保护的屏障。对于那些孩子在发展上尚未准备好去应对的东西，他们需要我们所能提供的一切保护。

Certainly, parents should model adult love and affection and should answer questions about sex and reproduction in a straightforward manner that is appropriate to the child’s ability to understand. And certainly, children’s spontaneous sexuality should be treated as natural. But children do sometimes have sexual feelings and are subject to being stimulated by parental nudity, overt sexual behavior or even innuendo, and intrusive information of a sexual nature. What is natural for healthy, loving adults may be too much for children, and this is not so much a problem to be overcome in the interests of eradicating “repression” as a reality to be respected for the sake of preserving a child’s sexual and emotional health.

诚然，父母应当示范成人之间的爱与亲昵，也应当以直截了当、与孩子理解能力相称的方式来回答关于性与生殖的问题。诚然，孩子自发的性表现应被当作自然之事来对待。但孩子有时确实会产生性感受，也容易因父母的裸露、露骨的性行为乃至性暗示，以及侵入性的性信息而受到刺激。对健康、相爱的成年人而言自然的事，对孩子来说可能就太过了；与其说这是一个为了根除"压抑"而需要克服的问题，不如说这是一个为了维护孩子性与情感健康而需要尊重的现实。

The sexual overstimulation of children by what they see or hear in the home is a form of emotional incest, and it can be just as damaging as physical incest and even more difficult to recall and work through as an adult because there was not so much a traumatic event as a climate of unclear boundaries that gratified the parent in some way at the expense of the child. These children often become sexually precocious or may masturbate in public as a way of expressing the blurred boundaries they feel.

孩子因在家中所见所闻而受到性的过度刺激，是情感乱伦的一种形式，它造成的伤害可能不亚于身体上的乱伦，成年后甚至更难回忆和梳理化解，因为这里并不存在某个明确的创伤事件，而是一种边界不清的氛围，它以牺牲孩子为代价，在某种程度上满足了父母。这些孩子往往会在性方面早熟，或可能在公共场合自慰，以此来表达他们所感受到的那种模糊不清的边界。

7. The way to build self-esteem is to tell children how “special” they are and to have them recite “affirmations” on a regular basis. Children shouldn’t have to accomplish anything in order to believe in themselves, and they should be spared the harmful effects of competition.

7. 建立自尊的方法，就是告诉孩子他们有多"特别"，并让他们定期背诵"自我肯定语"。孩子无须取得任何成就就该相信自己，而且应当让他们免受竞争的有害影响。

Where does confidence come from? Does it come from telling yourself that you are able to do something, or from taking the risk to try and finding out what you really can do?

自信从何而来？它是来自对自己说"我能做到某件事"，还是来自冒险一试、从而弄清自己究竟能做什么？

Self-esteem works the same way. When children are encouraged to make an effort, they discover their own competence and have an experience of mastery that becomes part of how they see themselves. If they stumble or fail along the way, that too is a learning experience that has the potential to help them become stronger and more realistic about themselves. Not everyone can be good at everything, but finding out what you are good at is part of forming a positive identity. Parents can help by creating an environment in which opportunities are offered, effort is valued, and children are given skills for handling disappointment.

自尊的运作方式也是如此。当孩子受到鼓励去付出努力时，他们便会发现自己的能力，获得一种掌控的体验，而这种体验会成为他们看待自己的一部分。倘若他们一路上跌跌撞撞甚至失败了，那同样是一种学习经历，有潜力帮助他们变得更坚强、对自己的认识更切合实际。不是每个人都能样样精通，但弄清自己究竟擅长什么，正是形成积极身份认同的一部分。父母可以通过营造这样一种环境来提供帮助：在其中机会得以提供、努力受到看重，孩子也被教会应对失望的本领。

It is important to reflect back to a child an accurate perception of that child’s true Self. Children are not fooled when we tell them they are special and they have done nothing to merit that accolade in their own or anyone else’s eyes. But children do buy into our expectation that they be special by clinging to their infantile grandiosity and omnipotence. Hollow affirmations only reinforce narcissism in children at the expense of their developing a real Self.

向孩子如实反射出对其真实自我的准确感知，是很重要的。当我们告诉孩子他们很特别，而他们在自己或任何人眼中都未曾做过任何配得上这份赞誉的事时，孩子是骗不过去的。但孩子确实会接受我们要他们"成为"特别之人的期望，方式便是紧抓住自己婴儿般的夸大与全能不放。空洞的自我肯定只会强化孩子身上的自恋，代价则是他们真实自我的发展。

In addition to love, all children need these things, from birth to emancipation: consistency, structure, good boundaries, empathic attunement, and someone to be an adult. They need to know who is in their family and who is not, what place or places they can call home, where and when they are supposed to eat and sleep, what are the rules of conduct of the household and to whom they apply (roles and responsibilities), what belongs to them and what does not. They need to be taught what their own personal boundaries are, who can violate them, and under what circumstances. They need to understand also that others have personal boundaries that need to be respected. They need to know whom they can depend on to meet each of their needs: who will comfort them when they are hurt or sick or frightened, who will protect them when they are in danger, who will provide them with the necessities of life, who will teach them what they need to know to become more self-reliant. The sum total of these “knowings” constitute the boundaries of their lives.

除了爱之外，所有孩子从出生到独立成人，都需要这些东西：一致性、结构、良好的边界、共情的契合，以及有一个肯做成年人的人。他们需要知道谁在自己的家庭之内、谁在其外，哪个或哪些地方可以称之为家，自己应当在何处、何时吃饭和睡觉，家中的行为规则是什么、适用于谁（角色与责任），什么东西属于自己、什么不属于。他们需要被教导自己的个人边界何在、谁可以越过它、在什么情形下可以越过。他们还需要懂得，他人也有需要被尊重的个人边界。他们需要知道，自己可以依靠谁来满足各自的需求：受伤、生病或害怕时谁来安慰他们，身处险境时谁来保护他们，谁来供给他们生活的必需品，谁来教会他们那些需要掌握、好让自己更加自立的东西。这些"知道"的总和，构成了他们生活的边界。

As parents, it is our job to set these structures in place and to maintain or alter them over time. It goes without saying that we will have a great deal of difficulty doing this for our children if we also have trouble doing it for ourselves. If your life is in chaos, you will need to figure out why and go about setting it straight. To the extent that you are able to be in control, you will need to set boundaries around the things that affect you and the ways you interact with other people. You will need to be a parent while you do this, and let your children be children. That is what is meant by good generational boundaries.

作为父母，把这些结构搭建起来、并随着时间加以维持或调整，正是我们的职责。不言而喻，如果我们连为自己做这件事都有困难，那么为孩子做起来也会困难重重。倘若你的生活一片混乱，你就需要弄清缘由，着手把它理顺。在你力所能及、能够掌控的范围内，你需要围绕那些影响你的事情、以及你与他人相处的方式来设立边界。做这一切的同时，你需要扮演好父母的角色，让你的孩子做孩子。这就是良好的代际边界所指的含义。

If you are a parent, you can help prevent narcissism—one child at a time.

如果你为人父母，你就能帮助预防自恋——一次一个孩子，从身边做起。
