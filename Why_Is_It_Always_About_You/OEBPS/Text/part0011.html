Arrogance

3 傲慢

THE PERSONA THAT MANY NARCISSISTS present to the world often comes across to others as a “superiority complex.” But behind the mask of arrogance is a fragile internal balloon of self-esteem that is never satisfied with being good or even very good—if they are not better than, then they are worthless. Value is always relative, never absolute. From their point of view, if someone else’s stock goes up, theirs automatically goes down. Conversely, if they are feeling deflated, they can reinflate themselves by diminishing, debasing, or degrading someone else. This is the reason why Narcissists are often bossy, judgmental, perfectionistic, and power-hungry. They are simply trying to secure the kind of status that will afford them the most distance from the taint of personal defect and shame. If their balloon gets torn by the ill winds of life, they can repair themselves by showing someone else to be inferior. At times, this can be very subtle.

许多自恋者向世人展示的形象，在他人看来往往像是一种“优越情结”。但在傲慢的面具背后，是一只脆弱的内在自尊气球，它永远不满足于优秀，甚至不满足于非常优秀——如果他们没有“胜过别人”，那他们就一文不值。价值永远是相对的，从不是绝对的。在他们看来，如果别人的身价上涨，自己的就自动下跌。反过来，如果他们感到泄了气，便可以通过贬损、矮化或羞辱他人来给自己重新打气。这正是自恋者往往专横、爱评判、追求完美、渴求权力的原因。他们不过是想确保获得一种地位，使自己尽可能远离个人缺陷与羞耻的污名。如果他们的气球被生活的逆风撕破，他们可以通过证明别人不如自己来修补自己。有时，这一切可能非常微妙。

Francine came to see me when she had been having panic attacks for nearly a month. Consciously, her anxiety centered on her husband’s recent slide into depression and the specific fear that he might lose his job. Although she spoke of the intense sexual connection that had sustained their troubled marriage for a decade, it was clear that his current emotional crisis had caused her to devalue him. In fact, she said, she was giving serious consideration to divorce. Distancing from him and the failure he represented seemed like the only way to restore her own equilibrium.

Francine来找我时，恐慌发作已经持续了将近一个月。在她意识层面，焦虑的核心是丈夫近来陷入了抑郁，以及她对他可能丢掉工作的具体恐惧。虽然她谈到那段维系了他们这段动荡婚姻十年之久的强烈性吸引，但显而易见，丈夫眼下的情绪危机已经让她贬低了他。事实上，她说，她正在认真考虑离婚。与他、以及与他所代表的失败拉开距离，似乎是她恢复自身平衡的唯一途径。

Francine needed me to know that she was well-educated, artistically gifted, and had risen above an assortment of personal misfortunes that had shaped her life. There had been a previous failed marriage, a drug habit, a bankruptcy, and, most notably, a depression of her own that had resulted from a work situation in which she had felt disrespected and out of control. She related these stories with a kind of aloofness, communicating a sense that she was not really responsible for any of her problems, an emotional detachment that is characteristic of the way narcissistic individuals bypass shame. There was neither pain nor embarrassment in the telling, but rather a sense that it was remarkable that so many bad things could have happened to someone such as her. In her eyes, she was a noble victim who had triumphed over adversity.

Francine需要让我知道，她受过良好教育，颇具艺术天赋，并且超越了一连串塑造了她人生的个人不幸。此前有过一段失败的婚姻、一段吸毒经历、一次破产，最值得一提的，是她自己也曾因一份让她觉得不受尊重、失去掌控的工作而陷入抑郁。她以一种冷漠超然的口吻讲述这些经历，传达出一种自己其实对任何问题都不必负责的感觉——这种情感上的抽离，正是自恋个体绕开羞耻的典型方式。讲述中既没有痛苦，也没有难堪，反倒透着一种意味：这么多坏事竟会发生在她这样的人身上，简直令人称奇。在她眼中，她是一个战胜了逆境的高贵受害者。

Although her health insurance limited our work to a type of short-term treatment called “solution-focused” therapy, Francine resisted my efforts to provide structure and help her set behavioral goals that could be accomplished in six to twelve sessions. When I attempted to interpret her predicament, she was quick to let me know if I was at all imprecise. I soon learned that my job was simply to reflect back the wonder of her unique strength, and with as few words as possible. To have allowed me to be any more helpful than that would have meant that I was in the superior position in our relationship, which was not at all acceptable. At times, I felt rather superfluous, as if I had little of value to contribute to her recovery. But that was exactly how Francine needed me to feel, and the subtle diminishment of me was to be part of her healing.

尽管她的医疗保险把我们的工作限定在一种叫作“聚焦解决”的短程治疗上，Francine却抵触我为她提供结构、帮她设定那些可在六到十二次会谈中完成的行为目标的努力。每当我试图解读她的困境时，只要我有一丝不精确，她就会立刻让我知道。我很快明白，我的任务不过是把她那独一无二的力量之奇妙映照回去，而且要用尽可能少的话语。若是允许我提供比这更多的帮助，就意味着在我们的关系中我占据了优越的位置，而这是绝对不可接受的。有时，我觉得自己相当多余，仿佛对她的康复贡献不了多少价值。但这恰恰正是Francine需要我去感受的，而对我这种微妙的矮化，本身就将成为她疗愈的一部分。

As I bore witness, neither challenging nor intruding but accepting her need to feel superior at my expense, Francine shifted her attention from her husband’s shortcomings to restoring her injured self-image. She became preoccupied with the intricacies of her own body, which was nearly as sensitive to food and medication as that of the princess in the fairy tale who could feel a pea through a mountain of mattresses. She began to nurture herself and grow more confident, confident enough, in fact, to compete for and win a plum job that would take her career in a new direction. My willingness to tolerate her subtle putdowns may have spared her husband from being similarly used and bought him some time for his own recovery. In any event, the marriage survived that particular crisis, and Francine’s panic attacks also subsided. But it was being selected out of a pool of competitors for the job of her dreams that really put Francine back on top.

在我作为见证者，既不质疑也不打扰，而是接纳她以贬低我来获得优越感的需要时，Francine把注意力从丈夫的缺点转向了修复自己受损的自我形象。她开始全神贯注于自己身体的种种微妙之处——她对食物和药物的敏感程度，几乎不亚于童话里那位隔着一座床垫山都能感觉到一颗豌豆的公主。她开始滋养自己、变得越来越自信，事实上自信到足以去竞争并赢得一份令她事业转向新方向的美差。我甘愿容忍她那些微妙的贬损，或许使她丈夫免于被同样地利用，也为他自己的康复争取了一些时间。无论如何，这段婚姻挺过了那场特定的危机，Francine的恐慌发作也随之平息。但真正让Francine重回巅峰的，是从一群竞争者中脱颖而出、得到了她梦寐以求的那份工作。

For Narcissists, competition of all kinds is a way to reaffirm superiority, although many will only compete when they anticipate a favorable outcome. Deeply shamed by defeat, they tend to choose arenas in which they can shine without much risk or effort, and when success happens, they may become compulsive in their pursuit of perfection. Along the way, they crave admiration from others. Admiration-seeking among such Narcissists usually means they are feeling a little unsure of their own superiority and in need of some refueling.

对自恋者而言，各种各样的竞争都是重新确认自身优越的一种方式，尽管许多人只有在预见到有利结果时才会参与竞争。由于失败会让他们深感羞耻，他们往往选择那些无需太多风险或努力便能大放异彩的舞台；而一旦获得成功，他们对完美的追求可能会变得带有强迫性。在这一过程中，他们渴求他人的钦佩。在这类自恋者身上，寻求钦佩通常意味着他们对自身的优越有些没把握，需要补充些“燃料”。

Those who are less successful at developing a talent or skill may rely on petty games of oneupmanship to sustain a sense of superiority. These are among the name-droppers, status seekers, and know-it-alls of the world who may not be able to depend on their accomplishments to prove that they are better than you. “Sore losers” are often Narcissists who cannot bear to be exposed as less than the best. For them, there is nothing acceptable about being ordinary or average—if they can’t be superior, they are nothing.

那些在培养某项才能或技艺上不太成功的人，可能会依靠一些上不了台面的争胜小把戏来维系优越感。他们属于这世上那些爱攀附名人、追逐地位、自以为无所不知的人——他们或许无法凭借自己的成就来证明自己比你强。“输不起的人”往往是无法忍受被揭穿自己并非最优秀的自恋者。对他们来说，平凡或普通是绝对无法接受的——如果不能高人一等，他们便一无是处。

Narcissists are seldom able to sustain a sense of superiority on their own and so are drawn to the limelight and the praise, applause, and recognition from others that keep them inflated. They can be quite sensitive to others’ opinions and often have unrealistic expectations of unconditional approval and admiration, even when their actual performance would not merit such a response.

自恋者很少能够独自维系优越感，因此他们被聚光灯，以及来自他人的赞美、掌声与认可所吸引，正是这些让他们保持膨胀。他们对别人的看法可能相当敏感，常常抱有不切实际的期待，渴望得到无条件的认可与钦佩，哪怕他们的实际表现根本配不上这样的回应。

If having an appreciative audience is good, having control over them is even better. Power for the Narcissist is not only an affirmation of superiority but also a means of controlling others to ensure “narcissistic supplies”—anything that feeds a fragile ego. The more power they have, the more freely they can diminish others to keep themselves inflated. This misuse of power in the service of someone’s narcissism has become an all-too-familiar feature of many modern work environments, causing morale and performance problems on the job and severe dysfunction within families. At the root of the harm inflicted on others is the need to get rid of the exaggerated shame of real or imagined inferiority.

如果说拥有懂得欣赏的观众是好事，那么掌控他们就更好了。对自恋者来说，权力不仅是优越的确证，也是控制他人、确保“自恋供给”的手段——而所谓自恋供给，就是任何能喂养脆弱自我的东西。他们握有的权力越大，就越能肆意地贬低他人，好让自己保持膨胀。这种为某人自恋服务的权力滥用，已经成为许多现代职场中再熟悉不过的特征，造成工作中的士气与绩效问题，以及家庭内部的严重功能失调。施加于他人的伤害，其根源在于一种需要——要摆脱因真实或想象中的低人一等而被夸大了的羞耻。

An arrogant and superior attitude thus serves as a protective barrier that keeps the “stink” of imperfection off the Narcissist, providing insulation from intolerable feelings of shame about personal shortcomings. This person carries within the burning eyes of a critical, shaming parent that must be avoided at any cost. So when you encounter arrogance, it’s not really pride that you’re seeing, it’s a deep and irrational fear of being worthless. The only way to still that fear is to feel important—more important than anyone else, as it turns out.

因此，傲慢而优越的姿态充当了一道保护屏障，把不完美的“臭气”挡在自恋者身外，使其隔绝于那种因个人缺陷而生、无法忍受的羞耻感。这样的人内心承载着一双批判、羞辱的父母之眼，那灼人的目光必须不惜一切代价地回避。所以，当你遇到傲慢时，你看到的其实并不是骄傲，而是一种对自身一文不值的深切而非理性的恐惧。平息这种恐惧的唯一办法，就是感到自己重要——而结果证明，是比任何人都更重要。
