CHAPTER 2
Understanding Parts, Understanding Traumatic Responses

第2章
理解各部分，理解创伤性反应

"When the images and sensations of experience remain in 'implicit-only' form …, they remain in unassembled neural disarray, not tagged as representations derived from the past … Such implicit-only memories continue to shape the subjective feeling we have of our here-and-now realities, the sense of who we are moment to moment, but this influence is not accessible to our awareness."

「当体验的意象与感觉仅以'内隐'形式存在……它们仍处于尚未整合的神经混乱之中，未被标注为源自过去的表征……这类纯内隐记忆持续塑造着我们对当下现实的主观感受，以及我们时时刻刻对自我身份的感知，但这种影响并不在我们的意识可及范围之内。」

(Siegel, 2010, p. 154)

Trauma often leaves its legacy in ways that don't fit traditional diagnostic or treatment molds. Rather than finding relief in disclosing their secrets, clients feel ashamed, mistrustful, or exposed. Instead of feeling better, they experience difficulty remembering or generalizing their new learning outside of therapy and return like Winnie the Pooh to the very same place over and over. Or there is no one "she" or "he" with whom the therapist can work. The agitated, angry client of last week is often replaced by someone who is depressed, shut down, unable to say more than a few dark words. Then, the next week, plans for the future are the subject of discussion rather than despair and suicide. When we mention last week's disclosure of sexual abuse, the client is surprised; the disclosure has been forgotten as if it hadn't occurred. Whereas, last week, therapy was the client's only safe place, today it feels unsafe and threatening. Worse yet, the determination to change has given way to the fear of change. Not only is the therapist confused by these shifting states of mind, so too is the client.

创伤留下的印记，往往不符合传统的诊断或治疗框架。来访者非但没有从倾诉秘密中得到宽慰，反而感到羞耻、不信任或暴露无遗。他们不仅没有好转，还难以记住或将在治疗中获得的新认知迁移到治疗室以外，像小熊维尼一样一次又一次回到同一个地方。抑或治疗师根本找不到一个稳定的"她"或"他"可以合作。上周那个焦躁易怒的来访者，这周往往换成了一个抑郁、关闭、只能吐出几句灰暗言词的人。而到了下一周，话题又变成了对未来的规划，而非绝望与自杀。当治疗师提及上周吐露的性虐待经历时，来访者却大感意外；那次披露仿佛从未发生过，已经被遗忘了。上周，治疗室是来访者唯一的安全之地；今天，它却令人感到危险和恐惧。更糟的是，改变的决心已让位于对改变本身的恐惧。不仅治疗师被这些变幻莫测的心理状态搞得困惑，来访者自己也是如此。

In the context of life threat, survival is a necessity. Being able to consciously witness the experience, preserve a sense of time, place, and identity, and clearly encode a memory of what happened frame-by-frame is an unnecessary luxury when human beings are in immediate danger. Faced with potential threat, the brain and body instinctively mobilize the emergency stress response, preparing the individual to take action: fleeing, fighting, ducking, and hiding. As danger cues are perceived by the sensory system, a chain reaction of neurochemical events is set in motion. The amygdala (a structure that serves as the brain's smoke detector and fire alarm) begins to "fire" more rapidly, activating another limbic system structure, the hypothalamus, to initiate an adrenaline release to "turn on" the sympathetic nervous system. As the adrenaline release speeds up heart rate and respiration to increase oxygen flow to muscle tissue, the body prepares to engage fight-flight impulses. The individual feels braced and strong; the events unfold in slow motion; an icy calm replaces fear; the eyes narrow; and the body prepares for action, clenching fists, engaging the leg muscles, biceps, and shoulders. As fight and flight responses unfold, release of another neurochemical, cortisol, begins to activate reciprocal activity in the parasympathetic nervous system. The parasympathetic system is best known for its role in recuperation, rest, and states of calm and is often referred to as an "energy conservation system" in contrast to the "energy expending" sympathetic system (Ogden et al., 2006). As the body mobilizes for fight and flight, the parasympathetic system prepares the body to freeze (like a deer in the headlights) to avoid exposure or to submit or "feign death" (Porges, 2011) if the individual is trapped with no way out, no way to defend. The parasympathetic system also helps the body recover from the massive expenditure of energy involved in fighting and fleeing, facilitating feelings of depletion, exhaustion, "just need to sleep," or numbing.

在生命受到威胁的情境下，生存是必然的选择。当人类处于即时危险之中，能够有意识地见证体验、保持对时间、地点和身份的感知，并逐帧清晰地编码所发生的记忆，不过是一种无谓的奢侈。面对潜在威胁，大脑和身体本能地启动紧急应激反应，为个体做好采取行动的准备：逃跑、战斗、躲闪与藏匿。当感官系统感知到危险信号时，一系列神经化学反应链便被触发。杏仁核（大脑的烟雾探测器兼火灾警报器）开始更加迅速地"放电"，激活另一个边缘系统结构——下丘脑，启动肾上腺素释放，从而"开启"交感神经系统。肾上腺素释放加速心率与呼吸，以增加肌肉组织的氧气供应，使身体准备好投入战斗或逃跑冲动。个体感到紧绷而有力；事件以慢动作展开；一种冰冷的平静取代了恐惧；眼睛微眯；身体准备行动，握紧拳头，收紧腿部肌肉、二头肌和肩膀。随着战斗和逃跑反应的展开，另一种神经化学物质——皮质醇——的释放开始在副交感神经系统中激活对应活动。副交感神经系统以其在恢复、休息和平静状态中的作用而闻名，通常被称为"能量储存系统"，以区别于"能量消耗"的交感神经系统（Ogden等，2006）。当身体为战斗和逃跑做好动员时，副交感神经系统会让身体进入冻结状态（如路灯前的鹿），以避免暴露；或者，当个体陷入无路可逃、无法防御的困境时，副交感神经系统会让其顺从或"装死"（Porges，2011）。副交感神经系统还帮助身体从战斗和逃跑所消耗的大量能量中恢复，带来精疲力竭、"只想睡觉"或麻木的感觉。

In traumatogenic environments where the threat of danger is ever-present, it is more adaptive for both children and adults when their bodies are conditioned to maintain a readiness for potential danger. These automatic patterns of response may be sympathetically activated (biased toward hypervigilance, high arousal, readiness to take action, impulsivity) or parasympathetically dominant (without energy, exhausted, slowed, numb, disconnected, hopeless and helpless). For children or victims of domestic violence who endure day-in-day-out conditions of threat or for whom being seen and not heard is the safest adaptation, it is common to see parasympathetic patterns of passivity, slowed thinking, and depression or shame dominating the individual's experience. In sympathetically dominant clients, it is more typical to see hyperactivity, reactivity, feelings of anger or fear, a readiness to act first and think later, and mistrust or hypervigilance.

在危险威胁无处不在的创伤性环境中，儿童和成人的身体被条件化地保持对潜在危险的警觉状态，这具有更强的适应性。这些自动反应模式可能是交感神经激活的（偏向于高度警惕、高度唤醒、随时准备行动、冲动性），也可能是副交感神经主导的（无精打采、精疲力竭、行动迟缓、麻木、断离感、绝望和无助）。对于那些日复一日承受威胁状态的儿童或家庭暴力受害者，或那些以"看得见但不能发声"作为最安全适应策略的人而言，常见的是副交感神经模式的被动性、思维迟缓以及抑郁或羞耻主导个体体验。而在交感神经主导的来访者中，更典型的表现是过度活跃、反应激烈、愤怒或恐惧感、先行动后思考的冲动，以及不信任或高度警惕。

Because survival depends upon sympathetic highs and parasympathetic lows to drive animal defense responses, these clients' nervous systems have been conditioned to dysregulate under stress. The parts connected to sympathetic arousal (fight and flight parts, the attach part, and the freeze part) and those connected to parasympathetic arousal (submit, the going on with normal life self) are primed for activation as the nervous system responds to triggers. Under traumatic conditions, individuals fail to develop or lose the capacity for a "window of tolerance" (Ogden et al., 2006; Siegel, 1999). The "window of tolerance" refers to the individual's bandwidth or capacity to tolerate intense emotions at the sympathetic end and bored, numb, or "low" feelings at the parasympathetic end. Because most threatening conditions for traumatized children are recurrent or "enduring" (Saakvitne, 2000), there is generally very little opportunity to develop a window of tolerance. To adapt, their bodies have had to be on high alert, ready for action, or to be disconnected, numb, passive, and able to endure whatever comes. When they are triggered later in childhood or as adults, their nervous systems are already conditioned to activate the same autonomic responses and animal defenses that served them best as children (Ogden et al., 2006). As Grigsby and Stevens (2000) stress, "An activity that previously has been adaptive is likely to recur because the brain functions automatically, but probabilistically, to produce that [same] behavior in similar circumstances" (p. 51). The body's instinct to prepare for the next threat, while ensuring survival, is inconsistent with the opportunity to recover from what has just happened, to feel a sense of "it's over now," or to reset the nervous system to a calm, resting state. Years later, clients often report a feeling of anxiety when they begin to access feelings of calm: "It feels so weird," they complain, "I'm not comfortable with it."

由于生存依赖于交感神经的高度唤醒和副交感神经的低落状态来驱动动物防御反应，这些来访者的神经系统已被条件化地在压力下失调。与交感神经唤醒相连的部分（战斗部分、逃跑部分、依恋部分和冻结部分）以及与副交感神经唤醒相连的部分（顺从部分、正常生活自我部分），在神经系统对触发因素做出反应时，均处于随时激活的备战状态。在创伤条件下，个体未能发展出或已失去了"耐受之窗"的能力（Ogden等，2006；Siegel，1999）。"耐受之窗"是指个体在交感神经端容忍强烈情绪、在副交感神经端容忍无聊、麻木或"低落"感受的带宽或能力。由于大多数受创伤儿童所面临的威胁性情境是反复发生或"持续性的"（Saakvitne，2000），发展耐受之窗的机会通常极为有限。为了适应，他们的身体不得不始终保持高度警觉、随时备战，或者处于断离、麻木、被动的状态，能够忍受任何降临的事情。当他们在童年后期或成年后被触发时，其神经系统已被条件化地激活与童年时期最有效的相同自主神经反应和动物防御（Ogden等，2006）。正如Grigsby和Stevens（2000）所强调的："曾经具有适应性的活动可能会重复发生，因为大脑以自动的、但有概率性的方式，在类似情境中产生[相同的]行为"（第51页）。身体为下一次威胁做好准备的本能，虽然保证了生存，却与从刚刚发生的事情中恢复、感受到"现在已经结束了"或将神经系统重置为平静安息状态的机会相悖。多年以后，来访者常常报告，当他们开始触及平静的感觉时会产生焦虑："感觉太奇怪了，"他们抱怨道，"我对此感到不舒服。"

In addition, when the autonomic nervous system is repeatedly activated, the hippocampus (the part of the brain responsible for putting experience into chronological order and perspective preparatory to being transferred to verbal memory areas) is suppressed (Van der Kolk, 2014). Without a functioning hippocampus or prefrontal cortex, the individual is deprived of an opportunity to witness what happened or to process it and is left instead with only the "sensory elements [of the experience] … unintegrated and unattached" (Van der Kolk, Hopper, & Osterman, 2001). For the very worst of human experiences, the body's survival responses have impeded the mind and body from making meaning of what has happened. Survivors are left with a confusing array of unfinished neurobiological responses and "raw data," i.e., the overwhelming feelings, physical reactions, intrusive images, sounds, and smells associated with the event encoded as implicit memories and therefore unrecognizable as "memory."

此外，当自主神经系统被反复激活时，海马体（大脑中负责将体验按时间顺序排列并进行整合、为转移至言语记忆区域做准备的部分）会受到抑制（Van der Kolk，2014）。在缺乏正常运作的海马体或前额叶皮层的情况下，个体被剥夺了见证所发生之事或对其进行加工的机会，只剩下体验中那些"感官元素……未经整合、悬而未决"（Van der Kolk, Hopper和Osterman，2001）。面对最为惨烈的人类遭遇，身体的生存反应阻碍了心智与身体对所发生之事赋予意义的能力。幸存者只剩下一堆令人困惑的、未完成的神经生物学反应和"原始数据"——即与事件相关的排山倒海的感受、身体反应、侵入性意象、声音和气味，这些都以内隐记忆的形式编码，因此无法被辨识为"记忆"。

With the advent of brain scan technology in the mid-1990s, it became possible to study traumatic memory by observing the brain's response to "script-driven provocation," a type of research in which subjects recall the details of a specific trauma while their brain activity is scanned and recorded. Bessel van der Kolk (2014; 1994) had been arguing for years that "the body keeps the score:" that traumatic memories did not resolve as did ordinary memories because they were physiologically driven rather than subject to deliberate recall. He believed that this underlying physiology accounted for the occurrence of "re-enactment behavior" in trauma patients and a host of other symptoms we associate with post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). In addition, most trauma survivors tended to have either too many unbidden intrusive memories or "not enough" memory to feel certain of what happened, very different from their memories of ordinary events that could be willingly recalled.

随着20世纪90年代中期脑扫描技术的出现，通过观察大脑对"脚本驱动诱发"的反应来研究创伤记忆成为可能——这是一种让受试者回忆特定创伤细节、同时对大脑活动进行扫描记录的研究方法。Bessel van der Kolk（2014；1994）多年来一直主张"身体铭记着一切"：创伤记忆之所以无法像普通记忆那样消解，是因为它们由生理驱动，而非受有意识回忆的支配。他认为，这种潜在的生理机制解释了创伤患者中出现的"重演行为"以及我们与创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）相关的一系列其他症状。此外，大多数创伤幸存者倾向于拥有过多不请自来的侵入性记忆，或对所发生之事"记忆不足"而难以确定，这与他们可以主动回忆的普通事件记忆截然不同。

The brain scan research slowly but surely revealed the causes of these unique characteristics of traumatic memory: when research subjects recalled a traumatic event in their own words, the language and narrative areas of the prefrontal cortex became inactive, while emotional memory centers in the right hemisphere limbic system, especially the amygdala, became highly activated. With the left hemisphere language centers inhibited, these subjects were speechless, leaving the amygdala to "fire" (i.e., stimulate an emergency stress response) unchecked—as if the event were happening all over again. This research thus confirmed another characteristic of traumatic memory: even if it could not be deliberately retrieved and verbalized, it could be activated by triggers (i.e., stimuli in some direct or indirect way connected to traumatic events), even decades after the events were over. Gillian provides us with a good example of these characteristics of traumatic memory.

脑扫描研究缓慢而确定地揭示了创伤记忆这些独特特征的成因：当研究受试者用自己的语言回忆一个创伤事件时，前额叶皮层的语言和叙事区域变得不活跃，而右半球边缘系统的情绪记忆中枢，尤其是杏仁核，则变得高度激活。随着左半球语言中枢受到抑制，这些受试者陷入失语状态，任由杏仁核不受约束地"放电"（即刺激紧急应激反应）——仿佛事件正在重新发生一般。这项研究因此证实了创伤记忆的另一个特征：即便无法被有意识地提取和用语言表达，它也可以被触发因素（即以某种直接或间接方式与创伤事件相关联的刺激）激活，甚至在事件结束数十年后仍然如此。Gillian为我们提供了这些创伤记忆特征的一个典型案例。

Gillian's traumatic experiences of 10 years before were reflected in triggered implicit memory states: swings from anger to numbing, shame and self-doubt, difficulty attaching to others but also difficulty tolerating aloneness or separation, feeling overwhelmed and wanting to die "to get it over with." She didn't remember clearly her mother's neglect or older brother's incestuous behavior, and she would never have thought to connect her extreme feelings to the events she could recall, but as a 26-year-old still living in her parents' home, she was constantly triggered by apparently benign stimuli. Though her mother was now less depressed and her brother was an adult and out of the home, the house was full of "landmines." The most innocuous stimuli (e.g., being alone in the house, not being "heard" when she tried to express her feelings, disappointment that no one understood her) evoked strong feelings that she still wasn't "safe."

Gillian十年前的创伤经历，以被触发的内隐记忆状态呈现出来：从愤怒到麻木的剧烈摇摆、羞耻与自我怀疑、难以与他人建立依恋关系，同时又难以忍受孤独或分离，感到被压垮，想要死去"一了百了"。她对母亲的忽视或哥哥的乱伦行为没有清晰的记忆，也从未想过将自己的极端感受与能够回忆起的事件相联系。然而，作为一个26岁仍住在父母家中的年轻人，她不断被表面上看似无害的刺激所触发。尽管母亲现在不那么抑郁了，哥哥也已成年离家，但这栋房子里却到处是"地雷"。最无害的刺激（例如独自一人在家、试图表达感受时不被"倾听"、没有人理解自己的失望感）都会激发强烈的感受——她仍然不"安全"。

Uninvited Remembering

不请自来的记忆

As difficult as it can be to deliberately recall traumatic experiences as a past event, the brain's "negativity bias" (Hanson, 2014), its tendency to perceive and prioritize negative stimuli more quickly than positive stimuli, results in long-term sensitivity to all cues related to previous danger. Even very subtle cues (e.g., Gillian's being alone at home or feeling disappointed) can stimulate the implicit memories and inadvertent uninvited "remembering." Without stimulus discrimination, unavailable when the prefrontal cortex is inhibited, the body responds as if the individual was facing life-or-death threat now. It instinctively mobilizes the same survival defense responses as if the client were in immediate danger. For survivors of trauma now in their 40s, 50s, and 60s, this reactivation of memory via triggers has been especially costly. Many have been victims of triggering for many more years than they were exposed to the actual traumatic events. Without awareness that their triggered responses are evidence of body and emotional memory, they "believe" the pounding heartbeat, burning shame, braced muscles, inability to breathe, numbing, and/or explosive rage are signs that they are in danger. When it becomes clear that they are not at risk, other fears arise: maybe they are going crazy, or have proof they are defective, or maybe they are just going through the motions of life "pretending." On the basis of this "evidence," many traumatized individuals isolate and withdraw, end healthy relationships prematurely or explosively, or can't end unhealthy ones. Many function but avoid living life fully to reduce their exposure to triggers, and others engage in self-destructive behavior to manage the overwhelming feelings and activation, only to feel more damaged and defective.

尽管有意识地将创伤经历作为过去事件加以回忆本已相当困难，但大脑的"负性偏向"（Hanson，2014）——即比正性刺激更快地感知并优先处理负性刺激的倾向——会导致个体对所有与既往危险相关的线索产生长期敏感性。即便是极其细微的线索（例如Gillian独自一人在家，或感到失望），也能激活内隐记忆，引发无意间不请自来的"记忆"。在前额叶皮层受到抑制而无法进行刺激辨别的情况下，身体的反应仿佛个体此刻正面临生死威胁，本能地动员起与来访者处于即时危险时相同的生存防御反应。对于如今年届四五六十岁的创伤幸存者而言，这种通过触发因素重新激活记忆的过程尤为代价高昂。许多人遭受触发的年数，远远超过他们实际暴露于创伤事件的年数。由于不了解自己被触发的反应是身体记忆和情绪记忆的证据，他们"相信"那砰砰直跳的心跳、灼烧般的羞耻感、绷紧的肌肉、无法呼吸、麻木和/或爆发性的愤怒，是自己正处于危险中的迹象。当明显不处于危险之中时，新的恐惧又随之而来：也许他们快要精神失常了，或者这是自己有缺陷的证明，抑或他们只是在"假装"度日。基于这些"证据"，许多受创伤的个体变得孤立和退缩，过早或激烈地结束健康的关系，或者无法结束不健康的关系。许多人虽能维持基本功能，却为了减少接触触发因素而回避充分地生活，另一些人则通过自我毁灭性行为来管理排山倒海的感受和激活状态，结果只是感到更加受损和有缺陷。

"Remembering" Actions and Reactions

"记忆"行为与反应

With a more scientific understanding of the neurobiology of trauma, traumatic memory can now be understood as a highly complex phenomenon. How each individual encodes memories of the traumatic past is unique and different, but what each has in common is the way in which memory is fragmented and unintegrated. Some trauma survivors have more explicit memories for events; some have little to none. All have a host of implicit memories, including trauma-related emotions, autonomic arousal responses, muscle and body memories, cognitive distortions, and visceral memories, as well as tactile-olfactory-visual and auditory memories.

随着对创伤神经生物学的科学认识不断深入，创伤记忆现在可以被理解为一种高度复杂的现象。每个人对创伤过去的记忆编码方式都是独特而各异的，但所有人的共同之处在于记忆的碎片化和未整合状态。一些创伤幸存者对事件有较多的外显记忆；另一些则几乎没有。所有人都拥有大量内隐记忆，包括与创伤相关的情绪、自主神经唤醒反应、肌肉和身体记忆、认知扭曲以及内脏记忆，以及触觉-嗅觉-视觉和听觉记忆。

And to a greater or lesser degree, all "remember" via trauma-related procedural or conditioned learning (Grigsby & Stevens, 2000), too. The procedural memory system is a subset of implicit or nonverbal memory that encodes function, action, and habit: that is, riding a bike, driving a car, social behavior like shaking hands or smiling on greeting others, and well-learned abilities from playing the piano to playing golf or tennis. Survival "habits" are also encoded as procedurally learned behavior—for example, tendencies to automatically disconnect from strong emotion or to feel overwhelmed by it, difficulty making eye contact, need for a certain physical proximity or distance from others, withdrawal or isolation, difficulty asking for help or disclosing feelings and personal information, a tendency to say "too much" or "too little," phobias of emotions or emotional expression, avoidance of having one's back to others or to doors and windows, habits of freezing, fighting or fleeing in response to stress or triggers.

而且，所有人在程度不一的情况下，都会通过创伤相关的程序性或条件化学习来"记忆"（Grigsby和Stevens，2000）。程序性记忆系统是内隐记忆或非言语记忆的一个子集，它编码功能、行为和习惯：例如骑自行车、驾驶汽车、握手或见人微笑等社交行为，以及从弹钢琴到打高尔夫或网球等习得性技能。生存"习惯"也以程序性学习行为的形式被编码——例如，自动与强烈情绪断开联系或被其淹没的倾向，难以进行眼神接触，对与他人保持特定身体距离或亲近感的需要，退缩或孤立，难以寻求帮助或披露感受和个人信息，倾向于说"太多"或"太少"，对情绪或情绪表达的恐惧，回避背对他人或门窗，以及在压力或触发因素面前习惯性地冻结、战斗或逃跑。

To the extent that the individual has difficulty identifying implicit emotional, physical, or procedural memories as "memory," reality-testing can become compromised, creating a kind of self-triggering. When particular people or situations are experienced as unsafe, there is a tendency for them to become "demonized," that is, to become associated with danger or menace. Once the trigger is also a trigger, the body responds to it as a danger signal in its own right; the prefrontal cortex shuts down; and there is no witnessing brain to discriminate memory from present reality. Gillian's fear of helping professionals illustrates how innocuous or even positive stimuli can become associated with a sense of threat and thereafter experienced as dangerous. It also demonstrates that if the implicit memories and trauma responses are associated with a child part, reality-testing will be even more challenging because a child part's cognitive style, limited by the age or developmental stage of that part, is likely to be more concrete.

在个体难以将内隐情绪记忆、身体记忆或程序性记忆识别为"记忆"的程度上，现实检验能力可能受损，从而产生一种自我触发。当特定的人或情境被体验为不安全时，就会产生将其"魔鬼化"的倾向，即将其与危险或威胁相关联。一旦触发因素本身也成为触发因素，身体就会将其作为危险信号做出反应；前额叶皮层关闭；没有见证性的大脑来区分记忆与当下现实。Gillian对助人专业人员的恐惧，说明了无害甚至正面的刺激如何能够与威胁感相关联，继而被体验为危险。这也表明，如果内隐记忆和创伤反应与一个儿童部分相关联，现实检验将更具挑战性，因为儿童部分的认知风格受限于该部分的年龄或发展阶段，往往更为具体化。

Gillian developed a fear of therapy and therapists in her early teens: as the family's identified patient, she was sent to one therapist after another to be "fixed." None of them "got" what her behavior was communicating about her mother's alcoholism or her brother's abuse and focused on strengthening her relationship with her family by getting her to be more compliant. "Not being understood," "not being heard," or "no one getting it" are all very powerful triggers for traumatized individuals. Without realizing it, the therapists' automatic assumption that this was a healthy but overly permissive family with a challenging child made them triggering stimuli and therefore dangerous. Isolated from her peers, the "best friend" of her fragile mother, dependent on her father's financial assistance, and triggered by the helping professionals, Gillian regarded therapy as a dangerous place she had to navigate rather than as a source of help and safety. Had she known that her fear was a communication from a young child part that longed for help but was triggered by the failure of former therapists to "believe her," Gillian would have been able to make a connection between the past and present. She might even have felt protective of this little girl and tried to stand up for "her." She might have been able to reassure the child that, even if no one else did, Gillian believed her unquestionably. Gillian knew what had happened. She might even have been able to tell the little girl that not being understood or believed was hurtful but it wasn't dangerous—as long as she was in Gillian's care.

Gillian在十几岁初期就对治疗和治疗师产生了恐惧：作为家庭中被认定的"病人"，她被一个接一个地送去治疗师那里，希望被"修复"。没有一位治疗师能"读懂"她的行为所传达的信息——那些关于母亲酗酒和哥哥虐待的信息——而是将重心放在通过让她变得更顺从来强化她与家人的关系。"不被理解"、"不被倾听"或"没有人懂我"，对受创伤的个体来说都是极为强烈的触发因素。治疗师们在不自觉中做出了自动化假设——认为这是一个健康但过于宽松的家庭，有个难以管教的孩子——这使他们自身成为触发因素，因而变得危险。Gillian与同龄人相互隔绝，是脆弱母亲的"最好的朋友"，依赖父亲的经济资助，又被助人专业人员所触发，她将治疗视为一个必须小心应对的危险场所，而非帮助与安全的来源。如果她知道自己的恐惧是来自一个渴望帮助却因前任治疗师未能"相信她"而被触发的幼小儿童部分的信息，Gillian便能在过去与现在之间建立起联系。她甚至可能对这个小女孩产生保护欲，试图为"她"站出来发声。她或许能够安抚这个孩子——即使没有其他人相信她，Gillian也毫无疑问地相信她。Gillian知道发生了什么。她甚至可能告诉这个小女孩，不被理解或相信是痛苦的，但并不危险——只要她在Gillian的照顾之中。

For trauma treatment to be effective, no matter what methods we employ, survivors have to be able to integrate past and present. Concretely, this step requires education: about what traumatic memory is and is not, about triggers and triggering stimuli, about learning to accurately label triggered states ("this is a feeling memory"—"a body memory"), and cultivating the ability to trust that triggered states "tell the story" of the past without the necessity to either recall or avoid recalling specific incidents. When the therapist can also help clients connect implicit memory states to young parts of the self, it is easier to address them as a record of old dangers instead of signs of current threat. Also, when the triggered sensations, emotions, and images are reframed as "the child part's feelings," clients are better able to tolerate their intensity. Feeling compassion or protectiveness for younger selves also helps the clients to feel their "big-ness," to appreciate the physical size differences, the adult capabilities and resources, and the greater respect which individuals are accorded as adults and the greater safety it enables them to count on.

无论采用何种方法，要使创伤治疗有效，幸存者必须能够整合过去与现在。具体而言，这一步骤需要心理教育：关于创伤记忆是什么、不是什么，关于触发因素和触发刺激，关于学会准确标注被触发状态（"这是一种感受记忆"——"一种身体记忆"），以及培养相信被触发的状态"讲述着"过去的故事、无需回忆或刻意回避特定事件的能力。当治疗师能够帮助来访者将内隐记忆状态与自我的年幼部分相连接时，将其作为旧有危险的记录而非当下威胁的迹象来处理，就会变得更容易。同样，当被触发的感觉、情绪和意象被重构为"儿童部分的感受"时，来访者也能更好地承受其强度。对年幼自我感到慈悲或保护欲，也有助于来访者感受到自己的"宏大感"，意识到身体尺寸上的差异、成人的能力和资源，以及成年后所获得的更多尊重，以及这种尊重使他们能够依靠的更大安全感。

Finding "Now," Not "Then"

找到"现在"，而非"过去"

It is not difficult for traumatized individuals to re-experience the past, explicitly or implicitly. What is more challenging is how to "be here now" when one's body is communicating "danger, danger—red alert!" We now know that, as important as it is to acknowledge the past, it is even more crucial for traumatized individuals to stay connected to present time: "Right now, I can feel my feet—I can see where I am—this is just a moment—it will pass." The past does not have to be denied or avoided. It simply is (Rothschild, in press). Acknowledging the past without exploring it or observing the past intruding into present experience is very validating for traumatized clients: "Of course you are sensitive to disappointment! After a childhood of neglect and false promises, who wouldn't be sensitive to disappointment?" Acknowledgment of the past while lingering in the awareness of the present is much more helpful to clients in the early stages of therapy than exploring the past in detail and inadvertently evoking its implicit components.

对于受创伤的个体而言，以显性或隐性的方式重新体验过去并不困难。更具挑战性的是，当身体传递着"危险，危险——红色警报！"的信息时，如何"活在当下"。我们现在知道，尽管承认过去至关重要，但对于受创伤的个体而言，保持与当下时刻的连接更为关键："此刻，我能感受到我的双脚——我能看见自己身处何地——这只是一个当下时刻——它会过去的。"过去无需被否认或回避，它只是存在着（Rothschild，即将出版）。在不深入探索的情况下承认过去，或者观察过去侵入当下体验，对受创伤的来访者来说极具肯定意义："当然，你对失望很敏感！经历了一个充满忽视和虚假承诺的童年，谁不会对失望敏感呢？"在停留于当下意识的同时承认过去，对于处于治疗早期阶段的来访者而言，远比详细探索过去、无意中唤起其内隐成分更有帮助。

When the implicit aspects of memory reactivate the sense of danger "now" instead of conveying that we are remembering dangers that are over, we can't look back on the past. There is no vantage point in the here-and-now from which to look back and view what happened "then." Rather than remembering what happened, once thought to be the goal of trauma treatment, we know now that resolution of the past requires transforming the memories. As Bessel van der Kolk wrote over 20 years ago, "[The] goal of treatment is to find a way in which people can acknowledge the reality of what has happened without having to re-experience the trauma all over again. For this to occur, merely uncovering memories is not enough: they need to be modified and transformed, i.e., placed in their proper context and reconstructed into neutral or meaningful narratives. Thus, in therapy, memory paradoxically becomes an act of creation, rather than the static recording of events …" (Van der Kolk, Van der Hart, & Burbridge, 1995, p. 2).

当记忆的内隐方面在"现在"重新激活危险感，而非传递我们正在回忆已经过去的危险时，我们便无法回望过去。在此时此地，没有一个制高点可以让我们回望并审视"那时"所发生的事情。与其说记住发生了什么——这曾被认为是创伤治疗的目标——我们现在知道，解决过去需要转化记忆。正如Bessel van der Kolk二十多年前所写的："治疗的目标是找到一种方式，让人们能够承认所发生之事的现实，而无需重新完整地体验创伤。要实现这一点，仅仅揭露记忆是不够的：它们需要被修正和转化，即被置于适当的情境中，并重构为中性的或有意义的叙事。因此，在治疗中，记忆以一种悖论性的方式成为一种创造行为，而非事件的静态记录……"（Van der Kolk, Van der Hart和Burbridge，1995，第2页）。

"Transformation" or "reconstruction" of traumatic memory occurs as the individual's relationship to both the implicit and explicit memories undergoes change, as tolerance for triggered or dysregulated states expands so that he or she can "be here now," live more fully in the present, and slowly reorganize the unprocessed implicit elements into a new narrative that, as Donald Meichenbaum (2012) says, tells a "healing story."

创伤记忆的"转化"或"重构"，发生于个体与内隐记忆和外显记忆的关系双双发生变化之时：随着对被触发或失调状态的耐受力扩展，个体得以"活在当下"，更充分地生活于现在，并缓慢地将未经加工的内隐元素重组为一个新的叙事——正如Donald Meichenbaum（2012）所说，讲述一个"疗愈的故事"。
