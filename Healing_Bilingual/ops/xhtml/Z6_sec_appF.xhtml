APPENDIX F
The Four Befriending Questions

附录F：四个结友问题

Step 1: The client is asked to identify a part that is in some kind of distress. Then the therapist initiates the dialogue by inviting the client to: "Ask this distressed part what she's worried about if you ________________________________? [e.g., go to the party, say "No," get angry, stand up to your boss, etc.]

第一步： 请来访者找出一个处于某种痛苦之中的部分。然后治疗师通过邀请来访者来启动对话："询问这个处于痛苦中的部分，如果你________________________________，她担心什么？"【例如，去参加聚会、说"不"、发怒、向老板据理力争，等等。】

Step 2: "Ask her what she's worried will happen next if her worry [repeat the part's exact description of it] really does come true."

第二步： "询问她，如果她的担忧【重复该部分对担忧的原话描述】真的成为现实，她担心接下来会发生什么。"

Step 3: "Ask her: if those worries that [repeat the part's exact description] really do happen, what is she worried will happen next?"Keep repeating Step 3 until a core fear is reached: usually either the fear of annihilation or the fear of abandonment.

第三步： "询问她：如果那些【重复该部分的原话描述】真的发生了，她担心接下来会发生什么？"持续重复第三步，直到触及核心恐惧：通常是消亡的恐惧或被遗弃的恐惧之一。

Step 4: "Acknowledge her fear by mirroring it back to her, then ask the part: what does she need from you right here, right now to not be so afraid of ________________?" "Right here, right now" is the operative word: the need must be small enough and sufficiently concrete that it can be met by the normal life part with coaching as appropriate from the therapist.

第四步： "通过将她的恐惧反映回给她来确认这份恐惧，然后询问这个部分：就在这里、就在此刻，她需要你做什么，才能不那么害怕________________？""就在这里、就在此刻"是关键所在：这个需求必须足够小、足够具体，使正常生活自我能够在治疗师适当引导下加以满足。
