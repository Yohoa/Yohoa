CHAPTER 1
The Neurobiological Legacy of Trauma: How We Become Fragmented

第1章
创伤的神经生物学遗产：我们如何走向碎裂

"If the elements of the trauma are replayed again and again, the accompanying stress hormones engrave those memories even more deeply into the mind. Ordinary, day-to-day events become less and less compelling. Not being able to deeply take in what is going on around [us] makes it impossible to feel fully alive. It becomes harder to feel the joys and aggravations of ordinary life, harder to concentrate on the tasks at hand. Not being fully alive in the present keeps [us] more firmly imprisoned in the past."

"如果创伤的各个元素被一遍遍地重演，伴随而来的应激激素会将那些记忆更深地刻入大脑。日常生活中的普通事件变得越来越无法引起共鸣。无法深深吸收周围发生的一切，使人不可能感到真正的活着。越来越难以感受日常生活的喜悦与烦恼，越来越难以专注于手头的任务。不能真正活在当下，使我们更紧地被囚禁在过去。"

(Van der Kolk, 2014, p. 67)

（Van der Kolk，2014年，第67页）

In the face of abuse and neglect, especially at the hands of those they love, children need enough psychological distance from what is happening to avoid being overwhelmed and survive psychologically intact. Preserving some modicum of self-esteem, attachment to family, and hope for the future requires victims to disconnect from what has happened, doubt or disremember their experience, and disown the "bad [victim] child" to whom it happened as "not me." By holding out some sense of themselves as "good" disconnected from how they have been exploited, abused children capitalize on the human brain's innate capacity to split or compartmentalize. That "good child" might be precociously mature, sweet and helpful, perfectionistic, self-critical, or quiet and shy, but, most importantly, he or she has a way to be acceptable and safer in an unsafe world. Splitting or fragmenting in this way is an ingenious and adaptive survival strategy—but one with a steep price. To ensure that the rejected "not me" child is kept out of the way (i.e., out of consciousness) requires that, long after the traumatic events are over, individuals must continue to rely on dissociation, denial, and/or self-hatred for enforcing the disconnection. In the end, they have survived the failure of safety, the abuse, and betrayal at the cost of disowning their most vulnerable and most wounded selves. Aware that their self-presentation and ability to function is only one piece of who they really are, they now feel fraudulent. Struggling to stay away from the "bad" side and identify with the good side, they have a felt sense of "faking it," "pretending," or of being what others want them to be. For some, this conviction of fraudulence engenders resentment; for some, shame and self-doubt. For both groups, the legacy of the trauma remains alive rather than resolved.

面对虐待和忽视，尤其是来自他们所爱之人的伤害，孩子需要与正在发生的事情保持足够的心理距离，以避免被淹没、在心理上完好无损地存活下来。为了维持一点点自尊、对家庭的依恋和对未来的希望，受害者需要与已发生的事情断开联系，对自己的经历产生怀疑或遗忘，并将那个"坏的（受害）孩子"认定为"非我"而加以抛弃。通过维持某种与被剥削方式相分离的"好"的自我感，受虐待的孩子利用了人类大脑天生的分裂或分隔化能力。那个"好孩子"可能表现得早熟、温顺乐于助人、完美主义、自我批评，或安静内向，但最重要的是，他/她有一种方式在不安全的世界中显得可被接受、相对安全。以这种方式分裂或碎裂是一种巧妙而适应性的生存策略——但代价高昂。为了确保被排斥的"非我"孩子被挡在意识之外，在创伤事件结束很久之后，个体仍必须继续依赖解离、否认和/或自我憎恨来强化这种断裂。最终，他们以抛弃最脆弱、最受伤的自我为代价，存活于安全的失败、虐待和背叛之中。意识到自己展现给他人的形象和功能运转只是真实自我的一部分，他们如今感到虚假。在努力远离"坏"的一面并认同好的一面的过程中，他们有一种"装腔作势"、"假装"或成为别人想要他们成为的样子的体感。对某些人而言，这种虚伪感带来了怨恨；对另一些人而言，则是羞耻和自我怀疑。对这两个群体而言，创伤的遗产依然鲜活，而非得到化解。

As children of abuse continue to grow through latency into adolescence and subsequently adulthood, this splitting of the self supports another important aspect of surviving trauma: mastering normal developmental tasks, such as learning in school, developing peer relationships, finding interests on which to focus and even enjoy. The "good" part of the child is free to develop normally while that other part of the child bears the emotional and physical imprint of the past, scans for signs of danger, and braces for the next set of threats and abandonments. To make the individual's situation more complicated, neither the "me" nor the "not me" self is likely to have well-developed chronological memories of the traumatic events that could provide a context for self-understanding. Due to the nature of traumatic memory, what can be "recalled" tends to appear in the form of intrusive images, emotions, and physical reactions (Van der Kolk, 2006; 2014) that occur spontaneously without warning, not a sequence of narrative memories that make a clear-cut case for what happened "beyond a reasonable doubt."

随着受虐待孩子从潜伏期成长经过青春期、最终进入成年，这种自我的分裂支撑着存活于创伤的另一个重要方面：掌握正常的发展任务，例如在学校学习、发展同伴关系、找到可以专注甚至享受的兴趣。孩子的"好"的部分可以自由地正常发展，而孩子另一部分则承载着过去的情感与身体印记，扫描危险的迹象，为下一轮威胁和遗弃做好准备。使个体处境更为复杂的是，"我"和"非我"的自我都不太可能对创伤事件拥有发展良好的时序记忆，而这种时序记忆本可以为自我理解提供背景。由于创伤记忆的本质，能够被"回忆"的内容往往以侵入性画面、情绪和身体反应（Van der Kolk，2006；2014）的形式出现，这些都是自发地、毫无预兆地发生的，而非能够"排除合理怀疑地"说明发生了什么的叙事性记忆序列。

The "Living Legacy" of the Past

过去的"活的遗产"

Without a clear chronological record of what happened but vulnerable to the uninvited activation of trauma-related feeling and body memories, individuals are left with a legacy of symptoms and reactions with no context that identifies them as memory. Survivors of trauma later present in therapy with descriptions of their anxiety, depression, shame, low self-esteem, loneliness and alienation, problems with anger, and impulsivity or acting out. They might be troubled by chronic expectations of danger: intrusive fear and dread, hypervigilance ("eyes in the back of my head"), chronic shame and self-hatred, a conviction that the worst is about to happen, hopelessness and helplessness, fear of abandonment, numbing and disconnection from emotions. Or they may come to therapy as a last resort because they are fighting a losing battle against addiction, self-harming impulses, eating disorders, or a longing or even determination to die. Often, they can tell us very little about what evokes these self-destructive impulses that bring quick short-term relief at great risk to their safety: "I do it to punish myself," "I hate myself," "I don't deserve to live," "I'm disgusting—I wish I were dead." They sometimes struggle with how to connect these patterns to the past—but, more often, they prefer not to think about what happened then or minimize it: "It wasn't that bad."

在没有清晰的时序记录的情况下，个体却又容易被创伤相关的情感和身体记忆所不请自来地激活，留下的是没有任何背景将其识别为记忆的症状和反应的遗产。创伤幸存者随后在治疗中呈现出对焦虑、抑郁、羞耻、低自尊、孤独和疏离、愤怒问题以及冲动或行动化的描述。他们可能受到慢性危险预期的困扰：侵入性的恐惧和忧虑、过度警觉（"脑后长眼"）、慢性羞耻和自我憎恨、最坏的事情即将发生的确信、无望和无助、被遗弃的恐惧、情绪麻木和断联。或者他们可能将治疗作为最后的手段，因为他们在与成瘾、自伤冲动、饮食障碍或渴望甚至决心去死的斗争中节节败退。通常，他们几乎无法告诉我们是什么引发了这些自我毁灭的冲动——这些冲动以巨大的安全风险为代价带来快速的短期解脱："我这样做是为了惩罚自己"，"我恨我自己"，"我不值得活着"，"我令人厌恶——我希望我死了"。他们有时挣扎于如何将这些模式与过去联系起来——但更常见的是，他们宁愿不去想那时发生了什么，或将其最小化："那没那么糟糕。"

In the early history of trauma treatment, therapists relied on the "talking cure," the most commonly accepted practice in psychotherapy from the time of Freud to the present day (Rothschild, in press), to address the strong emotional reactivity of traumatized clients coupled with their lack of clarity for the traumatic events. Clients were generally encouraged to keep retrieving the memories of "what happened" until they had established a detailed narrative of the chronological events. But, when therapists adopted this approach, rather than resolving the traumatic past, clients became flooded with overwhelming implicit memories and traumatic reactions, increasingly symptomatic rather than at peace (Herman, 1992; Van der Kolk, 2014). Talking about the events of the past, therapists discovered, led to more implicit "reliving" of it. Unwittingly, the therapist and the "good child," now a grown-up client, were at long last validating the events experienced by the disowned "not me" child—while at the same time, activating the trauma-related parts and triggering their implicit memories. Feeling endangered yet again, the "not me" children were crying for help, still without being heard.

在创伤治疗的早期历史中，治疗师依赖"谈话疗法"——从弗洛伊德时代到今天，这是心理治疗中最被广泛接受的实践（Rothschild，待出版）——来处理创伤来访者强烈的情绪反应与对创伤事件缺乏清晰认识的问题。来访者通常被鼓励不断检索"发生了什么"的记忆，直到他们建立起对时序事件的详细叙述。但当治疗师采用这种方法时，来访者不仅没有化解创伤性过去，反而被大量的内隐记忆和创伤反应所淹没，变得愈加有症状而非获得平静（Herman，1992；Van der Kolk，2014）。治疗师发现，谈论过去的事件导致了更多内隐性的"重温"。在不知不觉中，治疗师和那个"好孩子"（现在已成为成年来访者）终于确认了被抛弃的"非我"孩子所经历的事件——同时激活了创伤相关部分并触发了它们的内隐记忆。再次感到危险，"非我"孩子们在呼救，依然没有被听见。

I have long believed that trauma treatment must address the effects of the traumatic past, not its events. Being able to tolerate remembering a horrific experience is not as important a goal as feeling safe right here, right now—or being able to reassure oneself that the racing of the heart is just a triggered response, not a sign of danger—or being able to relate to shame, grief, and anger as the feeling memories of child selves too young to comfort themselves. In my view, resolution of painful past events cannot truly be achieved without reclaiming the lost children and disowned parts of ourselves, extending to them a helping hand, welcoming them "home" at long last, creating safety for them, and making them feel wanted, needed, and valued. It took many decades of scientific research for the clinical world to accept that child abuse constituted an epidemic, not a rare occurrence, and that untreated post-traumatic stress resulted in tremendous social costs, not just individual suffering. Only in the last ten years have the concepts of implicit memory and bodily-driven responses to trauma become increasingly widespread (Ogden et al., 2006; Van der Kolk, 2014), but, even now, theoretical ideas about splitting, parts of the self, and dissociation are still controversial and often avoided. We as a field have not yet accepted that compartmentalization is normal under stress and much more common than we generally recognize. In a parallel process, the mental health world has had a history of disowning the prevalence of child abuse, dissociation, and fragmentation of the personality, either by ignoring its manifestations or by invalidating it as "factitious" or "malingering." To be the "good child" in the psychiatric treatment world, therapists have been under pressure to "un-see" signs of dissociation, to diagnose voices as a psychotic symptom, and to treat fragmented clients "as if" they were whole integrated human beings. To be an integrated human, as Dan Siegel (2010) insists, requires "differentiation—with linkage," that is, it necessitates the ability to make distinctions between different parts of the self, to name them as parts, but also to link them to other parts and to the whole of which they are a part. Disowning parts of one's self and over-identifying with other parts does not facilitate integration and a sense of being whole, nor does it engender an internal sense of safety that could counteract the after-effects of an unsafe, unwelcoming hostile world.

我长期以来坚信，创伤治疗必须处理创伤性过去的影响，而非其事件本身。能够忍受对可怕经历的回忆，并不如现在此刻此地感到安全那么重要——也不如能够自我安慰"心跳加速只是一种被触发的反应，不是危险的信号"那么重要——也不如能够将羞耻、悲痛和愤怒理解为年幼自我太小而无法自我安慰的情感记忆那么重要。在我看来，如果不重新找回那些失落的孩子和被抛弃的自我部分、向他们伸出援手、最终欢迎他们"回家"、为他们创造安全感，并让他们感到被需要、被珍视，那么对痛苦过去事件的真正化解就无从实现。临床世界花了数十年的科学研究才接受儿童虐待是一种流行病而非罕见事件，以及未经治疗的创伤后应激会造成巨大的社会代价，而不仅仅是个体痛苦。内隐记忆和创伤躯体反应的概念只是在过去十年才变得越来越普遍（Ogden等，2006；Van der Kolk，2014），但即便如此，关于分裂、自我各部分和解离的理论思想仍然存在争议并常被回避。我们作为一个领域，尚未接受分隔化在压力下是正常的，且比我们通常认识到的更为普遍。在一个平行的过程中，精神健康世界有着否认儿童虐待、解离和人格碎裂普遍性的历史，要么忽视其表现，要么将其定性为"人为的"或"装病"而加以否定。为了成为精神病学治疗世界中的"好孩子"，治疗师被迫"看不见"解离的迹象，将幻听诊断为精神病症状，并将碎裂的来访者"当作"完整整合的人来治疗。正如Dan Siegel（2010年）所坚持的，成为一个整合的人需要"区分与连结"，即需要能够区分自我的不同部分、将它们命名为部分，同时也将它们连结到其他部分以及它们所属的整体。抛弃自我的各部分并过度认同其他部分，既不能促进整合和完整感，也不能在内心产生可以抵消不安全、不欢迎的敌对世界后遗症的安全感。

Parallel Lives: The Disowning of Dissociation

平行的生命：解离的否认

In the history of the trauma field, the concepts of dissociation and splitting have been repeatedly observed as complications of trauma but consistently rejected as "not me," that is, as not valid or believable within the prevailing diagnostic systems and therefore to be avoided. One of the difficulties with gaining acceptance for the existence of dissociative splitting and especially dissociative disorders has been an absence of studies demonstrating a scientific basis for such dramatic, difficult-to-treat symptoms. Theories of parts tend to be metaphorical rather than biologically or brain-based. In the dissociative disorders world, the explanatory hypothesis has historically been stress-related: when events are traumatic, the theory asserts, they exceed the brain's capacity to tolerate or process them as wholes. Therefore, they must be split or compartmentalized so that overwhelming event memories are shared by dissociated parts of the same age, each carrying some portion of the memory. In this model, each part is viewed as a repository of memory, representing the history of the client at a specific time. In treatment, the parts are encouraged to "download" or disclose their memories so that the "host" can share their pain and accept their shared past. Only then could the parts begin fusing into one homogenized whole (Putnam, 1989). Although this hypothesis makes intuitive sense to many clinicians and clients, it lacks the scientific validity necessary to overcome the skepticism and disowning of dissociation by the mental health world.

在创伤领域的历史中，解离和分裂的概念被反复观察为创伤的并发症，但一再被以"非我"的方式拒绝，即在主流诊断系统中被视为无效或不可信，因而应加以回避。获得对解离性分裂尤其是解离性障碍存在的认可，困难之一在于缺乏证明此类戏剧性、难以治疗的症状具有科学基础的研究。关于各部分的理论往往是隐喻性的，而非以生物学或大脑为基础。在解离性障碍领域，历史上的解释性假说与压力相关：当事件是创伤性的时，该理论主张，它们超过了大脑作为一个整体加以耐受或处理的能力。因此，它们必须被分裂或分隔化，以便不堪重负的事件记忆由同龄的解离部分共同承担，每个部分携带记忆的某个部分。在这个模型中，每个部分被视为记忆的储存库，代表着来访者在特定时期的历史。在治疗中，各部分被鼓励"下载"或披露它们的记忆，以便"主人格"能够分担它们的痛苦并接受共同的过去。只有这样，各部分才能开始融合成一个同质化的整体（Putnam，1989年）。虽然这一假说对许多临床医生和来访者来说具有直觉上的合理性，但它缺乏克服精神健康世界对解离的怀疑和否认所需的科学效度。

Another theory is that multiplicity is normal, that all human beings have multi-consciousness rather than uni-consciousness. A mindfulness-based approach to understanding parts based on this hypothesis is Internal Family Systems or IFS (Schwartz, 1995; 2001). Known for its compassionate tone and cultivation of mindful awareness, IFS also depends upon a metaphorical theory, this one based on intrapsychic defenses: the "not-me" child is termed an "exile," hidden from conscious awareness by the activity of "managers." When the manager parts do not offer enough protection to keep exiles out of awareness, acting out by another set of parts, the "firefighters," creates distraction and crisis. Healing occurs in the IFS model when the exiled parts are reclaimed, can feel safe enough with "self," the higher self of the client, to share the disowned memories and be "unburdened" of the painful emotions and beliefs connected to the trauma.

另一种理论认为多元性是正常的，所有人类都拥有多重意识而非单一意识。基于这一假说的正念导向的部分理解方法是内部家庭系统（IFS）（Schwartz，1995；2001年）。以其富有慈悲的基调和培养正念意识而著称，IFS同样依赖于一种隐喻性理论，这个理论基于内心防御：那个"非我"孩子被称为"流放部分"，被"管理部分"的活动藏匿于有意识的意识之外。当管理部分提供的保护不足以将流放部分挡在意识之外时，另一组部分——"救火部分"——的行动化制造了分心和危机。在IFS模型中，当流放部分被重新找回，能够在来访者的更高自我——"自我"——面前感到足够安全，愿意分享被抛弃的记忆，并从与创伤相关的痛苦情绪和信念中得到"减负"时，愈合就会发生。

But to bring credibility to such a controversial "not us" topic as dissociation, good clinical models without a theoretical basis are not sufficient. It took the neuroscience revolution to provide a scientific explanation for the concept of "splitting" and even to the terminology of "parts of the self." It has taken years of research to challenge the fixed negative beliefs about dissociation and dissociative disorders so prevalent in the field (Brand et al., 2016).

但要为解离这样一个颇具争议的"非我们"话题带来可信度，没有理论基础的优秀临床模型是不够的。正是神经科学革命为"分裂"的概念甚至"自我各部分"的术语提供了科学解释。研究人员花了多年时间来挑战该领域中关于解离和解离性障碍的根深蒂固的负面信念（Brand等，2016年）。

Compartmentalization under Stress: Exploiting the Fault Lines

压力下的分隔化：利用断层线

A biological basis for understanding compartmentalization under stress lies in the brain's innate "fault lines," the fact that its functions are tied to and governed by different regions and differentiated structures within each region (Van der Hart et al., 2004). One "fault line" for splitting available even at birth is the right hemisphere-left hemisphere split. Though children are born with both left and right hemispheres, they are right brain dominant for most of childhood (Cozolino, 2002; Schore, 2001). The slower developing left brain has spurts of growth around the age of language development and again at adolescence, but the development of left brain dominance is only achieved very gradually over the course of the first eighteen years of life. In addition, the corpus callosum, the part of the brain that makes possible right brain-left brain communication, also develops slowly and only becomes fully elaborated around the age of twelve (Cozolino, 2002; Teicher, 2004). Thus, in the early years of childhood, right brain experience is relatively independent of left brain experience, lending itself to splitting should the need arise. Studying brain development in children and adolescents, Martin Teicher has observed a correlation between a history of abuse and/or neglect and under-development of the corpus callosum compared to normal controls (2004), which would also support the hypothesis that trauma is associated with independent development of right and left hemispheres and that deficits in communication between the two brains may hinder right-left integration, leaving clients with "two brains" (Gazzaniga, 2015) instead of one integrated brain.

理解压力下分隔化的生物学基础在于大脑固有的"断层线"——即其功能与不同区域及每个区域内分化的结构相关联并受其支配这一事实（Van der Hart等，2004年）。甚至在出生时就已存在的一条分裂"断层线"是右半球与左半球的分裂。尽管孩子出生时左右半球都有，但他们在大部分童年期都是右脑主导的（Cozolino，2002；Schore，2001年）。发展较慢的左脑在语言发展年龄前后以及青春期出现增长高峰，但左脑优势的发展只是在人生最初十八年中逐渐实现的。此外，胼胝体——使右脑-左脑沟通成为可能的大脑部分——也发展缓慢，直到大约十二岁才完全发育（Cozolino，2002；Teicher，2004年）。因此，在童年早期，右脑体验与左脑体验相对独立，一旦需要就容易发生分裂。在研究儿童和青少年大脑发育时，Martin Teicher观察到虐待和/或忽视史与胼胝体欠发育之间存在相关性（2004年），这也支持了创伤与右脑和左脑独立发育相关的假说，以及两脑之间沟通的缺陷可能阻碍左右整合，使来访者拥有"两个大脑"（Gazzaniga，2015年）而非一个整合大脑的假说。

The "split-brain research" of the 1970s (Gazzaniga, 2015) was the first research to show the degree to which left and right hemispheres of the brain operate independently and quite differently. "Split-brain" research refers to studying patients whose right and left hemispheres have been separated by injury or surgery or who have suffered damage to the corpus callosum. Although these patients demonstrate that there seems to be some shared knowledge available to both hemispheres, only the left hemisphere uses language to describe experience and information, while the right hemisphere is more visual, better able to recognize differences and similarities between stimuli but lacking words to describe it. The right hemisphere tends to remember episodically and implicitly, whereas the left is specialized for autobiographical memory and acquired knowledge. But the left hemisphere's ability to encode information verbally does not imply that its recollection of events is more "accurate." "The left hemisphere has a tendency to grasp the gist of a situation, make an inference that fits in well with the general schema of the event, and toss out anything that does not. This elaboration has a deleterious effect on accuracy but usually makes it easier to process new information. The right hemisphere does not do this. It is totally truthful and only identifies the original [information]" (Gazzaniga, 2015, p. 152); that is, the right hemisphere does not "forget" the nonverbal aspects of experience and does not interpret it. Emotions, these researchers discovered, are experienced on both sides of the brain but can only be verbalized by the left hemisphere; the right hemisphere might act on the emotion but could not describe it in words. And without an exchange of information via the corpus callosum, researchers observed that the left hemisphere might have no memory of the right hemisphere's emotion-driven actions and reactions.

1970年代的"裂脑研究"（Gazzaniga，2015年）是第一个揭示大脑左右半球在多大程度上独立且截然不同地运作的研究。"裂脑"研究是指对因伤或手术导致左右半球被分离，或遭受胼胝体损伤的患者的研究。尽管这些患者表明两个半球似乎共享一些知识，但只有左半球使用语言来描述体验和信息，而右半球更具视觉性，更善于识别刺激之间的差异和相似性，但缺乏言词来描述它。右半球倾向于以情节性和内隐性方式记忆，而左半球则专门处理自传体记忆和习得知识。但左半球以语言形式编码信息的能力并不意味着其对事件的回忆更加"准确"。"左半球有一种倾向，抓住情境的要旨，做出符合事件总体图式的推断，并丢弃任何不符合的内容。这种精加工对准确性有有害影响，但通常使处理新信息更容易。右半球不这样做。它完全忠实，只识别原始信息"（Gazzaniga，2015年，第152页）；即右半球不会"忘记"体验的非言语方面，也不会对其进行诠释。研究人员发现，情绪在大脑两侧都有体验，但只能由左半球言语化；右半球可能基于情绪采取行动，但无法用语言描述它。在没有通过胼胝体进行信息交换的情况下，研究人员观察到左半球可能对右半球的情绪驱动的行动和反应毫无记忆。

Attachment research has also contributed to the literature supporting the concept of an innate tendency to compartmentalize under stress. In longitudinal studies of attachment behavior (Lyons-Ruth et al., 2006; Solomon & George, 1999; Solomon & Siegel, 2003), researchers have demonstrated that children with disorganized attachment status at age one are significantly more likely to exhibit dissociative symptoms by age 19 and/or to be diagnosed with borderline personality disorder or dissociative identity disorder in adulthood. When attachment figures are abusive, the child's only source of safety and protection becomes simultaneously the source of immediate danger, leaving the child caught between two conflicting sets of instincts. On the one hand, they are driven by the attachment instinct to seek proximity, comfort, and protection from attachment figures. On the other, they are driven by equally strong animal defense instincts to freeze, fight, flee, or submit or dissociate before they get too close to the frightening parent. Liotti (1999) hypothesizes that dissociative splitting is necessitated to manage this irresolvable struggle between two such strong emotional and physical drives and two very different internal working models: biologically, the attachment figure automatically elicits the cry for help response or proximity-seeking drive, yet approaching abusive or threatening adults also elicits fear and fight and flight responses.

依恋研究也为支持压力下固有分隔化倾向的文献做出了贡献。在依恋行为的纵向研究中（Lyons-Ruth等，2006；Solomon & George，1999；Solomon & Siegel，2003），研究人员证明，一岁时具有紊乱型依恋状态的儿童，到19岁时出现解离症状的可能性显著更高，和/或成年后被诊断为边缘性人格障碍或解离性身份障碍的可能性也显著更高。当依恋人物具有虐待性时，孩子唯一的安全和保护来源同时成为直接危险的来源，使孩子被两组相互冲突的本能所困。一方面，他们受依恋本能驱使，向依恋人物寻求亲近、安慰和保护。另一方面，他们受同样强烈的动物防御本能驱使，在接近令人恐惧的父母之前冻结、战斗、逃跑、顺从或解离。Liotti（1999年）假设，解离性分裂是管理这两种强烈的情绪和身体驱力之间以及两种截然不同的内部工作模型之间无法解决的斗争所必需的：在生物学上，依恋人物自动引发求救反应或寻求亲近的驱力，然而接近具有虐待性或威胁性的成人也引发恐惧、战斗和逃跑反应。

Van der Hart, Nijenhuis, and Steele (2004; 2006) cite yet another set of fault lines along which dissociative compartmentalization can occur: the "action systems" or drives that propel the stages of child development and adaptation to the environment. One set of drives can be seen in children's innate propensity to attach, explore, play, and develop social engagement and collaboration skills and then, as older children and adults, learn to regulate their bodily needs, mate and reproduce, and care for the next generation (Panksepp, 1998; Van der Hart et al., 2006). Equally, though, children have to depend upon their instinctive animal defenses (hypervigilance, cry for help, fight and flight, freeze, collapse and submission) to quickly inhibit exploration, social engagement, and regulating functions to ensure automatic self-protective behavior. For children raised in unsafe environments, both types of action system are necessary in response to changing internal and external demands: for example, going to school requires a part of the personality that can explore, pay attention in class, learn, and socially engage with peers and teachers. At home, with parents who may be withdrawn or neglectful at some times and violent at others, the ability to rapidly shift from state to state as needed to deal with different threats could be essential: for example, in response to the sound of the abuser's voice or footsteps, panic or fear could alert the individual to danger. Playfulness might lift the parent's irritable mood and facilitate a positive connection by making him or her laugh (social engagement). At times, it might be helpful to capitalize on the submission response to become the precociously responsible child who tries to protect younger siblings in the face of the violent behavior, but at other times, it could be safer to rely on hypervigilance, staying "on guard," carefully observing the parents' mood, and reacting in whatever ways best defend against their "frightened or frightening" behavior. These patterns of compartmentalization can be conceptualized as trauma-related procedural learning: it is safer to adapt using a system of selves rather than becoming a fully integrated "self."

Van der Hart、Nijenhuis和Steele（2004；2006年）还引用了另一组解离性分隔化可能发生的断层线：驱动儿童发展阶段和适应环境的"行动系统"或驱力。一组驱力体现在儿童固有的依恋、探索、游戏以及发展社交参与和协作技能的倾向，以及后来作为年长儿童和成人学会调节身体需求、交配繁殖和照顾下一代（Panksepp，1998；Van der Hart等，2006年）。然而同样地，儿童必须依赖其本能的动物防御（过度警觉、求救、战斗和逃跑、冻结、崩溃和顺从）来迅速抑制探索、社交参与和调节功能，以确保自动的自我保护行为。对于在不安全环境中成长的儿童，两种类型的行动系统都是回应不断变化的内外部需求所必需的：例如，上学需要一个能够探索、在课堂上专注、学习以及与同伴和老师社交参与的人格部分。在家里，与有时退缩或疏于照顾、有时又具有暴力倾向的父母在一起，根据需要迅速从一种状态转换到另一种状态以应对不同威胁的能力可能至关重要：例如，对施虐者的声音或脚步声做出反应，恐慌或恐惧可以警示个体危险的存在。嬉戏玩耍可能能够提振父母的烦躁情绪，通过逗他/她笑来促进正面的连结（社交参与）。有时，利用顺从反应成为在暴力行为面前试图保护年幼兄弟姐妹的早熟负责任孩子可能是有帮助的，但有时候，依赖过度警觉、保持"戒备"、仔细观察父母的情绪并以最能抵御其"受惊吓或令人恐惧"行为的方式做出反应，可能更为安全。这些分隔化模式可以被概念化为创伤相关的程序性学习：使用一个自我系统来适应，比成为一个完全整合的"自我"更为安全。

Extrapolating from the observations by Charles Myers of "shell-shocked" World War I veterans, Van der Hart and colleagues (2004) labeled these different drives or systems "part(s) of the personality." Although "part of the personality" remains a very controversial term in the mental health world, it has certain advantages: first, use of the word "part" clearly suggests that there is a whole person and personality—of which we are studying just one piece. Secondly, it is a word so commonly used to describe normal ambivalence or inner conflicts (e.g., "Part of me wants to eat that piece of cake so badly, but another part won't let me") that it is easily adopted by clients. And lastly, research has demonstrated the propensity of the brain to develop neural networks holding related neural pathways that consistently "fire" together, and these neural systems often encode complex systems of traits or systems (Schore, 2001) that represent aspects of our personalities or ways of being. For example, if neural pathways activating the proximity drive fire consistently in the presence of the attachment figure, along with neural pathway holding feelings of loneliness and yearning for comfort and a neural network holding the tendency to believe that "she loves me—she would never hurt me," the result might be a neural system representing a young child part of the personality with a toddler's yearning for comfort and closeness along with the magical thinking that the attachment figure will be safe and loving, yet also the uneasy feeling that something is not right. Such neural systems can be complex with a subjective sense of identity or can be a simpler collection of traits associated with different roles played by the individual.

从Charles Myers对一战"炮弹休克"退伍军人的观察推断，Van der Hart和同事们（2004年）将这些不同的驱力或系统称为"人格的部分"。尽管"人格的部分"在精神健康世界中仍是一个极具争议的术语，但它有某些优势：首先，使用"部分"一词清楚地表明存在一个完整的人和人格——我们只是在研究其中的一个部分。其次，这个词在描述正常的矛盾心理或内部冲突时极为常用（例如，"我的一部分非常想吃那块蛋糕，但另一部分不允许我"），因此很容易被来访者采纳。最后，研究表明，大脑有发展保持相关神经通路的神经网络并使其持续"一起激发"的倾向，这些神经系统往往编码了代表我们人格方面或存在方式的特质或系统的复杂系统（Schore，2001年）。例如，如果在依恋人物存在时激活亲近驱力的神经通路持续激发，连同保持孤独感和渴望安慰的神经通路以及保持相信"她爱我——她绝不会伤害我"倾向的神经网络，结果可能是一个代表人格年幼孩子部分的神经系统，带着幼儿对安慰和亲密的渴望，以及依恋人物会是安全和充满爱的魔幻思维，但同时也有事情不对劲的不安感。这样的神经系统可以是带有主观身份认同感的复杂系统，也可以是与个体扮演的不同角色相关的更简单的特质集合。

Van der Hart et al. (2006) borrowed the language of Myers in describing the aspect of self driven by daily life priorities as the "apparently normal part of the personality" and parts driven by animal defense responses as the "emotional parts of the personality," or, individually, the fight, flight, freeze, submit, or attach for survival parts. In this book, I will use terms that I have found more useful in clinical practice: the "going on with normal life part" and the "trauma-related parts" of the personality. In avoiding the words, "apparently normal," my goal is to emphasize the positive evolutionary function of parts of us driven to survive or persevere and to challenge clients' tendencies to see their ability to function as a "false self" and their trauma-related responses as the "true self." In addition, emphasizing the positive aims and goals of the "normal life" part encourages clients to strengthen their ability to regulate the tumultuous emotions and autonomic dysregulation of the animal defense-related parts, rather than either trying to ignore them or interpreting them as "the true self." Connecting different parts to the survival responses that drive their actions and reactions challenges the client's automatic shame and self-doubt: experiences of feeling rage make more sense when tied to a "fight part" triggered by an act of unfairness; automatic passivity and the inability to say "no" feels less shameful when connected to a young child submit part whose sense of safety is tied to pleasing others or feeling "less than." The concept that each part represents a way of surviving dangerous conditions, that each represents a different approach to self-protection, gives meaning and dignity to the fragmentation. The parts in this view are not repositories of memory; they were a means of surviving the 'worst of the worst,' not a means of remembering it. As I often say to my clients, "We wouldn't be sitting here together today if each part had not done its job well, if each hadn't helped you to survive." But as carriers of our instinctive survival responses, the parts remain poised for the next threat or the next trauma-related trigger for decades after "it" is over.

Van der Hart等人（2006年）借用了Myers的语言，将由日常生活优先事项驱动的自我方面描述为"人格的表观正常部分"，将由动物防御反应驱动的部分描述为"人格的情绪部分"，或单独称为战斗、逃跑、冻结、顺从或为生存而依恋的部分。在本书中，我将使用我发现在临床实践中更为有用的术语："正常生活的持续部分"和人格的"创伤相关部分"。通过避免"表观正常"这个词，我的目标是强调我们中受驱动去存活或坚持的部分的正面进化功能，并挑战来访者将其功能运转能力视为"虚假自我"、将其创伤相关反应视为"真实自我"的倾向。此外，强调"正常生活"部分的正面目标和目的，鼓励来访者加强其调节动物防御相关部分混乱情绪和自主神经失调的能力，而不是试图忽略它们或将它们解读为"真实自我"。将不同的部分与驱动其行动和反应的生存反应联系起来，挑战了来访者自动的羞耻和自我怀疑：当愤怒感与被不公正行为触发的"战斗部分"相联系时，愤怒体验变得更有意义；当与其安全感依赖于取悦他人或感到"低人一等"的年幼孩子顺从部分相联系时，自动被动和无法说"不"的感觉就不那么令人羞耻了。每个部分代表一种在危险条件下生存的方式、每个部分代表一种不同的自我保护方式这一概念，赋予了碎裂意义和尊严。在这种观点中，各部分不是记忆的储存库；它们是在"最糟中的最糟"中存活的手段，而不是记忆它的手段。正如我经常对来访者说的："如果每个部分没有做好它的工作，如果每个部分没有帮助你存活，我们今天就不会坐在这里了。"但作为我们本能生存反应的携带者，各部分在"那件事"结束后数十年内仍随时准备迎接下一个威胁或下一个创伤相关触发因素。

Recognizing the Signs of Structurally Dissociated Parts

识别结构性解离部分的迹象

Just as each individual responds to trauma differently, we would expect each client's structurally dissociated personality system to be unique. Clients whose histories of chronic trauma and/or multiple types of abuse and/or neglect necessitated more complex structural dissociation are likely to have a well-developed going on with normal life self and several different parts driven by the survival responses of fight, flight, freeze, submission, or cry for help. But even in these clients, fragmentation can be more subtle and permeable or more dramatic and rigid: some clients (e.g., those carrying PTSD or bipolar II diagnoses) might shift between clear-cut states (sometimes irritable, sometimes depressed, at other times anxious). Clients with borderline personality disorder might present at times as regressed and clinging; at other times, cold and angry; then, at still other times, hopeless and passively suicidal, while all the while functioning fairly well at work. With mild to moderate dissociative disorder not otherwise specified (DDNOS), the therapist might encounter clearly observable compartmentalization and some difficulty with memory (e.g., not clearly recalling the intense anger and aggressive behavior of their fight parts or the neediness of a young child part with separation anxiety). In clients with dissociative identity disorder (DID), not only will the number of trauma-related parts tend to be greater overall, but these clients are more likely to have other subparts serving the priorities associated with the going on with normal life self, for example, a professional self, a parenting part, or a part with special talents or social skills. In addition, as the neural systems governing each part become more elaborated and autonomous, DID clients start to exhibit switching and time loss as they are "hijacked" by parts who, when triggered, act outside the conscious awareness of the going on with normal life self.

正如每个人对创伤的反应不同，我们可以预期每位来访者的结构性解离人格系统都是独特的。那些慢性创伤和/或多种类型虐待和/或忽视的历史使他们需要更复杂的结构性解离的来访者，可能拥有发展良好的正常生活持续自我和几个由战斗、逃跑、冻结、顺从或求救等生存反应驱动的不同部分。但即使在这些来访者中，碎裂也可能更为微妙和可渗透，或更为戏剧性和刚性：一些来访者（例如，那些携带创伤后应激障碍或双相情感障碍II型诊断的人）可能在清晰的状态之间转换（有时烦躁，有时抑郁，另一些时候焦虑）。边缘性人格障碍来访者有时可能表现为退行和依附；另一些时候，冷漠和愤怒；然后，又在另一些时候，无望和被动性自杀，而始终在工作中运转得相当好。在轻至中度的其他未特指的解离障碍（DDNOS）中，治疗师可能遇到清晰可观察的分隔化以及一些记忆困难（例如，不能清楚回忆其战斗部分的强烈愤怒和攻击性行为，或具有分离焦虑的年幼孩子部分的需求性）。在解离性身份障碍（DID）来访者中，创伤相关部分的数量总体上往往更多，而且这些来访者更有可能拥有服务于正常生活持续自我相关优先事项的其他子部分，例如职业自我、父母角色部分或具有特殊才能或社交技能的部分。此外，随着支配每个部分的神经系统变得更加精细和自主，DID来访者开始表现出人格转换和时间失去，因为他们被那些在被触发时在正常生活持续自我有意识意识之外行动的部分所"劫持"。

While updating her curriculum vita, Celia, a successful organizational consultant, was surprised to discover that she had won an award in 1990 for which she had no memory. Not only could she not recall winning it, she couldn't recall what she had done to deserve it! Annie also discovered disturbing evidence of missing time and dissociative hijacking when she received a letter from her oldest friend asking her never to contact him again under any circumstances. "I will never forgive you for what you said to me last week—it was cruel, and I don't want to be hurt anymore." Lacking a memory of having spoken to him recently, she could not imagine why 'she' had been angry at him and what 'she' could have said that upset him so much.

在更新简历时，成功的组织顾问Celia惊讶地发现她在1990年获得了一个奖项，而对此她毫无记忆。她不仅不记得获奖，还不记得自己做了什么值得这个奖！Annie也发现了令人不安的时间缺失和解离性劫持证据，当她收到最老朋友的来信，要求她在任何情况下都不要再联系他时。"我永远不会原谅你上周对我说的话——那很残忍，我不想再受伤了。"由于没有最近与他交谈过的记忆，她无法想象为什么"她"对他生气了，以及"她"说了什么让他如此不安。

Characteristically, while the going on with normal life part tries to carry on (functioning at a job, raising the children, organizing home life, even taking up meaningful personal and professional goals), other parts serving the animal defense functions of fight, flight, freeze, submit, and "cling" or attach for survival continue to be activated by trauma-related stimuli, resulting in hypervigilance and mistrust, overwhelming emotions, incapacitating depression or anxiety, self-destructive behavior, and fear or hopelessness about the future, that is, the difficulties that often lead clients to seek psychotherapy.

典型地，当正常生活的持续部分试图继续运作（在工作中运转、抚养孩子、组织家庭生活，甚至承担有意义的个人和职业目标）时，其他服务于战斗、逃跑、冻结、顺从和为生存而"依附"或依恋等动物防御功能的部分，继续被创伤相关刺激激活，导致过度警觉和不信任、难以承受的情绪、使人丧失能力的抑郁或焦虑、自我毁灭行为以及对未来的恐惧或无望——即那些经常促使来访者寻求心理治疗的困难。

症状作为来自各部分的沟通

Although some clients may present with diagnosable dissociative disorders, many more will come to therapy with trauma-related symptoms that appear initially straightforward, such as PTSD, anxiety and mood disorders, or personality disorders. 许多来访者在被动物防御相关部分的创伤反应和内隐记忆所淹没或"劫持"后前来接受治疗；另一些来访者则是当他们试图断开或否认这些创伤反应的努力导致慢性抑郁或人格解体时才来。尽管某些来访者可能表现出可诊断的解离性障碍，但更多人会带着最初看起来较为直接的创伤相关症状前来治疗，如创伤后应激障碍、焦虑和情绪障碍或人格障碍。然而，某些症状可以提醒我们注意潜在结构性解离的存在，例如以下这些。

Signs of Internal Splitting

内在分裂的迹象

The client functions highly at work when stimulated by "positive triggers" (work assignments, collaboration with peers, responsibilities) while regressing at home or in personal relationships because of the trauma triggers associated with those environments. Or the client might report alternating fears of abandonment followed by pushing away those who try to get close or a tendency to initially idealize others followed by disillusionment and anger when they fail the client in some way. The fears of abandonment represent communications of the attach or cry for help part as closeness to others exacerbates that part's separation anxiety; the pushing away is the response of a fight part activated by the risk of vulnerability or hurt. Splitting often manifests in paradoxical behavior: fears of triggers might be intense, while the client lacks appropriate fears for real threats; the client plans a family vacation for the following summer while simultaneously ruminating on her determination to suicide; or he describes himself as "enlightened," kind, and reasonable, while family and friends portray the client as typically angry, arrogant, and demanding.

来访者在被"正面触发因素"（工作任务、与同事的合作、责任）刺激时在工作中高效运转，而在家里或个人关系中由于与那些环境相关的创伤触发因素而出现退行。或者来访者可能报告交替出现的被遗弃恐惧，随后是推开试图亲近的人，或最初将他人理想化，随后当他们以某种方式让来访者失望时出现幻灭和愤怒的倾向。被遗弃的恐惧代表了与他人亲近加剧其分离焦虑时，依恋或求救部分的沟通；推开是由脆弱或受伤风险激活的战斗部分的反应。分裂常常以矛盾行为表现出来：对触发因素的恐惧可能很强烈，而来访者对真实威胁缺乏适当的恐惧；来访者计划明年夏天的家庭度假，同时又沉浸在自杀决心的反刍中；或者他将自己描述为"开悟的"、善良的和理性的，而家人和朋友却将来访者描述为通常愤怒、傲慢和苛求的。

Treatment History

治疗史

The client reports a number of previous treatments that have resulted in little progress or clarity, or describes those treatments as rocky and tumultuous or having ended in some unusually dramatic way. The therapist or previous therapists report feeling "in over my head," "inadequate for what he or she needs," "don't have the skills," while the client reports fearing therapist abandonment more than therapeutic inadequacy.

来访者报告了若干没有带来多少进展或清晰认识的既往治疗，或将那些治疗描述为曲折动荡的，或以某种异常戏剧性的方式结束的。治疗师或既往治疗师报告感到"深不可测"、"无法胜任他/她的需求"、"没有相关技能"，而来访者报告对治疗师被遗弃的恐惧多于对治疗本身不足的担忧。

Somatic Symptoms

躯体症状

Unusual pain sensitivity or uncharacteristically high pain tolerance, stress-related headaches, eye blinking or drooping, narcoleptic symptoms, even physical symptoms with no diagnosable medical cause can be trauma-related or a symptom of dissociative activity. One of the most common indicators of structural dissociation is atypical or non-responsiveness to psychopharmacological medications (Anderson, 2014). In these instances, parts are communicating somatically: blinking or drooping of the eyelids often signals dissociative switching; a collapsed left shoulder and a tense, raised right shoulder might be evidence of a submissive part on the non-dominant side of the body and a part ready to fight connected to the physically stronger dominant side of the body.

不寻常的痛觉敏感或非典型性的高痛耐受性、与压力相关的头痛、眼睑眨动或下垂、发作性睡病症状，甚至没有可诊断医学原因的躯体症状，都可能与创伤相关或是解离活动的症状。结构性解离最常见的指标之一是对精神药物的非典型或无反应性（Anderson，2014年）。在这些情况下，各部分正在以躯体方式进行沟通：眼睑眨动或下垂通常预示着解离性转换；左肩塌陷而右肩紧绷抬高，可能是身体非主导侧顺从部分的证据，以及与身体更强壮的主导侧相连的准备战斗部分的证据。

"Regressive" Behavior or Thinking

“退行性”行为或思维

Sometimes, the client's body language seems more typical of a young child than an adult of his or her chronological age: he or she might appear shy, collapsed, fearful, unable to tolerate being seen, or unable to make eye contact. The message might be: "I'm scared—don't hurt me" or "Please notice me—please like me" or "Please don't leave me." Verbal and cognitive style can also reveal the presence of younger parts of the self: concrete or black/white thinking, words or style of expression more typical of a child than an adult. Children use shorter sentences, express themes related to separation, caring, and fairness, and are more likely to feel empathically failed when not well understood.

有时，来访者的肢体语言似乎更像是一个年幼孩子而非其实际年龄的成人：他/她可能看起来羞怯、蜷缩、充满恐惧、无法忍受被看见，或无法进行目光接触。信息可能是："我害怕——不要伤害我"或"请注意我——请喜欢我"或"请不要离开我"。语言和认知风格也可以揭示自我较年幼部分的存在：具体或非黑即白的思维，更像孩子而非成人的词语或表达方式。儿童使用较短的句子，表达与分离、关怀和公平相关的主题，并且在没有被充分理解时更容易感到共情的失败。

Patterns of Indecision or Self-Sabotage

优柔寡断或自我破坏的模式

Often misinterpreted as "ambivalence," a client's inability to make small everyday decisions or problems with carrying out his or her expressed intentions can reflect conflicts between parts with opposite aims. Often this phenomenon manifests in frequent changes of job, career, or relationship, or a history of success in life alternating with self-sabotage or inexplicable failure, high functioning alternating with decompensation, hard work being suddenly undone by self-destructive actions. Very crucial life decisions can be involved, but often this pattern appears in difficulties in daily living such as an inability to choose what to wear in the morning, what to eat for breakfast, or whether to keep a date with a friend for lunch.

通常被误解为"矛盾心理"，来访者无法做出日常小决策或难以执行其明确表达的意图，可能反映了目标相反的各部分之间的冲突。这一现象通常表现为频繁更换工作、职业或关系，或生活中成功史与自我破坏或莫名其妙的失败交替出现，高效运转与失代偿交替，辛苦工作突然被自我毁灭性行动所撤销。可能涉及非常重要的人生决策，但这种模式往往出现在日常生活困难中，例如早上无法选择穿什么、吃什么早餐，或是否去赴与朋友的午餐约会。

Memory Symptoms

记忆症状

While memory gaps and "time loss" are cardinal symptoms of dissociative disorders, more subtle memory problems can be indicative of structural dissociation. For example, all of the following memory issues are common manifestations of parts activity: difficulty remembering how time was spent in a day, difficulty remembering conversations or the focus of therapy sessions, "black outs," getting lost while driving somewhere familiar (such as going home from work), forgetting well-learned skills (such as how to drive), or engaging in behavior one does not recall.

虽然记忆空白和"时间失去"是解离性障碍的主要症状，但更细微的记忆问题可能表明结构性解离的存在。例如，以下所有记忆问题都是各部分活动的常见表现：难以记住一天中时间是如何度过的，难以记住对话或治疗会谈的重点，"黑暗时刻"，在驾车前往熟悉地点时迷路（例如从工作返回家中），忘记已熟练掌握的技能（例如如何驾驶），或做出自己不记得的行为。

Patterns of Self-Destructive and Addictive Behavior

自我毁灭与成瘾行为的模式

Many studies have demonstrated correlations between suicidality and self-harm and addictive behavior with a history of trauma, so it should not be surprising that therapists encounter traumatized clients who struggle against their own self-destructive behavior. My assumption is that unsafe behavior consistently reflects the activation of fight and flight-driven parts by trauma-related triggers. While the going on with normal life self of the client seeks therapy because he or she is committed to life, to wanting "all the things everyone wants," fight parts engage in high-risk behavior or attempt to harm the body or end life in the effort to get relief from the implicit memories at any cost. Parts driven by the flight response tend to engage in eating disorders that result in numbing or addictive behavior that alters consciousness, allowing distance from unbearable feelings and flashbacks. Fight-related parts are prone to more violent actions, whether aggression toward others or self-harm and suicidal behavior. In a pilot study using the structural dissociation model with a group of severely symptomatic inpatients hospitalized for 2–10 years to prevent intentional or unintentional suicide, six of the eight subjects demonstrated marked improvement after a year of psychoeducationally based treatment focused on identifying the parts connected to the unsafe behavior and strengthening the ability of the going on with normal life self to identify and separate from the impulses of self-destructive parts (Fisher, in press).

许多研究已经证明了自杀倾向和自伤及成瘾行为与创伤史之间的相关性，因此治疗师遇到与自身自我毁灭行为抗争的创伤来访者应该不足为奇。我的假设是，不安全行为始终反映了创伤相关触发因素对战斗和逃跑驱动部分的激活。当来访者的正常生活持续自我因其致力于生命、渴望"每个人都想要的一切"而寻求治疗时，战斗部分从事高风险行为或试图伤害身体或结束生命，不惜一切代价从内隐记忆中获得解脱。受逃跑反应驱动的部分倾向于从事导致麻木的饮食障碍或改变意识的成瘾行为，以便远离难以承受的感受和闪回。战斗相关部分倾向于更多的暴力行动，无论是对他人的攻击还是自伤和自杀行为。在一项使用结构性解离模型对一组因防止有意或无意自杀而住院2-10年的严重症状性住院患者进行的试验研究中，八名受试者中有六名在以识别与不安全行为相关的部分为重点并加强正常生活持续自我识别和分离自我毁灭部分冲动能力的心理教育为基础的治疗一年后显示出显著改善（Fisher，待出版）。

By the time the trauma survivor appears at our doorstep, the neurobiological and psychological effects of a dysregulated autonomic nervous system, disorganized attachment patterns, and structurally dissociated parts will have become a set of well-entrenched, familiar, habitual responses. He or she will be unconsciously driven by post-traumatic implicit procedural learning activated by trauma-related triggers. The symptoms and triggered reactions now will be so familiar and automatic that, subjectively, they feel like "just who I am." Although apparently unrelated to the past, these "just who I am" responses are the conveyors of a narrative that cannot be fully remembered or put into words, a history held by different parts of the personality with different perspectives, triggers, and survival responses.

当创伤幸存者出现在我们门口时，失调的自主神经系统、紊乱的依恋模式和结构性解离部分的神经生物学和心理影响，已经成为一套根深蒂固的、熟悉的、习惯性的反应。他/她将被创伤相关触发因素激活的创伤后内隐程序性学习无意识地驱动。当前的症状和被触发的反应现在是如此熟悉和自动，以至于从主观上感觉像是"就是我这个人"。尽管表面上与过去无关，这些"就是我这个人"的反应是一个无法被充分记忆或付诸语言的叙事的传递者，是由人格的不同部分以不同视角、触发因素和生存反应所承载的历史。

For Gillian, the anger, shame, and hopelessness she felt at age 26 had that quality: she didn't have to account for why she felt those feelings because they were so familiar, so much a part of her. She didn't think to be curious about how fearless she could become when angry or how quickly shame and hopelessness could take over her body and stop her words in mid-sentence. Nor was she curious about how confident she was in her ability as an artist, unafraid to market her work yet so afraid of displeasing people in personal relationships. Gillian didn't even feel alarmed by what her therapist called the "suicidal part"; she simply accepted as normal her strong wish not to live. Normal life was a distant memory—something that had once been important to her—but now long gone. She didn't see the subtle but meaningful signs that her going on with normal life part was still alive and well and had always been, even during the worst of times. She was still a wise, mature mother figure for her little sister and the emotional support of her mother—a role that gave her a sense of control over the chaos in the family. Secondly, she had used her aesthetic and creative abilities as a safe haven as a child—that was the side of her with which she identified as "the good side," leading her to become a professional ceramicist in her early twenties. She minimized it: "Hey, if I didn't have an alcoholic mother, I'd probably go in for drugs, so I'm addicted to making things—so what?"

对于Gillian来说，她26岁时感受到的愤怒、羞耻和无望正具有这种特质：她不必解释为什么会有这些感受，因为它们是如此熟悉，如此成为她的一部分。她没有想到要对愤怒时自己会变得多么无所畏惧感到好奇，也没有想到羞耻和无望会多迅速地接管她的身体并让话语在句中停止。她也没有对自己作为艺术家的能力有多自信感到好奇——她不惧推销自己的作品，却在个人关系中如此害怕令人不悦。Gillian甚至没有对治疗师所称的"自杀部分"感到警觉；她只是将自己强烈的不想活着的愿望当作正常接受。正常生活是遥远的记忆——曾经对她很重要的东西——但现在早已消逝。她没有看到她的正常生活持续部分仍然活着且运转良好，并且即使在最糟糕的时候也一直如此的那些微妙但有意义的迹象。她仍然是妹妹的智慧成熟的母亲式人物和她母亲的情感支撑——这个角色给了她对家庭混乱的控制感。其次，她从小就将她的审美和创造能力用作避风港——那是她认同为"好的一面"的部分，使她在二十多岁时成为了一名职业陶艺师。她将其最小化："嘿，如果我没有一个酗酒的母亲，我可能会染上毒品，所以我沉迷于制作东西——那又怎样？"

As Gillian attests, trauma survivors all too often develop other symptoms that represent neurobiologically regulating attempts to cope with the trauma: self-injury and suicidality, risk-taking, re-enactment behavior, caretaking and self-sacrifice, revictimization, and addictive behavior. All of these behaviors represent different ways of modulating a dysregulated nervous system and preparing for the next threat: self-injury and planning suicide induce adrenaline-related responses of power, icy calm, control, and physical strength but also a relaxation effect from increased production of endorphins; restricting, binging and purging, and overeating all induce emotional and bodily numbing; and addictive behaviors can be tailored to evoke either numbing or increased arousal or a combination of both. Historically, in the mental health field, we have addressed these issues first by stabilizing unsafe behavior and then treating the traumatic events. But narrative memories are connected to intense states of autonomic arousal. Because the activation "readies" us for danger, remembering is likely to reactivate self-destructive impulses. Even "thinking about thinking about" the memories (Ogden et al., 2016) is often enough to cause a reactivation of the nervous system as if the events were recurring in the here-and-now. The neurobiological research and a better understanding of the somatic legacy of trauma advise us to take a new and different course in treatment (Van der Kolk, 2014; Ogden et al., 2006). (See Chapter 2 on Understanding Parts, Understanding Traumatic Responses and Chapter 7 on Working with Suicidal, Self-Destructive, Eating Disordered, and Addicted Parts.)

正如Gillian所证实的，创伤幸存者常常发展出其他代表着神经生物学上调节性的应对创伤尝试的症状：自伤和自杀倾向、冒险、重演行为、照顾他人和自我牺牲、再次受害以及成瘾行为。所有这些行为代表了调节失调的神经系统并为下一个威胁做好准备的不同方式：自伤和计划自杀诱发与肾上腺素相关的力量感、冷静、控制和身体力量反应，但也有内啡肽分泌增加的放松效果；限制饮食、暴饮暴食和清除以及过度饮食都诱发情绪和身体麻木；成瘾行为可以被定制为引发麻木或增加唤醒或两者的组合。历史上，在精神健康领域，我们首先通过稳定不安全行为然后处理创伤事件来解决这些问题。但叙事记忆与自主神经唤醒的强烈状态相连。因为激活使我们为危险做好"准备"，记忆可能会重新激活自我毁灭的冲动。甚至"思考关于思考"记忆（Ogden等，2016年）往往足以导致神经系统的重新激活，仿佛事件在此时此地再次发生。神经生物学研究和对创伤躯体遗产的更好理解建议我们在治疗中采取新的不同路线（Van der Kolk，2014；Ogden等，2006年）。（参见第2章理解各部分、理解创伤性反应，以及第7章与自杀、自我毁灭、饮食障碍及成瘾部分工作。）

Helping Clients and Their Parts "Be Here" Now

帮助来访者及其各部分"活在当下"

When their symptoms represent implicit memories held by trauma-related parts expressing emergency and survival responses, individuals continue to feel unsafe, and their parts continue to defend, as if threatened now. When trauma responses are misinterpreted in these ways, it threatens the parts: it feels like proof that they are endangered, defective, or trapped in a hopeless situation. Once again, the felt sense is that they are in danger, alone, without protection. Our first priority in treatment must be to challenge this subjective perception that their symptoms are indicative of current danger or proof of their defectiveness or "just who they are." Therapists need to counteract the habitually triggered danger signals and trauma responses by calling attention to these reactions as communications from parts. When clients are provided with psychoeducation about structural dissociation, encouraged to become mindful and curious instead of reactive, helped to develop new responses to triggers, they begin to build the capacity to self-regulate and to "be here now." Then, by pacing the exploration of the past in such a way that the autonomic nervous system gets a chance to experience regulation instead of dysregulation, clients can experience moments of what I call "being present in the present," moments of feeling calm in the body, being able to think clearly, knowing they are safe.

当他们的症状代表着由表达紧急和生存反应的创伤相关部分所持有的内隐记忆时，个体继续感到不安全，他们的各部分继续防御，仿佛当前正受到威胁。当创伤反应被这样误解时，它威胁到各部分：感觉就像是他们处于危险中、有缺陷或被困在无望处境中的证明。再一次，体感是他们处于危险中，孤独，没有保护。我们在治疗中的首要任务必须是挑战他们的症状表明当前危险或其缺陷性或"就是他们这个人"的主观感知。治疗师需要通过将这些反应作为来自各部分的沟通加以关注，来抵消习惯性触发的危险信号和创伤反应。当来访者获得关于结构性解离的心理教育，被鼓励变得正念和好奇而非反应性，被帮助对触发因素发展出新的反应时，他们开始建立自我调节和"活在当下"的能力。然后，通过以自主神经系统有机会体验调节而非失调的方式引导对过去的探索，来访者可以体验我所称的"当下在场"的时刻，感到身体平静、能够清晰思考、知道自己是安全的片刻。

In the next chapter, we will explore how to understand the issues and symptoms described by our clients as manifestations of the "living legacy" of trauma. Without an understanding of post-traumatic implicit memory or structural dissociation, not knowing they have been triggered by some cue reminiscent of the past, they interpret fear, shame, and anger as signs of imminent danger or deep-seated inadequacy. It can be a relief to discover that their stuckness, resistance, chronic depression, fear of change, entrenched fear and self-hatred, crisis and conflict, even suicidality all can be communications from parts who fear for their lives, unaware that the dangers they are bracing against are now in the past. Disappointment, criticism, closeness, or distance, even authority figures, may no longer be life-threatening, but each nonetheless evokes trauma-related implicit memories and the parts that hold them. Helping clients learn to become curious and interested in their symptoms and able to identify the voices that speak through their reactions can change their relationship to themselves and to the past from one of shame and dread to one of compassion. Knowing that each part is charged with the mission to survive, each in its own way, helps clients to see that how they survived was more crucial than how they were victimized. Understanding how each part participated in survival increases the sense of "we, together" and challenges the sense of being abandoned and alone. Feeling warmth and empathy for young wounded selves feels healing and comforting.

在下一章中，我们将探讨如何将来访者所描述的问题和症状理解为创伤"活的遗产"的表现。在没有对创伤后内隐记忆或结构性解离的理解的情况下，不知道自己已被过去的某些线索触发，他们将恐惧、羞耻和愤怒解释为迫在眉睫的危险或根深蒂固的不足的迹象。发现他们的停滞、抵抗、慢性抑郁、对变化的恐惧、根深蒂固的恐惧和自我憎恨、危机和冲突，甚至自杀倾向，都可能是那些为生命担忧、不知道他们戒备的危险现在已成为过去的各部分发出的沟通，这可能会带来宽慰。失望、批评、亲近或距离，甚至权威人物，可能不再是危及生命的，但每一个仍然唤起创伤相关的内隐记忆和承载它们的各部分。帮助来访者学会对自己的症状感到好奇和感兴趣，并能够识别通过其反应说话的声音，可以将他们与自己和过去的关系从羞耻和恐惧转变为慈悲的关系。知道每个部分都被赋予了以自己的方式存活的使命，帮助来访者看到他们如何存活比他们如何被害更为重要。理解每个部分如何参与了存活，增强了"我们，在一起"的感觉，挑战了被遗弃和孤独的感觉。对年轻受伤的自我感到温暖和共情，感觉是愈合的和令人安慰的。
